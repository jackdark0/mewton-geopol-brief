## SHOW FODDER

Choose the three strongest discussion topics.

### Topic

**Central question:** The strongest substantive question for discussion.  
**Competing interpretations:** The major defensible readings of the evidence.  
**Key fact:** The fact or piece of evidence most useful for adjudicating the disagreement.  
**Bad argument to avoid:** A misleading, unsupported, or oversimplified framing.
