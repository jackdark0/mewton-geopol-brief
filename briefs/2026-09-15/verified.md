# Source Audit — 2026-09-15

Research cutoff: **2026-09-15 02:00 America/New_York (06:00 UTC)**.

## Audit standard

Direct pages were inspected during this run. Search snippets and aggregator summaries were not treated as evidence. Official statements establish what an institution said or did, not the truth of every underlying allegation. Anonymous-source claims remain labeled. Events first reported after the cutoff are excluded even if they concern the window.

## Verified developments

### A. Saudi East-West pipeline repair horizon — ACCEPT

- **Verified fact:** Saudi Arabia confirmed a September 10 attack, a precautionary shutdown, injuries, and an ongoing assessment. [Saudi Press Agency](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2674017)
- **Corroborated report:** AP reported on September 14, citing two unnamed regional officials, that repairs could take three to five weeks and that the line was mostly offline, with partial flow possible earlier. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/saudi-pipeline-oil-iran-war-efa431e2fa771e34880c8453c62ffb92)
- **Quantification:** AP attributed a 2.6–4 million barrel-a-day recent flow estimate to Rystad and roughly 4 million barrels a day of bypass capacity to the IEA. The [IEA's September report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-september-2026) supplies broader production and inventory context.
- **Constraint:** Do not state that 4 million barrels a day have disappeared from global supply. The repair timing is anonymous-source reporting, not an official Saudi timetable.

### B. Houthi island capture and Saudi attack wave — ACCEPT WITH ATTRIBUTION

- **Verified report:** Houthi officials said the movement seized Greater and Lesser Hanish. AP reported the claim and its location near Bab el-Mandeb. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-yemen-houthis-israel-saudi-oil-b95a5a6c73a26bbbb1caddcbf365ca62)
- **Independent reporting:** Reuters cited Yemeni government officials saying the Houthis hold almost all of the western coast and reported a new missile-and-drone wave, Saudi alerts in four cities, and a Saudi-led coalition statement that 13 civilians were wounded. [Reuters](https://ca.investing.com/news/world-news/houthis-strike-saudi-targets-anew-as-talks-over-strait-of-hormuz-stall-4839039)
- **Human effect:** AP cited IOM saying nearly 94,000 people had been displaced since the escalation.
- **Constraint:** Treat island control as participant-sourced pending stronger visual or neutral confirmation. Do not say the Houthis control or have closed Bab el-Mandeb. Do not repeat claimed military damage without evidence.

### C. Oman postponed Gulf-Iran talks — ACCEPT AS A DIPLOMATIC SETBACK

- **Verified fact:** Oman announced that the planned meeting was postponed. Reuters and AP both reported the postponement. [Reuters](https://ca.investing.com/news/world-news/houthis-strike-saudi-targets-anew-as-talks-over-strait-of-hormuz-stall-4839039), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-yemen-houthis-israel-saudi-oil-b95a5a6c73a26bbbb1caddcbf365ca62)

- **Constraint:** Describe the channel as stalled or inconclusive, not dead. Do not present an Iran-Oman agreement as established.
