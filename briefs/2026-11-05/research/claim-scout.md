## Claim C-01: Four oil tankers were destroyed by mines south of Hormuz on November 4

- **Origin / principal promoters:** IRGC Public Relations Office, reporting operations by its Navy and Aerospace Force. The directly inspected [Tasnim statement relay](https://www.tasnimnews.ir/en/news/2026/11/04/3739846/4-oil-tankers-destroyed-mq-9-drone-downed-irgc/amp), lines 17–23, attributes the claim to a Wednesday-morning statement. Tasnim is an interested, IRGC-affiliated outlet conveying the claimant's statement, not independent confirmation. Other discovered reports trace to that same IRGC announcement, sometimes through IRNA.
- **Atomic proposition / scope:** Four distinct oil tankers suffered mine strikes and destruction during the November 4 episode. This does not adjudicate the separate two-vessel interception or the separate MQ-9 claim in the same announcement. It also does not merge November 3's three alleged tanker fires, earlier October announcements, or the independently reported projectile incident into a cumulative total.
- **Why it matters:** Four destroyed tankers would materially change the assessment of maritime attrition, mine clearance, and the commercial risk of attempting the southern route. Mistaking repetition for confirmation could amplify coercive claims about Iran's effective control of transit.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Tasnim reports that four tankers attempting a route Iran prohibited hit mines and remained on fire. It supplies a date-specific institutional assertion. This establishes the announcement, not the underlying damage or mine attribution.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No hull names, IMO numbers, flag states, casualty accounting, exact coordinates, operator notices, or verifiable imagery accompany the inspected announcement. [The Maritime Executive](https://maritime-executive.com/index.php/article/iran-claims-strikes-on-multiple-tankers-emboldened-after-us-elections), lines 45–50, says most claims lack independent verification. It reports one November 3 tanker incident involving two unknown projectiles into an engine room near Limah, Oman, and a separate November 2 bulker incident. Neither is confirmation of four mine-struck tankers on November 4. Its report also notes delayed/non-reporting by tankers, which limits arguments from silence.
- **Independent corroboration:** **No** for the four-vessel count, mine mechanism, or destruction. **Partial** only for the broader existence of other shipping attacks, which is a different proposition.
- **Current assessment:** **Unclear — unverified underlying event.** Supported that the IRGC made the claim. The inspected evidence neither establishes four losses nor disproves them. Publication-safe wording: Iran claimed four tankers were destroyed by mines; independent confirmation of those four cases was not found.
- **Confidence:** **Medium** in the assessment that the public packet is insufficient; **low** in the claimed count/mechanism. Reasons: one interested origin, missing vessel identifiers, and incomplete incident-reporting coverage.
- **Timing:** Tasnim displays **November 4, 2026, 08:13**, without an explicit timezone in the inspected page; do not silently convert it. It describes a statement that morning. Maritime Executive displays **2026-11-04 14:45:03**, timezone unstated. Both dated bodies precede the cutoff regardless of plausible local timezone. Exact event times for the four alleged mine strikes remain unspecified. A Reuters syndication discovery result dated 03:50 UTC on November 4 could not be opened (429); its snippet is not evidence here.
- **Sources:**
  - [Tasnim, November 4 statement relay](https://www.tasnimnews.ir/en/news/2026/11/04/3739846/4-oil-tankers-destroyed-mq-9-drone-downed-irgc/amp) — **claimant/near-primary statement relay**; supports exact origin, asserted four-vessel count, mine mechanism, and alleged continuing fires. English translation; Persian original not inspected. Interested source; no independent incident evidence attached.
  - [Maritime Executive, November 4](https://maritime-executive.com/index.php/article/iran-claims-strikes-on-multiple-tankers-emboldened-after-us-elections) — **specialist secondary reporting**, with distinct UKMTO/Vanguard/Lloyd's List lineages for separate incidents; supports the corroboration limitation and the need to keep events distinct. Its two-vessel and fire wording differs from Tasnim, so do not use it to silently rewrite the original statement.
  - [UKMTO recent-incidents page](https://www.ukmto.org/recent-incidents) — **primary interface attempted; unusable for historical corroboration**. Returned zero reports in the text extraction. That may reflect rendering or interface limits and is not evidence that no incident occurred. The original November 3 notice was not recovered directly.
- **Questions for auditor:** Can any of the four hulls be identified through flag states, operators, IMO, UKMTO notices, or dated satellite imagery? Are the four alleged November 4 cases demonstrably distinct from earlier announcements? Does the Persian statement distinguish sunk/destroyed from burning/damaged? ## Claim C-02: The Ra'am vote-buying case establishes that Israel's election was stolen

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Benjamin Netanyahu, in an election-day video statement, according to [Times of Israel's November 4 court report](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/rejecting-police-appeal-court-orders-unconditional-release-of-last-detainee-in-alleged-raam-vote-buying-case/), lines 123–124. The report quotes his characterization as an attempt **“to steal the election from us.”** The original video was not inspected. Later Likud rhetoric and the distinct foreign-financing investigation must not be treated as the same factual file.
- **Atomic proposition / scope:** Evidence from this specific alleged Ra'am vote-buying case establishes election theft at a scale capable of invalidating the overall result. This is a test of the stronger election-integrity narrative, not a claim that every allegation of local wrongdoing has been disproven. The original attributed wording alleges an attempt; a completed, outcome-changing theft would be a stronger and unsupported extension.
- **Why it matters:** The narrative could affect recognition of the result and bargaining over a replacement government. The November 4 custody ruling is new evidence about the strength of this particular investigative file, while delivery of official results changes the procedural context.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The inspected court report says police originally alleged cash envelopes and planned vote-buying. It also reports a defense dispute about both the amount and intended use. An arrest or remand proceeding is evidence that an investigation exists, not an adjudication of guilt or a measured effect on the election.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Times of Israel reports Judge Eran Tzabari ordered Ibrahim al-Garibi's unconditional release and rejected police requests for house arrest/internet restrictions, criticizing lack of investigative progress. This narrows confidence in the specific case; it is not an acquittal or proof no attempt occurred. Separately, the [President's Office primary record](https://www.president.gov.il/en/president-herzog-receives-the-official-results-of-the-elections-to-the-26th-knesset/) records receipt of official results on November 4. The bare presidential record establishes that procedural act, not the absence of all misconduct. No inspected tally of affected ballots, constituency-level effect, judicial invalidation, or causal change in seat allocation supports the sweeping claim.
- **Independent corroboration:** **No** for outcome-changing theft. **Partial** for the context: the presidential record independently establishes results delivery, while the custody decision is presently available through named court reporting. The court judgment itself was not recovered.
- **Current assessment:** **Weak at the election-wide scope; underlying alleged attempt remains unresolved.** Preserve both distinctions: investigation versus proof, and release versus exoneration. Do not infer knowing deception or motive from the evidence gap.
- **Confidence:** **Medium** in the narrowing assessment. The missing underlying video, court order, and nonpublic investigative record prevent a stronger comprehensive verdict. High confidence only that the inspected President's Office record reports receipt of official results.
- **Timing:** Times of Israel's fixed liveblog entry displays **November 4, 2026, 14:20** (site local time, Israel; UTC+02:00 at that date). The President's Office record is dated **04/11/2026**, with no clock time. The accusation itself predates the window; the new in-window evidence is November 4's release and results delivery. Current site sidebars are excluded.
- **Sources:**
  - [Times of Israel, Noam Lehmann, November 4, 14:20](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/rejecting-police-appeal-court-orders-unconditional-release-of-last-detainee-in-alleged-raam-vote-buying-case/) — **named secondary court reporting**; supports release, the judge's reported criticism, competing police/defense accounts, and attribution of Netanyahu's earlier video. Does not replace the full court order or original video.
  - [President of Israel, official results receipt, November 4](https://www.president.gov.il/en/president-herzog-receives-the-official-results-of-the-elections-to-the-26th-knesset/) — **direct primary institutional record**; supports results delivery by CEC chair Justice Noam Sohlberg. No full speech, investigative findings, or seat table in the extracted body.
- **Questions for auditor:** Recover the court order/case number and Netanyahu's complete video before quoting or rating his original attempted-theft allegation more broadly. What specific evidence is alleged to affect how many votes? Keep the police vote-buying case separate from Shin Bet's foreign-financing inquiry. ## Claim C-03: Vance's claim that the Iran war was the decisive reason Republicans lost the midterms

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Vice President JD Vance's November 4 X post, linked by [ABC's November 4 report](https://abcnews.com/amp/Politics/vance-blames-iran-war-republicans-midterms-defeat-trump/story?id=137849295) to [the original post](https://x.com/jdvance/status/2118004988433641835?s=46). X returned 403. ABC and [Reuters' direct reporting via StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Vance%2Brepudiates%2BIran%2Bwar%2Bafter%2BUS%2Belectoral%2Bdefeat/27288446.html) reproduce **“The reason we lost is the war.”** Their agreement establishes the reported statement, not the causal claim.
- **Atomic proposition / scope:** The Iran war was decisive in Republican congressional losses. Do not mutate Vance's shorthand into a claim that literally no other factor mattered: his reported discussion also lists inflation, domestic priorities, and the Israel relationship. Nor does an antiwar election interpretation establish what policy an incoming Congress will enact.
- **Why it matters:** The administration's own vice president assigning defeat to the war can change the political constraints on renewed combat and alliance policy. The causal assessment matters separately from the observable political break.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The public statement and reported account of a pollster warning Vance about angry 2024 Trump voters support a substantive electoral-cost hypothesis. The actual pollster analysis is not public in the inspected material. ABC's first-party account of its voter poll reports **34% of House voters** rated the Iran war crucial to their vote. That supports salience for a sizable group, not the number of marginal seats caused by the war.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The same ABC report gives **49%** for cost of living and **55%** for government corruption on the crucial-to-vote measure. These are overlapping issue responses, not mutually exclusive vote causes. War-related costs can operate through economic concerns, so the ranking does not disprove a decisive indirect effect. Conversely, no inspected counterfactual, district-level causal estimate, item-level respondent counts, full questionnaire, or turnout-versus-switching decomposition demonstrates that the war decided the overall result.
- **Independent corroboration:** **Partial** for the political statement and the war's salience; **no** for a decisive causal effect. AP and ABC voter-poll coverage share the **SSRS Voter Poll** and are not independent polling replications. Vance's unidentified pollster is a separate alleged lineage whose methods/data were not inspected.
- **Current assessment:** **Plausible as an important contributor; unclear as a decisive causal explanation.** Report the vice president's assessment as his assessment. Do not present the election as a single-question referendum or interpret issue salience as causal attribution.
- **Confidence:** **Medium** in this qualification. Polling provides contemporaneous evidence about reported priorities, but decisive causation and the exact mechanism remain unmeasured here.
- **Timing:** ABC article displays **November 4, 2026, 16:36 ET**. StreetInsider displays **November 4, 12:25 EST**, but its current Reuters body incorporates later responses without a visible revision clock; use ABC for the specific response chronology. The inspected SSRS PDF is stamped **Last Updated 11/4/2026 5:23 PM** (timezone unstated). All are before the frozen cutoff. Exact X publication time was not recovered.
- **Sources:**
  - [ABC, Fritz Farrow, Emily Guskin and Ivan Pereira, November 4](https://abcnews.com/amp/Politics/vance-blames-iran-war-republicans-midterms-defeat-trump/story?id=137849295) — **independent political reporting plus first-party poll-partner findings**, lines 58–99; supports statement, pollster attribution, and the three issue percentages. Sample for those percentages: US House voters nationally, not all adults, all citizens, or Republicans alone. Exact item n and full response scale remain missing.
  - [Reuters via StreetInsider, November 4](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Vance%2Brepudiates%2BIran%2Bwar%2Bafter%2BUS%2Belectoral%2Bdefeat/27288446.html) — **wire reporting, syndicated carrier**; separate newsroom confirmation of the public statement. All copies of this Reuters story constitute one lineage. Mutable body/revision-time limitation noted above.
  - [SSRS primary methodology PDF](https://ssrs.com/wp-content/uploads/The-Voter-Poll-by-SSRS-Methodology-Statement_Final.pdf) — **direct primary methods record**, pages 1–2 and 6–7; distinguishes over 100,000 interviews overall from a national sample over 80,000, describes online/phone/in-person collection and weighting, and reports October 19–November 3 fieldwork overall. Washington, DC was not sampled. It contains no Iran item questionnaire. Do not transfer the overall sample size or margin of error to an unknown subgroup/item denominator.
  - [Original Vance X post](https://x.com/jdvance/status/2118004988433641835?s=46) — **original attempted, inaccessible (403)**; not independently inspected. Quotations above rest on directly inspected ABC/Reuters text.
- **Questions for auditor:** Recover the complete X post and timestamps to preserve Vance's four-factor context. Locate the final questionnaire, item n, response options, and pre-cutoff national table. Can any public evidence distinguish turnout losses from vote switching? Avoid treating AP and ABC as two polls. Seek district-level/counterfactual evidence before upgrading decisive causation. Keep an economic transmission mechanism possible without assuming all cost-of-living concern arose from the war.
