## Finding: Do not turn projections into a certified Congress

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** “Democrats win both chambers” and all references to an at-least-54 Senate majority.
- **Problem:** The evidence supports independent newsroom projections and a cutoff-safe Reuters floor, not final certification or a complete 100-seat result. “Win” can read as final if the body does not preserve that distinction.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** CBS explicitly says “projected”; Reuters says “at least 54” and identifies remaining transition work. The prior brief already used projection language for the House.
- **Best competing interpretation:** The political expectation is effectively settled enough for transition planning, but legal certification and final committee arithmetic remain open.
- **Required editorial action:** Use “projected to control” or “set to control” in the headline and first sentence. State that the incoming Congress begins January 3 and the final margins remain pending.
