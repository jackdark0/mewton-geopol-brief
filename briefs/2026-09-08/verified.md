- [CLAIMED] [CLAIM; confidence: moderate] Iranian reporting attributed improved range, accuracy, and payload to a missile identified with the Qassem Basir program. Those performance specifications were not independently tested in the inspected evidence.

- A causal link between the statement and a specific later strike.

### Source provenance

It explicitly says the tested weapon's identity was unclear. The Iranian technical claims remain one actor lineage.

### Open questions

### Recommendation
