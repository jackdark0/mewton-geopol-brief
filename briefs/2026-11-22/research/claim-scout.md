# Claim Scout — 2026-11-22

**Research cutoff:** 2026-11-22 02:00:00 America/New_York (07:00 UTC)

**Working window:** approximately 2026-11-20 20:00 America/New_York through the cutoff. Later developments are excluded.

## Consequential contested claims

### Claim 1: Iran attacked three tankers near Hormuz

- **Claimant / venue:** Iranian and regional reporting described a mine incident; some coverage grouped it with UKMTO reports.
- **Verified substrate:** UKMTO logged two outbound tankers hit by unknown projectiles at 05:00 and 05:50 UTC November 21. A third UKMTO incident occurred November 20 outside the nominal window.
- **Counterevidence / gap:** UKMTO did not attribute the projectiles. Reuters could not verify Iran's mine account or link it to the UKMTO incidents.
- **Verdict:** **Unsupported as stated.** Multiple incidents are real; common Iranian responsibility and a three-tanker chain are not established.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Sources:** [UKMTO](https://www.ukmto.org/recent-incidents); [Reuters](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/tanker-hit-unknown-projectile-near-075656148.html)

### Claim 2: Iran struck U.S. positions in Bahrain

- **Claimant / venue:** The IRGC said its operation hit U.S. positions at al-Riffa and Awali.
- **Verified substrate:** Bahrain reported an Iranian attack on an unnamed civilian industrial facility, a fire, material damage and additional interceptions.
- **Counterevidence / gap:** Inspected public evidence did not identify a U.S. facility hit or establish damage to U.S. forces. Bahrain's preliminary report said no injuries by the cutoff.
- **Verdict:** **Unverified.** The Iranian operation and Bahrain damage are supported; the claimed U.S. target effects are not.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Source:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/bahrain-says-iranian-attack-causes-fire-at-industrial-facility)

### Claim 3: Sparta II was carrying Russian military cargo

- **Confidence:** High.
- **Sources:** [Finnish Border Guard](https://www.epressi.com/tiedotteet/turvallisuus/the-finnish-border-guard-continues-to-lead-the-rescue-operations-in-the-gulf-of-finland..html?c=Uskonto); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/21/russian-cargo-ship-struck-by-drone-in-gulf-of-finland-finnish-authorities-say)
