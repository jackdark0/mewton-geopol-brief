## Audit method

The audit split compound assertions into atomic claims, opened the underlying source rather than relying on search snippets, checked whether each source entailed the proposed wording, and kept verdict separate from confidence. Official statements establish what an institution said or did; they do not independently prove the institution's contested attribution. Multiple outlets repeating one official account count as one evidence line.

## Verified atomic claims

### A. Gulf escalation: Bahrain and Hormuz

1. **FACT — High confidence:** UKMTO logged an outbound tanker struck on the stern by an unknown projectile 11 nautical miles north of Khasab at 05:00 UTC November 21. The crew reported no casualties; environmental impact was unknown. [UKMTO recent incidents](https://www.ukmto.org/recent-incidents)
2. **FACT — High confidence:** UKMTO logged another outbound tanker struck in its engine room by an unknown projectile 17 nautical miles east of Khasab at 05:50 UTC. The crew reported no casualties; environmental impact was unknown. [UKMTO Warning 216-26](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261121-ukmto_warning_216-26.pdf?rev=98c26e7c459246539923ed000abcdf27)
3. **CLAIM — Medium confidence in occurrence, low confidence in linkage:** Iran's navy said a tanker hit a mine east of the strait. Reuters could not verify the account or connect it to either UKMTO projectile incident. [Reuters](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/tanker-hit-unknown-projectile-near-075656148.html)
4. **FACT about an official account — High confidence:** Bahrain's Interior Ministry said an Iranian attack caused a fire and material damage at an unnamed civilian industrial facility November 21; its preliminary cutoff-time report said there were no injuries. Bahrain also said defenses intercepted several projectiles. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/bahrain-says-iranian-attack-causes-fire-at-industrial-facility)
5. **CLAIM — Low-to-medium confidence:** The IRGC said it targeted U.S. positions at al-Riffa and Awali. No inspected evidence established hits on those positions or U.S. casualties by the cutoff. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/bahrain-says-iranian-attack-causes-fire-at-industrial-facility)
6. **AUDIT VERDICT:** The defensible synthesis is that Gulf escalation widened across territorial and commercial-maritime domains. It is not defensible to say Iran attacked three tankers or demonstrably hit U.S. positions.
