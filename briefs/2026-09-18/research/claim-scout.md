## Claim 2: Two February 28 U.S. strikes were indiscriminate war crimes

- **Claimant:** U.N.-commissioned Independent International Fact-Finding Mission on Iran.
- **Support:** The mission reports reasonable grounds based on 73 interviews, satellite imagery, documents, photographs, and video. It says the Minab school was the intended impact point and attributes the Lamerd strike to a U.S. precision-strike missile. Earlier AP reporting cited a U.S. official and a person briefed on a preliminary review saying outdated intelligence likely led to the school strike.
- **Counterevidence / missing:** The finding is nonbinding and not a court judgment. Both the U.S. and Iran failed to answer information requests. The mission lacked unrestricted access amid conflict and an internet shutdown. CENTCOM denies responsibility for Lamerd, and the Pentagon has not published a final Minab investigation. The White House and State Department reject the mission's credibility but did not provide a public evidentiary rebuttal in the inspected material.
- **Verdict:** **Supported as a serious reasonable-grounds finding; not finally adjudicated.** The Minab attribution is better supported publicly than the Lamerd attribution.
- **Confidence in verdict:** Medium-high for Minab; medium for Lamerd.
- **Sources:** [AP on the report](https://apnews.com/article/un-human-rights-us-iran-99e690efd1e818e835d4f5a00e0d261b); [AP Minab investigation](https://apnews.com/article/iran-school-strike-baluch-trump-2a134a5c74d80db763db4c3eb6d0d847); [AP on preliminary review](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-school-hegseth-trump-2ffff06808f7a584b0a03831897ab0b8).
