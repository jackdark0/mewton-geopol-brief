## Audit rules applied

- Material facts require an inspected source, not a search snippet.
- Official statements prove what an institution announced; they do not independently prove disputed conduct.
- A wire report repeating an official claim is not independent confirmation of that claim.
- Post-cutoff reporting is excluded from the edition even if it later clarifies a pre-cutoff event.
- Legal findings retain their stated evidentiary threshold and are not converted into final judgments.
- Proposed sales, review options, and expected meetings are not treated as completed decisions.

## Verified major-development packets

### A. Russia and China veto the Iran Panel of Experts extension

**Verified facts**

- The Security Council vote was 11 in favor, China and Russia against, with Pakistan and Somalia abstaining. The two permanent-member no votes defeated the draft. [Official meeting record](https://transcripts.un.org/en/sc/10192)
- The draft would have extended the Panel of Experts for one year. The panel's function is to assist the 1737 Committee, gather and analyze compliance information, support listings, and report. [U.N. mandate page](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/sanctions/1737/panel-of-experts/work-and-mandate)
- The underlying sanctions were not lifted by this vote. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/united-nations-iran-sanctions-russia-china-c509e344bc45ba87670b9a668afbbb49)
- Reuters reports that panel appointments had already been blocked for about a year because consensus was required. [Reuters](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/09/17/russia-china-end-mandate-for-independent-monitoring-of-un-sanctions-on-iran)

**Disputed / qualified**

- Western states treat the 2025 snapback as valid; Russia, China, and Iran dispute that legal position. The brief must describe the dispute rather than resolve it by assertion.
- “Russia and China ended sanctions” is false. “They blocked an extension of the independent monitor” is supported.

**Editorial verdict:** Include. High confidence.

### D. U.N. fact-finding mission issues reasonable-grounds findings on U.S. and Iranian conduct

**Verified facts**

- The mission reported reasonable grounds to believe two February 28 U.S. strikes—Minab and Lamerd—were indiscriminate attacks amounting to war crimes, with at least 178 civilians killed across the two incidents. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/un-human-rights-us-iran-99e690efd1e818e835d4f5a00e0d261b)
- It relied on 73 interviews plus satellite imagery, photographs, video, documents, and information from organizations. Both the U.S. and Iran failed to respond to information requests; war and an internet shutdown constrained evidence gathering. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/un-human-rights-us-iran-99e690efd1e818e835d4f5a00e0d261b)
- The mission also reported crimes against humanity by Iranian authorities.
- Earlier AP reporting said a preliminary U.S. review pointed to outdated intelligence in the Minab school strike. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-school-hegseth-trump-2ffff06808f7a584b0a03831897ab0b8)
- The U.S. rejected the mission's conclusions; CENTCOM denied responsibility for Lamerd. No final public Pentagon report was identified.

**Disputed / qualified**

- This is a nonbinding investigative finding, not a court judgment.
- Minab has more public corroboration than Lamerd.
- The finding does not establish an intent to kill civilians.

**Editorial verdict:** Include with the legal threshold and counterevidence explicit. Medium-high confidence on the mission's finding; medium on the underlying Lamerd attribution.

### F. China's Djibouti base shows a larger communications and intelligence architecture

**Verified facts**

- Dated commercial imagery shows new HF and V/UHF antennas, SATCOM dishes, probable 5G towers, radomes, control buildings, and cable trenches added since mid-2024. [CSIS](https://features.csis.org/hiddenreach/china-djibouti-base-upgrade/)
- CSIS assesses the installations as complementary parts of a base-wide C4ISR architecture capable of improving redundant over-the-horizon communications and likely signals collection. Reuters independently reported the study, not the underlying satellite analysis. [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/china-s-djibouti-base-upgrades-should-boost-its-monitoring-reach-report-says-ce785bd3d18ff326)
- The base is China's only formally acknowledged overseas military base and sits near the Bab al-Mandeb.

**Disputed / qualified**

- The equipment is observable. Exact missions, staffing, collection targets, and performance are analytic inferences.
- This is a newly published capability assessment of gradual construction, not a deployment completed in the last 30 hours.

**Editorial verdict:** Include because the capability change is strategic and undercovered. High confidence on physical upgrades; medium on operational effect.
