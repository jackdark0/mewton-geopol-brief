# Red-team review - 2026-09-30

## Finding: Estonia's suspects are not proved to remain in custody

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** Estonia analysis, “Three suspects are in Estonian custody after transfer from Latvia.”
- **Problem:** The verified record establishes detention in Latvia and transfer to Estonia on September 17-18. It does not establish each suspect's custody status at the September 30 cutoff.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** Transfer is not the same proposition as continuing pretrial detention. The government page says the criminal investigation continues but does not supply a current custody disposition.
- **Best competing interpretation:** The suspects may remain detained, may be subject to another restraint, or may have been released under conditions; the inspected record does not decide among them.
- **Required editorial action:** **correct** to “three suspects were transferred from Latvia to Estonia” and omit current custody status.

## Finding: Fairford's negative vehicle search does not settle the wider offense theory

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** Fairford headline and the statement that the finding removes the strongest public version of the immediate evidence.
- **Problem:** “No IEDs in the vans” is precise and strong. A broader “no explosives” or “bomb plot disproved” formulation would exceed the police statement. Petrol may be evidentially relevant, and the investigation covers possible activity outside the searched vehicles.

- **Required editorial action:** **qualify** every negative finding with “in the three vans” or “vehicle examination.” Explicitly say this does not exonerate the suspects or close other theories. Do not call petrol an incendiary component without evidence.

## Finding: The pipeline story rests on two non-equivalent measures

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** Pipeline “What changed,” strategic significance, and any BLUF wording.
- **Problem:** An anonymous official's “near pre-attack levels” claim and Kpler's 12.5 million barrels loaded at two terminals are not measurements of the same variable. Combining them can launder an unverified throughput claim through visible terminal activity.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** Vessel loading is a stock/flow and source-attribution problem. Stored oil or supply from another source can support loadings without establishing current East-West line throughput. No baseline, flow meter, or operator record was inspected.
- **Best competing interpretation:** The pipeline is operating to some material degree, while the scale and sustainability of recovery remain unknown.
- **Required editorial action:** **qualify** the headline and BLUF: Saudi official says the line is back near baseline; Kpler separately observes large terminal loadings. Never convert the 12.5 million barrels into a restoration percentage or attribute all of it to the pipeline.
