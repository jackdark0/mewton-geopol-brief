- **Atomic claims / direct evidence:** (1) Ukrainian authorities reported specific energy-site damage; (2) Zelenskyy said Ukraine was observing a pause; (3) Trump said a pause had been agreed; (4) Russia had not publicly accepted it by the cutoff; (5) Lavrov's statement cuts against treating the pause as operational.
