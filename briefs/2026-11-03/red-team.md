## Medium findings and countercases

### RT-09 — Iran drone claim needs symmetrical attribution, not false balance

The IRGC claim and the US denial are both government-lineage assertions, but they are not equally evidenced physical accounts: neither side supplied inspected proof. Present the conflict briefly and end with the missing evidence rather than inviting a vote between two unsupported assertions.

### RT-10 — Oleshky can support a limited humanitarian conclusion only

Ukraine's ombudsman said both sides upheld the evacuation obligations. That does not resolve whether either side regrouped militarily. The brief should not infer that humanitarian compliance proves military restraint.

### RT-11 — Israel inquiry and election result must remain separate propositions

An inquiry can be open while the result remains legally certifiable. The committee's refusal to delay certification does not prove no foreign activity occurred; the open inquiry does not prove the result changed. Avoid both inferences.

### RT-12 — KSE's revenue estimate is not a Russian budget number

The $17.7 billion estimate is gross oil-export revenue based on a research model. Taxes, discounts, payment frictions, ownership, and collection separate it from fiscal receipts. Keep it in Rapid Fire with the denominator and model label.

### RT-13 — Sudan complaint is an act; its allegations remain unverified

The complaint itself matters diplomatically, but the audit did not obtain a public filing or independent evidence for Ethiopian launch sites. Do not let the existence of a complaint launder its allegation into fact.

### RT-14 — NATO's FUSE language is demonstration language

Testing communications, counter-UAS, and targeting integration is not proof of fieldwide adoption, interoperability at scale, or combat performance. Use it as a sign of development priorities.

5. **Uganda transition thesis:** A phased announcement provides time for Somali and AU forces to plan. **Counter:** A long exit horizon does not guarantee financing, replacement units, or readiness, and public deadlines can aid adversary planning.
