## Candidate: D2 — Reported US gas bargain encounters an existing EU legal barrier

- **Underlying development:** The Kyiv Independent reports a US-backed proposal to restore Russian pipeline gas sales through Ukraine using an American intermediary, with possible third-country escrow. It says its own sources confirmed the idea first reported by the Wall Street Journal; named Commission spokespeople reaffirm the Russian-gas phase-out. [Reporting](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-eu-split-over-us-backed-plan-to-bring-russian-gas-back-to-europe/).
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The important issue is control of the bargain: Washington cannot alone promise access to an EU market governed by EU law. An intermediary changes the seller but does not necessarily change the gas's origin or legal treatment.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Transit income and market access could create incentives for an energy ceasefire. The bargain loses value if buyers reject it, origin rules prohibit it, Moscow refuses, or escrow cannot credibly prevent access to revenues. These are distinct obstacles.
- **What evidence is observable now:** **D2a, attributed proposal, moderate confidence:** KI reports the intermediary and escrow options, says these were discussed in September, and describes implementation as conditional on an end to war or a ceasefire. Newly reported detail is not a newly signed agreement. **D2b, FACT about EU policy, high:** the Council's January adoption notice establishes a stepwise ban, origin verification, transition periods, and a limited emergency mechanism. **D2c, attributed response, moderate:** Reuters reports Peskov saying the proposal cannot be implemented under current conditions. No accepted package or restored flow is established.
- **What remains inference:** The scheme's precise legal route, volumes, price effect, revenue allocation, enforceable escrow terms, and acceptance. A US intermediary is **not an established loophole**. Conversely, do not claim no conceivable temporary authorization exists: the primary record contains exceptions and an emergency provision. No evidence here shows these were activated for the proposal.
- **Initial priority:** **High.** S/E/U/N/A/X = 4/3/4/3/3/3; score 32. Strong major-development and contested-narrative candidate.
