## Audit method

This ledger applies the Pangburn EFCS and Replication Standard as separate, controlling standards. It uses retrieval before claims, atomic propositions, source entailment, provenance checks, two-sided evidence, and separate verdict and confidence labels. Official statements establish what an institution said or did; they do not independently prove the underlying allegation. Search snippets were used only for discovery.

Verdicts describe the evidence, not the importance of a claim:

- **CONFIRMED:** directly supported by a primary record or by strong, independent reporting.
- **SUPPORTED:** supported, but with a material provenance or access limitation.
- **CLAIMED:** attributable, consequential, and not independently established.
- **DISPUTED:** material sources directly conflict.
- **REJECTED:** the available evidence does not support the claim as framed.

### V05 — A reported US plan to restart Russian gas transit met EU law and Russian objections

**Atomic claims**

1. **SUPPORTED | Medium confidence:** The Kyiv Independent reported that US negotiators were promoting a post-ceasefire arrangement under which Russian gas could transit Ukraine through an American intermediary, with proceeds potentially held in escrow. The report relied on people familiar with the discussions. Source: [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-eu-split-over-us-backed-plan-to-bring-russian-gas-back-to-europe/).
2. **SUPPORTED | Medium confidence:** Reuters reported the Wall Street Journal's account of US support for a rapid resumption but said it could not independently verify the plan. Source: [Reuters via Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447007).
3. **CONFIRMED | High confidence:** EU Regulation 2026/261 prohibits direct and indirect imports of Russian-origin or Russian-exported natural gas on a staged timetable. It contains limited transitional exceptions for certain existing contracts and a short emergency-suspension mechanism; an intermediary or escrow account does not by itself change the gas's origin. Sources: [EUR-Lex regulation PDF](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0261), [Council of the EU summary](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/01/26/russian-gas-imports-council-gives-final-greenlight-to-a-stepwise-ban/).
4. **CONFIRMED | High confidence:** Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said transit through Ukraine was impossible under current conditions. Source: [Reuters syndication](https://www.myparaluman.ph/business-1/russian-gas-transit-via-ukraine-remains-off-the-table-kremlin-says).

**Provenance note:** The plan itself is anonymously sourced and not a published US proposal. EU law and Peskov's statement are direct constraints. The law's exceptions prevent the stronger claim that resumption is absolutely impossible under every future legal scenario.

**Editorial use:** Major story. The key point is the widening gap between a reported commercial-diplomatic concept and the legal and political conditions required to execute it.
