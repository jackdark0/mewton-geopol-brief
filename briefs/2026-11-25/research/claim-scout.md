# Claim Scout - 2026-11-25

**Cutoff:** 2026-11-25 02:00:00 America/New_York
**Window:** approximately 2026-11-23 20:00:00 to 2026-11-25 02:00:00 America/New_York

## Claim: Three U.S.-escorted tankers struck Iranian naval mines and burned

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy through Iranian state media and Telegram.
- **Why it matters:** If established, the claim would show functional Iranian mine control over U.S.-facilitated routing and a materially different hazard than projectile attacks.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The IRGC described three noncompliant tankers, U.S. aerial protection, explosions, and fires.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No names, flags, owners, imagery, distress traffic, mine remnants, or independent damage reports were supplied. JMIC said Iranian tanker mine claims had not been confirmed by official or national authorities and catalogued recent incidents as unknown-projectile strikes.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for the mine mechanism and three-ship set; yes that other tanker attacks occurred.
- **Current assessment:** unclear / unsupported at the claimed specificity.
- **Confidence:** high that the claim remains unverified; low on the true mechanism for any incident that might underlie it.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-gaza-israel-houthis-libya-november-24-2026-ba01cb036a849ba69873da5000ee08d5) reports the claim; [JMIC Update 116](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/jmic_advisory_update_116_22-nov.pdf?rev=7d2a81d10d1d467cbe75f405f2392f59) provides the contrary/narrowing incident record; [UKMTO warning 221-26](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261124-ukmto_warning_221-26.pdf) documents a separate projectile strike.
- **Questions for auditor:** Can the Iranian locations be matched to any UKMTO incident? Did any U.S. escort report an explosion, fire, or mine clearance?

## Claim: Houthis are now within 60 km of Aden and control territory around Bab el-Mandeb

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Yemeni government military spokesmen; repeated by AP and Reuters; partly supported by JMIC.
- **Why it matters:** The proposition bears on the survival of the government's interim capital and the Houthis' leverage over a global chokepoint.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Government officials acknowledged withdrawal and described a defensive line; Reuters placed the advance as close as 60 km; JMIC assessed Houthi possession of vacated coastal positions and continued pressure.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Exact coordinates, control maps, force strength, and neutral ground verification are absent. Different reports give 60 km as an advance distance or proximity, which should not be silently normalized.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial.
- **Current assessment:** supported core; exact 60 km and breadth of control require qualification.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Houthis%2Bwont%2Bseize%2BAden%2C%2BYemeni%2Bgovernment%2Bforces%2Bsay/27393395.html); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-gaza-israel-houthis-libya-november-24-2026-ba01cb036a849ba69873da5000ee08d5); [JMIC Update 116](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/jmic_advisory_update_116_22-nov.pdf?rev=7d2a81d10d1d467cbe75f405f2392f59).
- **Questions for auditor:** Separate distance advanced, distance from Aden, and control of the strait; seek imagery or neutral monitoring.

## Claim: Russia's 2027 national-defense allocation rises by roughly one-third year over year

- **Origin / principal promoters:** AP's reading of the Duma-approved budget; similar specialist reporting.
- **Why it matters:** The comparison is a concrete denominator for Russia's war-resource commitment.
- **Evidence offered for it:** 17.15 trillion rubles for 2027 compared with 12.93 trillion for 2026 implies a nominal increase of 4.22 trillion, about 32.6%.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Inflation, execution rates, classified reallocation, and category consistency can change real purchasing power and total security spending. The bill is not yet through the Federation Council.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial; public reporting appears to rely on the same budget text.
- **Current assessment:** supported as a nominal like-category comparison, pending primary-table check.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-2027-budget-economy-tax-hikes-ukraine-war-b95975f534fd290cb2fbd3eef81b4c01).
- **Questions for auditor:** Recompute from a primary budget table; state nominal versus real and authorization versus execution.

## Claim: The Huawei verdict proves the U.S. government's full racketeering narrative

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A maximal reading of DOJ rhetoric and guilty-verdict headlines.
- **Why it matters:** It can be used to justify wider technology restrictions and to characterize Huawei's entire corporate culture.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Huawei and subsidiaries were convicted on 10 counts spanning sanctions, fraud, laundering, and trade-secret conduct.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The jury acquitted Huawei of racketeering conspiracy and obstruction conspiracy. Huawei plans to appeal, and sentencing has not occurred.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes on the mixed verdict; no on the maximal narrative.
- **Current assessment:** false/overstated as phrased; the broad conviction is supported, the racketeering theory was rejected.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/chinese-tech-giant-huawei-and-subsidiaries-convicted-sanctions-violations-fraud-conspiracy); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/huawei-racketeering-trial-verdict-b39ac780e2e31140015a1be02f0329ab).
- **Questions for auditor:** Produce a count-level table before using labels such as sanctions conviction or trade-secret conviction.

## Claim: Eastern Libya will retaliate against Ukraine for two naval-officer deaths

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Eastern Libya's Libyan National Army.
- **Why it matters:** Follow-through could internationalize the Ukraine conflict further through attacks, detention, diplomatic action, or deeper Russian access.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The LNA announced two deaths and vowed unspecified retaliation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No target, timeline, decision, capability allocation, or named authorizing commander was disclosed. The deaths themselves remain attributed to one interested authority.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for the threat or casualties beyond reporting of the LNA statement.
- **Current assessment:** claimed; retaliation remains speculative.
- **Confidence:** high on claim status, low on forecast.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-libya-drone-attack-warship-officers-5719d6750c0619695c7f23a373cecd7b).
- **Questions for auditor:** Verify casualty identities and source level; define observable retaliatory acts rather than treating rhetoric as action.

## Claim: Russia's warning means war with Estonia is imminent

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Alarmist interpretation of a Russian foreign-ministry protest.
- **Why it matters:** Estonia is a NATO and EU member, so an imminent-war reading would have alliance-wide implications.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Moscow described Estonian actions as near the threshold of open war and bundled border, maritime, grain-transit, language, and alleged Ukrainian-use grievances.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No accompanying mobilization, operational order, attack, or new force posture was established in the inspected record. Estonia rejects the claim that it permits military operations from its territory, and there is no official naval blockade.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** weak as an imminence claim; supported only as escalatory rhetoric.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/11/24/russia-estonia-conflict-open-war-border-nato-ukraine-eu/) traces both governments' positions and the underlying measures.
- **Questions for auditor:** Look for force-posture indicators and direct primary statements; keep rhetoric separate from intent.
