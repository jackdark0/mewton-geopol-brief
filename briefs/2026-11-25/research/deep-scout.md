## Candidate: Russia converts 2027 war intent into a larger budget baseline

- **Underlying development:** The State Duma's final lower-house reading allocates about 17.15 trillion rubles to national defense in 2027, versus 12.93 trillion in 2026 and a prior 13.6 trillion forecast for 2027, while separate measures raise taxes on mining and investment income.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Daily battlefield reporting can obscure the fiscal structure that sustains force generation and procurement.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A higher defense baseline can preserve procurement and compensation even as growth, sanctions, interest rates, labor shortages, and refinery attacks strain civilian activity. Funding it through additional taxation spreads war costs into politically and economically sensitive sectors.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The Duma vote, budget category, comparative figures, and tax votes. The bill still goes to the Federation Council.
- **What remains inference:** Actual execution, classified off-budget spending, inflation-adjusted purchasing power, procurement mix, and whether tax pressure creates meaningful political constraint.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-2027-budget-economy-tax-hikes-ukraine-war-b95975f534fd290cb2fbd3eef81b4c01) supports the enacted lower-house step and figures.
- **Questions for auditor:** Verify like-for-like definitions; distinguish authorized spending from realized output; identify the upper-house and presidential timetable.

## Candidate: Congo uses a full export suspension to reset cobalt quotas

- **Underlying development:** ARECOMS will halt all cobalt exports from November 30 until a later decision, extinguishing automatic carryover for previously authorized volumes and reviewing the ban after four months.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** It is a regulatory change in a commodity market, but it affects battery, aerospace, and defense supply chains and the bargaining power of the world's leading producer.
- **Strategic mechanism:** By withholding exportable material, Congo can reduce visible supply, support prices, and rewrite quota allocations. The same move may encourage inventory drawdown, substitution, recycling, Indonesian production, or accelerated non-Congolese investment.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The effective date, universal scope within Congo, treatment of unused quotas, review point, and immediate withdrawal of market offers.
- **What remains inference:** Duration, enforcement leakage, stockpiles outside Congo, price persistence, and producer compliance.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/congo-halts-cobalt-exports-again-to-tackle-oversupply-support-prices-industry-regulator-says-ce7851dada89f121) states the regulator's rationale and conditions; [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/112326-drc-temporarily-suspends-cobalt-exports) details quota treatment, export data, prices, and immediate market reaction; [ACP](https://acp.cd/economie/la-rdc-suspend-la-commercialisation-du-cobalt-des-le-30-novembre-2026/) provides Congolese official-news confirmation.
- **Questions for auditor:** Quantify Congo's global production/export share from a current primary or methodological source; avoid treating price reaction as a durable supply shortage.

## Candidate: Huawei verdict creates legal leverage but not an immediate market exit

- **Underlying development:** Huawei and three subsidiaries were convicted on 10 of 12 U.S. criminal counts, including sanctions, fraud, laundering, and trade-secret offenses, while winning acquittals on racketeering and obstruction conspiracy.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The headline can be simplified into either total vindication or total conviction; the mixed disposition matters to penalties, appeal, and allied policy.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A final judgment and large penalties could strengthen U.S. diplomatic arguments for supplier restrictions and affect access to finance. But Huawei is already restricted in the United States and continues large global and Chinese-market operations, limiting immediate operational effect.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Jury verdict, charge disposition, appeal announcement, sentencing date, and existing U.S./allied restrictions.
- **What remains inference:** Penalty amount, appellate survival, effect on third-country procurement, and Chinese retaliation.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/chinese-tech-giant-huawei-and-subsidiaries-convicted-sanctions-violations-fraud-conspiracy) states the government's case and convictions; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/huawei-racketeering-trial-verdict-b39ac780e2e31140015a1be02f0329ab) reports 10 of 12 counts, acquittals, defense response, and sentencing timetable.
- **Questions for auditor:** Build a count-by-count disposition; separate trial findings from prosecutorial rhetoric; confirm corporate penalty mechanics.

## Candidate: Government withdrawal changes the Aden defense problem

- **Underlying development:** Government-aligned units left Bab el-Mandeb coastal positions, Houthis occupied the vacated ground, and government forces now describe defensive lines north and west of Aden.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The news is often framed as another battlefield movement, but the mechanism is loss of depth around an interim capital and a shipping chokepoint.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Holding coastal high ground and forcing an Aden-focused redeployment can improve Houthi surveillance, firing positions, political leverage, and ability to threaten government cohesion. It does not automatically close the strait or prove operational control of all shipping.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The acknowledged government withdrawal, JMIC's assessment of Houthi possession, and defensive preparations.
- **What remains inference:** Exact front line, force strength, logistics, command cohesion, ability to take Aden, and effect on commercial transits.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Houthis%2Bwont%2Bseize%2BAden%2C%2BYemeni%2Bgovernment%2Bforces%2Bsay/27393395.html) reports the government account and strategic redeployment; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-gaza-israel-houthis-libya-november-24-2026-ba01cb036a849ba69873da5000ee08d5) reports the defensive line; [JMIC Update 116](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/jmic_advisory_update_116_22-nov.pdf?rev=7d2a81d10d1d467cbe75f405f2392f59) assesses coastal control and continued pressure.
- **Questions for auditor:** Find imagery, independent front-line mapping, or neutral monitoring; keep territorial control separate from sea-control claims.

## Candidate: UK proposes an input-control layer for Myanmar air operations

- **Underlying development:** Alongside four new individual designations, the UK announced forthcoming legislation to stop British export and supply of aviation fuel used by Myanmar's military.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Individual sanctions are routine; the prospective fuel rule could affect trading, finance, insurance, brokerage, and transshipment if drafted broadly.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Restricting fuel and related services can raise the cost and complexity of air operations, but effectiveness depends on third-country enforcement and precise end-use rules.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Four designations and a ministerial commitment to legislate.
- **What remains inference:** Draft text, extraterritorial reach, implementation date, covered products, and measurable effects on sorties.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Sources:** [UK sanctions notice](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6b045944e676615eab145929/Sanctions_Notice__Myanmar__23_November_2026.pdf) lists operative designations; [Foreign Secretary statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/foreign-secretary-statement-to-the-house-of-commons-23-november-2026) announces the future fuel measure.
- **Questions for auditor:** Do not describe the fuel restriction as current law; reconcile the government's reference to five people with the four-addition notice.

## Candidate: NATO adds airborne warning capacity without resolving intent

- **Underlying development:** SACEUR sent an AWACS aircraft to Poland after Polish findings that three Russian Kh-101 missiles struck Polish territory.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** It is a concrete force-posture adjustment amid louder political rhetoric.
- **Strategic mechanism:** More persistent airborne warning can reduce detection gaps and improve command time, while signaling alliance attention without committing to offensive action or an Article 5 finding.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Poland's account of the North Atlantic Council meeting and announced AWACS deployment.
- **What remains inference:** Duration, supporting fighters or sensors, operational rules, Russian intent, and whether further resources follow.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [Polish mission to NATO](https://www.gov.pl/web/nato-en/north-atlantic-council-emergency-meeting-initiated-by-poland) gives the direct public account.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain NATO/SACEUR confirmation beyond Poland; avoid treating origin as proof of deliberate targeting.

## Candidate: Reported CIA open-source breach exposes a collection-layer risk

- **Underlying development:** An unclassified system used by CIA and ODNI for open-source intelligence was reportedly breached; officials say no classified system or critical capability was affected.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Unclassified data can still reveal commercial acquisitions, analytic priorities, source identities, and correlations with classified workflows.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Compromised holdings or metadata can help an adversary map collection gaps and manipulate inputs. But the system, accessed data, exfiltration, and actor remain undisclosed.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Consistent anonymous-official reporting that an unclassified OSINT system was affected and classified systems were not.
- **What remains inference:** Damage, attribution, persistence, connection to other federal incidents, and corrective action.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/cia-cyberattack-unclassified-system-bfa6d3b02e3b0e2779dfb613c900bd77) reports one familiar source; [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/11/24/hackers-breach-cia-unclassified-computer-system/) reports CIA and ODNI system impact and an ODNI assurance.
- **Questions for auditor:** Determine whether the reports share sources; seek an on-record ODNI/CIA statement and incident-scope indicators.
