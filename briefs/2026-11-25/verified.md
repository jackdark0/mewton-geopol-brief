It recorded no corroborated attack on commercial shipping in the Red Sea since August 24. [JMIC Update 116](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/jmic_advisory_update_116_22-nov.pdf?rev=7d2a81d10d1d467cbe75f405f2392f59)
