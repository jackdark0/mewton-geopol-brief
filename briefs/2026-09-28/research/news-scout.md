## Scout-wide audit flags

- The RAF Fairford, UAE visit, Nuristan incursion, Hormuz drone and Yemen junction stories are unusually attribution-sensitive. Lead with the verified investigation/announcement and label each underlying allegation.
- A page's later retrieval is not a license to import later facts. Several live pages have changing sidebar material; only the dated story or entry cited above is in scope. The Netanyahu/Channel 12 entry especially needs a stable original timestamp and independent corroboration.
- Oil-flow claims in Trump interviews involve incompatible time windows and denominators; I did not convert them into a throughput finding. Libya's “restarted” means initial units, not verified full capacity or fuel delivery.
- The strongest source gaps are original Ethiopia party statements/independent battlefield checks, a second origin for the reported UAE visit, and original records for the South Korea–Ukraine secrecy dispute. If audit cannot close these gaps, down-rank or omit rather than smoothing over them.
