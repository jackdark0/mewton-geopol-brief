## Candidate: Switzerland rejects a constitutional lock on neutrality

- **Strategic mechanism:** ASSESSMENT: Rejection preserves Bern's discretion to align with EU Russia sanctions and cooperate with European/NATO partners within existing Swiss neutrality law. It does not join NATO, authorize weapons transfers to Ukraine, or mandate any new sanction.
