## Claim: The United States has decided to strike Iran again after the midterms

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Headline and social-media extrapolation from reported US briefings to regional governments.
- **Why it matters:** Treating contingency preparation as a decision can move markets, force posture, and public expectations toward an unnecessary countdown.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Two diplomats told AP that partners were warned to prepare for potential US action after 2026-11-03 and Iranian retaliation; the United States has deployed substantial forces.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The diplomats said no final decision was communicated; the White House declined comment; no order, target list, or named decision-maker confirmed a strike.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial; AP notes related Wall Street Journal reporting, but the underlying briefings may be the same source chain.
- **Current assessment:** unclear as a decision claim; supported only as a warning and contingency-preparation claim.
- **Confidence:** high in the distinction; medium in the underlying anonymous-diplomat account.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-strikes-midterm-elections-c7df622f1e87de6035173be8fb8c4add) supports both the warning and no-decision caveat.
- **Questions for auditor:** Has any recipient government publicly confirmed the briefing? Does any source establish presidential authorization?

## Claim: Ethiopia has already taken delivery of all 12 announced Su-35 fighters

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Ethiopian state-aligned headlines and compressed secondary headlines following Abiy Ahmed's announcement.
- **Why it matters:** Delivered aircraft represent immediate inventory; an announced package represents future capability. Confusing the two overstates the current regional balance.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Ethiopian messaging described a 12-aircraft acquisition and some reports used “gets 12” language.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Rosoboronexport told Reuters that the first batch delivered that day consisted of two Su-35s. No inspected imagery or serial-number accounting establishes 12 aircraft in country, and no S-400 delivery was demonstrated.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for a first delivery; no for delivery of all 12.
- **Current assessment:** weak as a claim of 12 physically delivered; supported that at least two were delivered and a 12-aircraft package was announced.
- **Confidence:** high on the distinction; medium on the exact current inventory beyond the two acknowledged aircraft.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/ethiopia-says-taken-delivery-12-122823000.html) supports the conflicting official formulations; [Addis Media Network](https://www.amn.gov.et/en/ethiopia-gets-twelve-su-35-fighter-jets-sets-to-receive-air-defense-system-prime-minister/) supports the Ethiopian framing.
- **Questions for auditor:** Can observable imagery resolve the inventory without relying on transaction parties?

## Claim: Flávio Bolsonaro is already a fugitive from Brazilian justice

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Political commentary after his arrival in Miami and failure to surrender passports.
- **Why it matters:** “Fugitive” implies a legal status and can prematurely frame US protection as harboring a wanted person.
- **Evidence offered for it:** A court ordered passport surrender and prohibited departure; police say the passports were not delivered and no exit appears in Brazilian border records.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The Federal Police explicitly said those restrictions alone do not make him a fugitive or authorize arrest. No inspected source showed a final judicial finding of evasion or an arrest warrant by the cutoff.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for his presence in Miami and the police investigation; no for formal fugitive status.
- **Current assessment:** false as a settled legal label at the cutoff; supported that police are investigating noncompliance and an unrecorded departure.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Brazil Federal Police](https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/nota-a-imprensa-43) states the legal distinction; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/brazil-election-flavio-bolsonaro-ecuador-argentina-9fc1f409ed993403736dbae07bd74bd8) supports his US arrival.
- **Questions for auditor:** Keep any post-cutoff warrant or status change out of this fixed edition.

## Claim: More than 100 civilians were killed in a Sudanese army drone strike on Nyala market

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Emergency Lawyers and other Sudanese activist or aligned networks; some reports attribute the strike to the Sudanese Armed Forces.
- **Why it matters:** The toll, civilian share, weapon, and perpetrator affect atrocity assessment and escalation narratives.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Witnesses described a crowded market; Emergency Lawyers reported more than 100 dead; MSF treated a mass influx and called it a drone strike.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** MSF directly recorded 36 bodies and more than 150 wounded, while another medical network reported at least 21 dead. AP witnesses described many RSF fighters and women vendors at the site. No inspected forensic or imagery evidence establishes the platform or perpetrator.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for a large casualty event; partial for a civilian toll; no for the highest toll and attribution.
- **Current assessment:** unclear as stated. A minimum of 36 bodies received by MSF is supported; total deaths, civilian proportion, weapon, and attribution remain disputed.
- **Confidence:** high in the minimum/uncertainty distinction; medium in any aggregate estimate.
- **Sources:** [MSF](https://msf.org.uk/article/sudan-msf-teams-witness-mass-casualty-event-following-appalling-drone-strike-market) supports its treatment count; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/sudan-darfur-nyala-market-attack-012abd09b3cd9ca20a733f5eb114d588) compares witnesses and competing tolls.
- **Questions for auditor:** Can satellite imagery, munition remnants, hospital lists, or named victim records resolve any component?
