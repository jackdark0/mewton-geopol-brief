## Story: Lula wins Brazil's runoff; Bolsonaro challenges the declared result

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED]** Brazil's Superior Electoral Court announced Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's reelection with 64,256,232 votes, 51.79% of valid votes, against Flávio Bolsonaro's 59,826,041 and 48.21%. The official denominator is valid votes, not eligible voters or turnout. [TSE](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Outubro/presidente-do-tse-anuncia-reeleicao-de-lula-e-faz-balanco-do-2o-turno-das-eleicoes-2026)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The Supreme Court extended voting by two hours only at stations still open. The order came after the scheduled closing time; thousands of machines were already closed and could not reopen, and tally receipt was paused. [TSE](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Outubro/presidente-do-tse-anuncia-reeleicao-de-lula-e-faz-balanco-do-2o-turno-das-eleicoes-2026)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Bolsonaro refused to concede and asked that the result be annulled. Both campaigns had requested extensions for different access problems. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/brazil-election-lula-bolsonaro-runoff-vote-president-e2c9fe8fc4aa3e3675b9a8ec15405d93)
- **[SUPPORTED]** The procedural disruption is real, but the inspected record does not establish an outcome-changing effect. The number and distribution of post-17:00 votes remain undisclosed.

### Material claims not established

- That the extension changed the winner, met the legal threshold for annulment, or reflected fraud.
- That U.S. tariffs or sanctions determined the vote. The election result cannot supply that causal finding.

### Source provenance

- TSE is primary for its announced count and administrative sequence, but not independent proof that every procedure was flawless.
- AP independently observed closures in Brasília and Rio and reported the two campaigns' requests. Its legal assessment quotes an outside law professor but is not a court ruling.

### Open questions

- Was a petition filed before the cutoff, and what remedy and evidence did it contain?
- How many stations and voters were affected after 17:00, by region and prior voting access?

### Recommendation

- include major; carry annulment grounds into contested claims.
