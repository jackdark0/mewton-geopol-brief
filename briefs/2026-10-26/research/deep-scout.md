Its continuity language limits claims of an expansion; the absence of an end date or operational detail limits material-effect estimates. The joint statement provides corroborating official context, not an independent operational evidence stream.
- **Initial priority:** High within this scout's list; potentially a supporting paragraph within the broader Saudi story.
- **Verdict / confidence:** SUPPORTED / High for the announced continuation decision. HOLD / Low for any claim of added capability or verified combat effect.
