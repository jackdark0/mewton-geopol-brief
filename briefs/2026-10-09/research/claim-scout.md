## Claim: C001 — Russia has carried out successful sabotage against Danish defence companies supplying Ukraine

This is the stronger proposition being tested, not a quotation from PET. The inspected Reuters version now says attempted sabotage. Attribution confidence: high for the published institutional allegation, incomplete for the underlying operations.
- **Why it matters:** Successful disruption of weapons production and preparation for an attack imply different immediate costs and escalation risks. Transferring evidence from Europe generally to Denmark would inflate the demonstrated campaign.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Reuters reports a PET official alleging Russian attempts directed at Danish suppliers. PET's September primary releases had already described concrete planning and preparation targeting defence industry and Ukraine support.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The updated report distinguishes operations carried out across Europe from attempts in Denmark. It identifies no Danish company, physical damage, successful attack, forensic record or whether attempts were prevented. The Russian embassy rejected the allegations; denial is not exculpatory proof.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for successful Danish sabotage; partial for PET's publicly documented threat assessment. A primary PET statement and Reuters reporting of PET remain one institutional attribution lineage.
- **Current assessment:** weak. Successful Danish sabotage is not established; do not label it false merely because operational evidence is unavailable.
- **Confidence:** medium. Strong confidence in the narrower published wording; weaker confidence in what undisclosed evidence could establish.
