## Candidate: Egypt's defence council commits support to Saudi Arabia, but parliament and mission remain open

- **Event time/date:** 2026-10-18, before 11:59 a.m. Cairo time.
- **Region / actors:** Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Yemen's Houthis.
- **What changed:** Egypt's National Defense Council resolved to provide Saudi Arabia with "all necessary and possible forms of support" for its security, resources, territory, and Islamic holy sites. The official statement did not announce a force package or deployment. Reuters reported that parliamentary discussions on the separate authorization for an overseas mission would continue Monday.
