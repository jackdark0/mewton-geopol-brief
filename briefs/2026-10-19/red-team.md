- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** "An unidentified object tests NATO's sensor-to-decision chain over Lithuania"; the sentence saying the object approached from Poland.
- **Problem:** The analysis risks converting Lithuania's preliminary track direction into an established route even though Poland said its radar did not confirm a crossing.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** The verified ledger contains conflicting limits: Lithuanian authorities described approach from the Polish side, while Poland did not confirm a violation. No common radar reconstruction was public.

- **Required editorial action:** **qualify** as "Lithuania said it approached from the Polish direction" and keep Poland's nonconfirmation adjacent.
