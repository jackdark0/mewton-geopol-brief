# News Scout — 2026-10-23

**Frozen research cutoff:** 2026-10-23 02:00:00 America/New_York (06:00:00 UTC).  
**Window:** 2026-10-21 20:00:00 through 2026-10-23 02:00:00 America/New_York (2026-10-22 00:00:00 through 2026-10-23 06:00:00 UTC).  
**No-post-cutoff rule:** No later event, statement, publication, or source update is admissible. Retrieval occurred after the frozen cutoff; publication and event dates are checked separately. Dynamic sidebars and related-story widgets are excluded. A source without demonstrable pre-cutoff publication is marked **TIME HOLD** and supplies a retrieval lead only, not in-window evidence.

**Role and method:** Stage 1 discovery only; no final audit or finished brief. Read the repository instructions, kickoff, priority/source policies, template, role, and both Pangburn sibling standards in full. The Empirical Fact-Checking System and Forensic Fact-Checking Replication Standard retain separate numbering and are applied by name here. Prior edition (October 22) was inspected for continuity only. All descriptions below are paraphrases, not quotations. FACT concerns a record or observed act; CLAIM concerns an attributed account; ASSESSMENT is the scout's inference. A preliminary verdict classifies a proposition; confidence describes how securely that classification is supported.

**Triage:** 18 distinct candidates considered; 12 ranked verification priorities. Counts describe this deliberately selected candidate set, not the world's news or an error rate. S/E/U/N/A/Q = strategic consequence (0–5), escalation potential (0–5), US relevance (0–4), novelty (0–3), discussion value (0–3), contested uncertainty (0–3). Score = 3S + 2E + 2U + N + A; Q is recorded separately and is not subtracted. Scores are editorial sorting aids, not calibrated probabilities. Overlapping Gulf claims should be merged in the final product rather than counted as separate major developments.
