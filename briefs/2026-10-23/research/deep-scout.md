Its strategic effect depends on definitions, exceptions, enforcement, and available alternative investment, rather than the number of countries named.
- **What evidence is observable now:** A published Treasury announcement, a 163-page draft regulation, and a ten-page fact sheet. Announcement/publication: October 22, exact hour unavailable; Washington date places publication pre-cutoff. The draft is expressly pending Federal Register processing and may receive editorial changes.

The draft retains the statutory phrase concerning Venezuela under Maduro, while its explanatory text says the currently recognized government does not fit that definition. A headline saying Venezuela was simply removed is less precise than that mechanism. The US recognition history is older context, not a new October 22 event.
- **Initial priority:** high. Dimensions strategic/escalation/US/novelty/discussion = 5/2/4/3/3; triage score 33. Uncertainty 2/3.

Permits cannot by themselves solve those constraints. The percentage is a share of property area in a specified transaction, not the share of Ukraine's defense industry protected underground.
