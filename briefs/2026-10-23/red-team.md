## Finding: The Ukraine strike and satellite dispute are concurrent, not causally linked

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** “The strike and the service dispute reinforce each other” and the combined lead-story framing.
- **Problem:** The analysis could invite readers to infer that the reported restrictions degraded Ukraine's response to this specific attack. The verified record establishes a large strike and an unresolved service dispute in the same window, but not an operational causal link between them.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** No inspected source connected a particular failed interception, casualty, command delay, or targeting problem to Starlink or Starshield restrictions.
- **Best competing interpretation:** The two developments illustrate a general dependency risk but were operationally unrelated in this episode.
- **Required editorial action:** Qualify. Pair them as simultaneous strategic pressures while stating that no effect on the night's defense was established.

## Finding: The Treasury draft may be narrower in practice than the sector list sounds

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** “a larger U.S. outbound-investment security perimeter” and any “technology containment” framing.
- **Problem:** A list of additional technology areas can sound like a blanket sector ban. The proposal includes prohibited and notifiable transactions, definitions, exceptions, and technical country rules; final scope can change.
