## Audit method

The audit decomposed each candidate into atomic claims, checked source publication time against the fixed cutoff, traced outlet reports to named or anonymous underlying sources, and separated reported fact from official claim, inference, and forecast. Multiple articles that repeat one wire report or one official statement count as one lineage. Verdict and confidence are recorded separately.

## High-confidence facts

### 1. Russia launched a large overnight attack on Ukraine; Kyiv was hit in three ballistic-missile waves

- **Verdict:** Verified, with quantities attributed to Ukrainian authorities.
- **Confidence:** High for the occurrence and broad scale; medium-high for the official tallies.
- **Atomic claims:**
  - Ukraine's Air Force said the attack began at 18:00 on October 21 and involved 210 drones, of which 181 were destroyed or suppressed by 09:00 on October 22.
  - Reuters reported that Ukraine said Russia also launched 21 missiles and that Kyiv was struck in three waves of ballistic missiles.
  - Ukrainian officials reported civilian deaths and injuries. Exact casualty totals were still liable to change during the window and should be attributed and time-bounded.
- **Evidence for:** [Ukrainska Pravda, citing the Air Force](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/22/8059193/); [Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/10/22/russia-ukraine-exchange-strikes-as-us-envoy-leaves-after-talks).
- **Evidence against / limits:** Neither source independently verifies every official count. The Air Force page did not give a missile total. The evidence does not support claims about Russia's remaining stockpile or a durable change in strike capacity.
- **Use:** Lead story. Keep the numbers attributed.

### 2. Saudi Aramco told remaining Dhahran headquarters staff to work remotely; no production halt was established

- **Verdict:** Credible, multiply sourced report of a precautionary personnel measure.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high for the remote-work order; low on its trigger and operational significance.
- **Atomic claims:**
  - Reuters cited six people familiar with the matter who said Aramco told staff to leave its Dhahran headquarters and work remotely.
  - AFP separately cited two sources who said remaining staff were told to work from home until further notice; residential areas were not evacuated.
  - Neither report established a production halt or independently verified earlier damage claims.
- **Evidence for:** [Reuters](https://au.marketscreener.com/news/saudi-aramco-tells-staff-to-evacuate-dhahran-as-clashes-with-yemen-s-houthis-continue-ce785cdedb88fe26); [AFP](https://www.vsatdaily.com/reports/261022131710.g6srm5v2.html).
- **Evidence against / limits:** Aramco did not comment. The reason for the instruction was unclear. Two reports are partly independent but both depend on anonymous human sources rather than a company notice available to the audit.
- **Use:** Major story as an energy-security risk signal. Do not imply lost output.

### 3. Ukraine reported continuing Starlink/Starshield restrictions despite President Trump's restoration claim

- **Verdict:** The public record was contradictory at the cutoff; full restoration was not established.
- **Confidence:** High that the contradiction existed; low-to-medium on the precise technical state and decision chain.
- **Atomic claims:**
  - President Trump said access had been restored.
  - President Zelensky said speed restrictions remained, and Ukrainian officials told reporters that functionality had not fully returned.
  - AP reported conflicting accounts from Trump, Vice President Vance, Secretary Rubio, Secretary Hegseth, Elon Musk, and Ukrainian officials about the restrictions and who controlled them.
- **Evidence for restoration claim:** [Reuters](https://wsau.com/2026/10/22/trump-asks-musk-to-lift-ukraine-starlink-restrictions-wsj-reports/); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/starlink-starshield-ukraine-russia-trump-vance-musk-1a9279f37cfd4d2a65c8ce4cc3af6f41).
- **Evidence against full restoration:** [Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/10/22/russia-ukraine-exchange-strikes-as-us-envoy-leaves-after-talks); [RFE/RL](https://config-exception.rferl.mobi/a/ukraine-russia-war-starlink-starshield-congress/33881387.html); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/starlink-starshield-ukraine-russia-trump-vance-musk-1a9279f37cfd4d2a65c8ce4cc3af6f41).
- **Evidence limits:** Public reporting did not establish a single technical definition of “restored,” did not resolve whether Starlink and Starshield controls were identical, and did not settle responsibility among the company, military, and White House.
- **Use:** Pair with the attack story. State the contradiction, not a resolved verdict on motive or command authority.

### 4. Treasury proposed a broader outbound-investment security regime; it is not yet effective

- **Verdict:** Verified official proposal.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Atomic claims:**
  - Treasury issued a notice of proposed rulemaking on October 22 under the COINS Act.
  - The proposal would expand covered technologies to include areas such as high-performance computing and supercomputing, hypersonics, and biotechnology.
  - Comments are due December 7, 2026.
  - The proposal is not a final rule and has no current effective date.
- **Evidence for:** [U.S. Treasury release](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0667); [Treasury fact sheet and FAQs](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/206/Fact-Sheet-FAQs-COINS-Act-NPRM.pdf).
- **Evidence against / limits:** Not every covered transaction would be prohibited; some would be subject to notification and exceptions. The country definitions are technical and should not be compressed into an unsupported blanket ban. Final scope may change.
- **Use:** Major story. Call it a proposed expansion, never an enacted ban.

### 6. Ukraine opened a framework for privately financed protected defense production and partner-funded component imports

- **Verdict:** Verified government decisions.
- **Confidence:** High for the policy announcements; low for eventual capacity effects.
- **Atomic claims:**
  - Ukraine's government approved a mechanism for private investors to build protected underground production facilities for eligible defense firms.
  - Official descriptions include a state participation condition of at least 25 percent in relevant projects.
  - The government also approved customs-duty and VAT relief for defense components imported with partner funding.
- **Evidence for:** [Ukraine Ministry of Defence](https://mod.gov.ua/news/uriad-sprostyv-pidzemne-budivnytstvo-dlia-pidpryiemstv-opk); [Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/uriad-ukhvalyv-nyzku-rishen-dlia-oboronno-promyslovoho-kompleksu).

- **Use:** Major story. Separate policy design from industrial result.

### 7. The Philippines is exploring cable-security cooperation with Taiwan; no agreement or joint patrol exists

## Contested or unsupported claims

### Iran's claim that seven vessels were struck or mined

- **Claim:** The IRGC said four vessels carrying military equipment were struck and three other vessels hit mines in Gulf waters.
- **Verdict:** Unverified official claim.
- **Confidence:** High that Iran made the claim; low that the seven incidents occurred as described.
- **Evidence for:** [Press TV relay of the IRGC statement](https://www.presstv.co.uk/Detail/2026/10/22/778916/IRGC-Navy-four-vessels-hit-Persian-Gulf); [AP report of the claim](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-yemen-saudi-israel-october-22-2026-dde5b98a8bf961224514994b02babce9).
- **Evidence against / missing:** No ship identities, flags, owners, casualty reports, imagery, port records, or independent maritime-security confirmation were available by the cutoff.
- **Editorial ruling:** Include only in the contested-claims section. Do not say seven ships were hit as fact.

### President Trump's allegation that the Iranian vessel account was fabricated

- **Claim:** Trump alleged that Iran fabricated the ship-strike account.
- **Verdict:** Unsupported counterclaim.
- **Confidence:** High that he made the allegation; low on the truth of the allegation.
- **Evidence for:** The absence of independent corroboration weakens Iran's account but does not prove fabrication.
- **Evidence against / missing:** No positive evidence of fabrication was presented. Failure to corroborate is not proof of invention.
- **Editorial ruling:** Present as a counterclaim, not a fact-check verdict.

### “Hormuz flows are above prewar levels”

- **Claim:** Energy exports through the Strait of Hormuz were said to have exceeded prewar levels.
- **Verdict:** Scope-dependent and misleading without a denominator.
- **Confidence:** Medium.
- **Evidence for:** Some reporting described broader Gulf exports, including bypass routes, as recovering above a prewar comparison point.
- **Evidence against / missing:** Direct Hormuz transit and total Gulf exports are different denominators; reporting in the scout file put direct Hormuz flows at roughly 82 percent of the comparison level for the week through October 18.
- **Editorial ruling:** Do not use a single “above prewar” line. Specify route, product, dates, and comparison period or omit.

### Starlink was “fully restored” and a single actor controlled the restriction

- **Claim:** Access was fully restored and responsibility lay clearly with either the company or the U.S. administration.
- **Verdict:** Not established.
- **Confidence:** High that public accounts conflict; low on a definitive control chain.
- **Evidence for / against:** See the verified Starlink ledger above.
- **Editorial ruling:** State the contradiction and the operational uncertainty. Do not infer motive.
