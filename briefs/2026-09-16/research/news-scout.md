## Candidate: House passes a third Iran war-powers resolution

- **Event time/date:** 2026-09-15 22:32 America/New_York.
- **Region / actors:** United States / Iran; U.S. House, Trump administration.
- **What changed:** The House agreed 220-204 to H. Con. Res. 93, directing removal of U.S. forces from hostilities with Iran. Seven Republicans joined 213 Democrats. The White House had opposed the measure and argued that a concurrent resolution lacks the force of law.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It is the third House vote against the war and shows widening, though still limited, Republican defections.
  - It does not by itself end operations; executive-legislative conflict over war powers remains unresolved.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** House roll call is primary; AP independently reports the political history and limits. The White House policy statement establishes the administration's position, not the legal merits.
- **Sources:** [House Clerk roll call](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026307?Page=1) — vote, time, tally, party split; [White House statement](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SAP-HCR93.pdf) — administration opposition and legal position; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-powers-resolution-8519dae8197214b4edf2192e174c82f4) — context and prior failed or reversed efforts.
- **Questions for auditor:** What Senate action would be needed now? How should the brief describe the disputed legal effect without adopting either side's conclusion?

## Candidate: CBO prices the Iran war and flags interceptor depletion

- **Event time/date:** Report dated 2026-09-15.
- **Region / actors:** United States / Iran; Congressional Budget Office, Department of Defense.
- **What changed:** CBO estimated about $38 billion in Pentagon costs through August 1, with another month costing roughly $2 billion at low May-June intensity or $3 billion at July intensity. CBO called depleted missile-defense interceptors the conflict's main military opportunity cost and warned inventories would remain reduced for years.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It converts a political argument about war costs into a nonpartisan, documented estimate.
  - Interceptor depletion affects readiness for other missile-heavy contingencies, including a possible Taiwan conflict.
  - CBO said the estimates carry considerable uncertainty because DoD did not answer its information requests.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** CBO is the primary estimate; AP and Reuters repeat it. DoD's earlier estimate and Inspector General report are separate official evidence streams with different dates and scopes.
- **Sources:** [CBO](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/62756) — methods, $38 billion estimate, monthly scenarios, opportunity cost, uncertainty; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-cbo-estimate-b99089b11749f04bbc8ddb1b3c64a373) — political context and limitations; [Lead Inspector General report](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Sep/09/2003993626/-1/-1/1/OEF_Q3_JUN2026_FINAL_508%20SECURE.PDF) — earlier official $33.4 billion estimate and munitions bottlenecks.
- **Questions for auditor:** Do not add unlike cost categories. Verify whether any precise stockpile fraction or replenishment time appears in CBO's own document before using it.

## Candidate: NATO jet shoots down a drone over Lithuania

- **Event time/date:** Shortly after midnight local time, 2026-09-15.
- **Region / actors:** Lithuania, Belarus, NATO, Italy; possible Russia/Ukraine connection.
- **What changed:** An Italian fighter in NATO's Baltic mission shot down a drone near Pratkūnai after it spent about 30 minutes in Lithuanian airspace. Lithuania said it likely entered from Belarus and that an explosive device was found and neutralized. Authorities had not established origin or purpose by cutoff.
- **Why this may matter:**

- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high; low for attribution.
- **Source chain:** Lithuanian defense ministry and crisis center are primary; Reuters and AP are independent reporting but rely on those officials for the core event. Italy's defense minister offered a stronger Russian-origin assessment that Lithuania had not confirmed.
- **Sources:** [Lithuanian Ministry of Defense](https://kam.lt/r-kaunas-su-italijos-gynybos-ministru-nakties-incidentas-patvirtino-kad-esame-vienas-aljansas/) — confirms successful destruction and NATO mission context; [Reuters](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/09/15/nato-fighter-shoots-down-drone-in-lithuania) — time, route, unresolved origin; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-lithuania-drone-downed-nato-9671662649ebed2eca41879982f366ad) — explosive finding and official caution.
