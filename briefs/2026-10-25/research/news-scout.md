## Method and temporal firewall

Searches used October 23–25 date variants and correction/alternative formulations. This produced **16 distinct candidate clusters**, including two explicitly rejected continuity items. **11** merit auditor attention, some conditionally pending retrieval.

Only article bodies successfully opened are treated as inspected evidence. Search results, including long search extracts, remain discovery only. Several useful pages repeatedly returned fetch errors; these have HOLD status and cannot support publication until retrieved. A successful search result does not cure a failed source opening. Reuters syndications are one lineage; AP syndications are one lineage.

No source first published or updated after the frozen cutoff is admitted as in-window evidence. Inspected pages display October 24 or older publication/version dates; where an update clock or timezone is absent, the version limitation is explicit below. Navigational headlines and live widgets are excluded. For mutable stories, the auditor must preserve or independently establish the pre-cutoff version. Source bodies are paraphrased, not quoted, unless expressly identified otherwise. No numeric confidence probabilities or global misinformation percentages are generated.

The Pangburn AI Empirical Fact-Checking System and Pangburn Forensic Fact-Checking Replication Standard are separate controlling sibling documents. Their section/step numbering is not combined.
