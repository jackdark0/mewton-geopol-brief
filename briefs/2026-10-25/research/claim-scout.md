## C-01 — Claim: Ukraine's monitored Starlink/Starshield connections were stable while equipment moved above 400 km/h on October 24

- **Exact proposition / type / scope:** Ukraine's Defense Ministry recorded stable connections above 400 km/h on October 24. Empirical technical-status claim, restricted to the monitoring described; it is not proof that every terminal, every location, or every mission was restored.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Ukrainian Defense Ministry and Defense Minister Yevhenii Khmara; attribution high for the ministry's own published statement.
- **Why it matters:** The proposition changes the immediate account of a restriction affecting wartime communications and drones. It is stronger evidence of recovery than a political assurance alone, but the measurement's coverage matters.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The [ministry statement](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/starlink-and-starshield-speed-restored-in-ukraine), dated October 24, 12:18 PM, says its monitoring recorded restored operating speed and stable connectivity above 400 km/h. It says monitoring continues. This is the claimant's direct report, not released raw telemetry.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No terminal count, regional distribution, equipment list, test duration, failure rate, raw log, or independent testing dataset is supplied. “400 km/h” describes movement speed, not data throughput. Earlier reports of continuing limits are temporally compatible with a subsequent recovery and do not by themselves refute it.
- **Independent corroboration:** Partial. [AP reporting inspected through CityNews](https://edmonton.citynews.ca/2026/10/24/musk-says-he-was-behind-starlink-speed-restriction-after-service-in-ukraine-is-restored/) reports Zelenskyy's restoration announcement. It independently reports the announcement, rather than independently testing the network.
- **Provenance lineage:** Ministry monitoring → ministry statement; Zelenskyy's public statement → AP/other reports. These are related Ukrainian official streams, not independent technical observations. Reuters and AP repeating the same authorities do not multiply the number of network tests.
- **Current assessment:** Supported in the bounded, attributed form; universal restoration remains unestablished.
- **Confidence:** Medium on the operational proposition; high that the ministry published it. Missing methods limit confidence more than source quantity does.
- **Sources / temporal fit:** Ministry article body, heading and paragraphs on monitoring; October 24 printed stamp says EET although Kyiv was still on summer time that date. That label inconsistency does not affect inclusion within this window. CityNews/AP body, paragraphs on restoration; posted October 24 04:46 and last updated October 24 15:06, zone not printed. Both displayed article versions precede the frozen cutoff; ignore current sidebars.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain raw or independently witnessed tests; identify terminal/product types and coverage. Compare the Ukrainian-language ministry page. Preserve the distinction between movement speed and bandwidth. Do not carry yesterday's denial forward as today's service state.

## C-02 — Claim: Ukraine violated commercial Starlink terms by using it for offensive drone strikes

- **Exact proposition / type / scope:** Ukrainian military offensive drone use of commercial Starlink breached applicable contractual terms during the episode Musk addressed on October 24. Legal/contractual and empirical claim; separate from whether SpaceX closed a technical loophole, and separate from Starshield's military permissions.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Elon Musk, according to inspected AP and Reuters reporting of his October 24 X post. The original post was not independently recovered here; attribution is medium-high through direct reporting, not an audio/video verification.
- **Why it matters:** A claimed contract violation can explain or justify restrictions in public debate. Product confusion could make a commercially valid rule appear to settle the separate question of restrictions on a U.S. military service.
- **Evidence offered for it:** [AP/CityNews](https://edmonton.citynews.ca/2026/10/24/musk-says-he-was-behind-starlink-speed-restriction-after-service-in-ukraine-is-restored/) reports Musk's assertion and his account of closing a loophole. It distinguishes his position on commercial Starlink from his claim of U.S. control over Starshield.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** [Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/ukraine-says-starlink-starshield-operating-091314000.html) reports adviser Serhiy Beskrestnov's denial that Ukraine could use Starlink for long- and medium-range attack-drone operations. That denial's scope is narrower than all possible offensive uses. No applicable contract, identified terminal, particular mission, violation notice, or technical account of the loophole was inspected.
- **Independent corroboration:** No for the breach. AP and Reuters can corroborate that the accusation was made; both draw the accusation from Musk. The Ukrainian response is a competing interested account, not a contractual adjudication.
- **Provenance lineage:** Musk X post → AP and Reuters; Beskrestnov Telegram post → Reuters. The two wire services are separate reporting organizations but share the underlying speakers for this proposition. No inference of deception follows from the disagreement.
- **Current assessment:** Unclear; hold the breach conclusion pending contract and mission evidence. Musk's statement is reportable with attribution.
- **Confidence:** Medium that the claim remains unresolved; low on the underlying breach finding.
- **Sources / temporal fit:** AP/CityNews article body under the section about Musk, October 24 posted/updated stamps noted in C-01. Reuters/AOL, by Yuliia Dysa, updated October 24 20:57 UTC, accusation and adviser-response paragraphs. These are pre-cutoff versions. The [UNN report](https://unn.ua/en/news/musk-said-that-ukraine-violated-the-rules-for-using-starlink-for-drone-strikes) was found only in search and is a lead, not additional evidence.
- **Questions for auditor:** Retrieve the original X and Telegram posts with timestamps; inspect the terms effective for the actual terminals; identify whether commercial Starlink or Starshield enabled the disputed missions. Do not turn a denial of long-range use into proof of no offensive use anywhere.

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Saudi official accounts describe missiles toward Makkah/Madinah regions; stronger holy-city targeting language appears in coverage and religious condemnations. Houthis deny targeting Mecca and Medina. Do not attribute the strongest formulation to every Saudi agency.
- **Why it matters:** Targeting holy places has potentially exceptional mobilizing and diplomatic consequences. Slippage from region to city to shrine can exaggerate intent while leaving the real civilian hazard under-described.
- **Evidence offered for it:** [Arab News's inspected Civil Defense account](https://www.arabnews.com/saudi-arabia/debris-falls-in-makkah-jeddah-taif-after-interception-of-houthi-missiles-3008090) reports intercepted missiles and debris in Wadi Al-Mughammas, Jeddah, Taif, and Rabigh. That supports an attributed debris/hazard account; it does not itself establish the missiles' aim points.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** [Reuters through the Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/iran-backed-houthis-claim-they-didnt-attack-mecca-and-medina-after-saudis-warned-of-falling-debris/) records the Houthi denial and claim of oil/military targets. No radar track, impact ellipse, recovered guidance data, authenticated targeting order, or independently assessed launch trajectory was inspected. The denial also does not prove which facilities were targeted.
- **Independent corroboration:** Partial for the competing statements; no independent confirmation of intended holy-city or shrine targets in this packet.
- **Provenance lineage:** Civil Defense statement → Arab News/other reporting; Houthi statement → Reuters → Times of Israel. Different publications repeating Civil Defense remain one location/attribution stream. The Reuters item is a separate report of the denial, not independent trajectory analysis.
- **Current assessment:** Unclear; retain the attributed interception/debris account and hold the intentional holy-site claim. Do not label the absence of injuries in a statement as a verified zero-casualty count.
- **Confidence:** Medium on this limitation; low on intent. Missing primary operational evidence is decisive.
- **Sources / temporal fit:** Arab News published October 24 20:39 and updated October 24 23:33 (Saudi publication context; zone not printed). Times of Israel/Reuters liveblog item has a fixed October 24 21:24 stamp (site-local zone not printed); exclude the rest of the changing liveblog and current related-story sidebar.
