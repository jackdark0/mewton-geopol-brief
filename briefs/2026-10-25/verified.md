## Story: Ukraine reports Starlink and Starshield speeds restored

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **CONFIRMED:** Ukraine's Defense Ministry said on October 24 that its monitoring showed Starlink and Starshield operating speeds had been restored, including stable connectivity on platforms moving faster than 400 km/h. The 400 km/h figure describes platform movement, not data throughput. [Ukraine Ministry of Defense](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/starlink-and-starshield-speed-restored-in-ukraine)
- **CONFIRMED:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said restoration began overnight and that Ukrainian tests showed service at pre-restriction speeds. [Office of the President of Ukraine](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/vidnovili-robotu-starlinku-starshildu-na-shvidkosti-yaka-bul-107025)
- **SUPPORTED:** Elon Musk said SpaceX had throttled commercial Starlink because Ukraine violated service terms by using it for offensive drones. He separately said the U.S. government controlled Starshield. President Donald Trump said he did not order the restriction; Secretaries Marco Rubio and Pete Hegseth denied administration involvement. [Associated Press via CityNews](https://edmonton.citynews.ca/2026/10/24/musk-says-he-was-behind-starlink-speed-restriction-after-service-in-ukraine-is-restored/)

### Material claims not established

- The Ukrainian statements do not publish test geography, sample size, duration, throughput, latency, or terminal class. They support a bounded operational recovery, not proof that every affected terminal is restored.
- No inspected contract, mission order, audit log, or written U.S. directive resolves whether the commercial service breached its terms or whether a U.S. official ordered any Starshield change.

### Source provenance

- The two Ukrainian statements are separate official publications but share the same Ukrainian operational monitoring and are not independent technical audits.
- The AP report independently records public statements by Musk and senior U.S. officials; it does not independently prove their competing accounts.

### Open questions

- Which terminal classes and operating areas were tested, and what authority governs future restrictions on Starshield during combat?

### Recommendation

- include major; carry the decision-chain dispute into contested claims.

[The Straits Times via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/10/24/gas-supply-cut-from-indonesia-accounts-for-less-than-2-of-singapores-daily-gas-demand-ema)

[Singapore Energy Market Authority](https://www.ema.gov.sg/our-energy-story/energy-market-landscape/gas) [EMA supply FAQ](https://www.ema.gov.sg/resources/faqs/energy-prices-in-singapore-what-you-need-to-know/how-singapores-energy-supply-affects-you/how-is-ema-ensuring-singapore-has-sufficient-fuel-supply-for-power-generation)

### Source provenance

### Open questions
