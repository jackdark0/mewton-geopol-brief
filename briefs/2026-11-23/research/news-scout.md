## Candidate: Trump sets a New Year decision point for renewed Iran strikes

- **Event time/date:** November 22, before 17:56 UTC.
- **Region / actors:** United States, Iran, Gulf states.
- **What changed:** Fox News correspondent Trey Yingst reported that President Donald Trump said he was inclined to resume strikes on Iran after the New Year if no agreement was reached. Trump also threatened that Iranian vessels trying to run the U.S. naval blockade would be sunk. This is a conditional public threat, not an attack order or a fixed deadline in an announced agreement.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It gives diplomacy a visible political horizon while preserving military escalation.
  - The shipping threat raises the risk that a disputed tanker encounter becomes a U.S.-Iran clash.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** medium-high; the statement is reported from a direct Fox interview, but the full primary transcript was not available.
- **Source chain:** Iran International and other reports repeat Fox correspondent Trey Yingst's account; they are one lineage.
- **Sources:** [Iran International summary of the Fox interview](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611224547); [Daily Beast report with the quoted conditional threat](https://www.thedailybeast.com/trump-drops-ominous-hint-about-his-new-years-plans-for-iran).
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain the full Fox clip or transcript; distinguish “inclined” from an operational decision; verify whether “after the New Year” or “by year-end” was the exact condition.
