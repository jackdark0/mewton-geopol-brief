## Candidate: Trump's Iran threat links diplomacy, blockade enforcement and force posture

- **Underlying development:** President Trump told Fox News he was inclined to resume strikes after the New Year absent a deal and threatened to sink Iranian vessels attempting to run the U.S. blockade.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The headline threat can obscure the operational coupling: negotiating delay, maritime interdiction and air-strike options now share one escalation ladder.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A public time horizon may increase bargaining pressure, but a shipping encounter could trigger escalation before the stated horizon. The threat also narrows room for de-escalation if Tehran tests the blockade for domestic or economic reasons.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Trump made the conditional statement; indirect talks continue; UKMTO recorded another tanker-military incident off Chabahar.
- **What remains inference:** Whether the White House has chosen targets, set a deadline, or authorized a strike package; whether the UKMTO event involved U.S. forces.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Iran International on the Fox account](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611224547); [UKMTO Advisory 217-26](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261122_ukmto_-advisory_217-26.pdf); [Reuters on Bahrain's latest attack and competing claims](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/11/22/bahrain-warns-residents-of-potential-threat-as-iran-conflict-persists).
- **Questions for auditor:** Full interview wording; current U.S. rules of engagement; confirmed mediation terms; vessel identity and action off Chabahar.

## Candidate: Benghazi strike tests the boundaries of the Ukraine war and Libya's partition

- **Underlying development:** A Russian corvette was severely damaged at Benghazi Naval Base; eastern Libyan officials alleged Ukrainian sea drones launched from western Libya, while no actor claimed responsibility by the cutoff.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The military loss is visible, but the larger issue is the use of rival Libyan-controlled spaces for Russian logistics and alleged Ukrainian reach.
- **Strategic mechanism:** If Ukraine can strike Russian naval assets using western Libya, Russia may harden or expand its footprint in the east, while eastern authorities may pressure Tripoli or foreign sponsors. If the launch-origin claim is wrong, acting on it could still destabilize Libya.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Explosion, fire, a capsized/damaged vessel, Russian access to the base for refueling and supplies, and official Libyan investigations.
- **What remains inference:** Attacker, launch point, drone count, vessel operational status, cargo, and the degree of government control over any launch network.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP report and imagery](https://apnews.com/article/libya-russia-vessel-drone-benghazi-59f6f304f3053c69c8cc890138a94d1b); [UNSMIL concern over attacks](https://unsmil.unmissions.org/en/press-releases/unsmil-expresses-serious-concern-over-the-attacks-carried-out-at-benghazi).
- **Questions for auditor:** Independent imagery or forensic evidence; Russian confirmation; whether the separate Maltese-flagged vessel incident is established.

## Candidate: COP31 failure exposes a consensus-governance problem, not total institutional paralysis

- **Underlying development:** Parties failed to agree the presidency-led cover decisions, while other decisions and action-agenda packages proceeded.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** “COP collapse” is punchy but can erase the distinction between failure of the overarching political package and adoption of narrower work.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Without a cover decision, countries lose a shared implementation signal on fossil fuels, finance and the 1.5-degree overshoot. Narrower decisions can continue, but the political coordination mechanism is weaker.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The UN climate chief said both presidency-led decisions failed; EU members and others objected to drafts without fossil-fuel language; the UNFCCC reported separate progress on just transition and the action agenda.
- **What remains inference:** Whether the failure will reduce national policy ambition, accelerate parallel clubs, or drive procedural reform before COP32.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [UNFCCC closing remarks](https://unfccc.int/news/un-climate-change-executive-secretary-simon-stiell-remarks-at-the-closing-plenary-of-cop31); [AP negotiation report](https://apnews.com/article/cop31-climate-talks-united-nations-global-warming-baed7f1491d16a00ee9b60a35596e1f6); [official draft report](https://docs.unfccc.int/documents/10000401/files/en/CP31_draft_report.pdf).
- **Questions for auditor:** Exact adopted decision list; what became of the 35-percent electrification target; which items move to COP32.

## Candidate: A 258-seat House majority changes congressional capacity before it changes policy

- **Underlying development:** Democrats reached at least 258 House seats after a recount-based race call, with a 56-seat Senate majority also due to take office in January.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Coverage emphasizes electoral scale; the operational effect is a large governing margin for investigations, appropriations, sanctions and foreign-policy legislation.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Leaders can absorb dozens of defections and still pass House measures, strengthening oversight and bargaining leverage against the executive. Enactment still depends on Senate procedure and presidential signature or veto override.
- **What evidence is observable now:** AP's calls; four House races remained outstanding; incoming leaders announced broad oversight plans.
- **What remains inference:** Specific Iran-war votes, funding limits, sanctions policy and whether the caucus will act as a coherent bloc.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP on the final-scale majority](https://apnews.com/article/democrats-house-majority-election-2026-congress-721096a4d9ba2cd68683692361798cdb); [AP on the decisive recount](https://apnews.com/article/23732afcd78a853e98acd9b72d0cbd14).
- **Questions for auditor:** State certification; incoming committee chairs; publicly stated foreign-policy agenda.

## Candidate: Russia attribution moves from “hybrid” sabotage to alleged proxy assassination

- **Underlying development:** Denmark's PET assessed that Russian intelligence planned an imminent killing at a public concert using people tied to a banned gang. Poland then identified the target as the same Ukrainian-American it says Russia targeted in August.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The individual case matters less strategically than the alleged method: intelligence services outsourcing violence to local criminal networks across EU states.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Criminal proxies lower attribution and deployment costs but create policing and intelligence seams. A repeated target across two NATO states could drive Schengen, sanctions or counterintelligence measures.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Weapons and knives were found near the Danish venue; two men were charged; Denmark expelled Russia's defense attaché; Poland confirmed a prior arrest involving the same target.
- **What remains inference:** Public proof of Russian tasking, whether the two plots share command, and the breadth of the proxy network. Criminal liability remains for courts.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [PET assessment and legal-status caveats](https://pet.dk/pet/nyhedsliste/rusland-udnytter-danske-kriminelle-miljoeer-til-likvideringsforsoeg-paa-dansk-grund/2026/11/20); [AP on Poland's disclosure](https://apnews.com/article/poland-denmark-russia-ukraine-rapper-plots-291d2e6c33441c06963c4807c32eb7cd); [European Commission response](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/statement_26_2556/STATEMENT_26_2556_EN.pdf).

## Candidate: EU-Indonesia pact is a signed diversification instrument, not an active tariff regime

- **Underlying development:** The EU and Indonesia signed CEPA and an investment-protection agreement on November 21.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Strategic-raw-material framing can outrun the legal reality that ratification and provisional application remain ahead.
- **Strategic mechanism:** If implemented, the agreements can diversify EU access to Southeast Asian markets and raw-material supply chains while giving Indonesia broader market access. Signature alone changes expectations, not current tariffs.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Signatures, published EU next steps, and counterpart official statements.
- **What remains inference:** Ratification timing, realized trade flows, environmental enforcement and actual nickel-supply effects.
- **Initial priority:** medium; defer because the signing likely preceded the nominal window.
