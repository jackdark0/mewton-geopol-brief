[Reuters](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/iranian-negotiators-doha-continue-talks-122402000.html) [Press TV report of Araghchi's remarks](https://www.presstv.co.uk/Detail/2026/11/01/779702/araghchi-says-iran-holding-indirect-talks-through-qatar-rules-out-direct-meeting-with-us)

[Press TV](https://www.presstv.co.uk/Detail/2026/11/01/779702/araghchi-says-iran-holding-indirect-talks-through-qatar-rules-out-direct-meeting-with-us)

### Material claims not established

- Washington accepted the two-day proposal, Tehran and Washington share a common text, or a deal is imminent.
- Commercial traffic can return at ordinary volume two days after an agreement; clearance, insurance, port, security, and route constraints were not demonstrated.
- Continued talks imply a decision against renewed US or Israeli strikes.

### Evidence for / evidence against

- **For substantive movement:** three days of mediated consultations, a named implementation sequence, and regular Qatari contact with Washington.
- **Against a breakthrough reading:** Araghchi said there was no outcome; no joint text, US acceptance, Qatari endorsement of all terms, or implementation notice was published by the cutoff; a Gulf official said positions remained far apart.

### Source provenance

- AP's Manama reporting is independent for Gulf officials' caution, not for the secret terms.

### Open questions

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Lead with active mediation and the absence of an accepted bargain.

### Material claims not established

- A prior refinery pause was a formal bilateral commitment lasting through the November 3 US election.

- **Against an operating deal:** no US acceptance, transfer order, quantity, delivery date, monitoring rule, or Russian assent was published; conflicting reports remain about the earlier pause.

### Source provenance

- The prior-pause dispute rests on separate anonymous-source chains in the Wall Street Journal and Kyiv Independent.

### Open questions
