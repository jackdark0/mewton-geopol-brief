# Adversarial review - 2026-11-02

**Scope:** Test the verified ledger and analysis against the strongest plausible alternative readings before publication.
**Cutoff discipline:** No event or update after 2026-11-02 02:00:00 America/New_York is admissible.

## Global failure modes

1. **Process inflation:** The draft repeatedly risks treating talks, proposals, investigations, target levels, and provisional results as completed outcomes.
2. **Shared-source inflation:** Multiple outlets often trace to the same speech, company statement, public video, or UN announcement. They do not become independent confirmation through repetition.
3. **Attribution drift:** Regional proximity can tempt the editor to connect *Al Mayeda*, UKMTO notices, and Iran's tanker claims. The record does not support that merge.
4. **Denominator slippage:** Precinct protocols are not turnout; quota levels are not barrels; an app failure is not every warning channel; a seat margin is not unlawful votes.
5. **Mutable-page leakage:** The Bulgarian CEC and Ukraine alert pages changed after cutoff. Only time-locked, pre-cutoff details may appear.

## Story challenges

### Doha

**Strongest contrary case:** Iran's public timetable may be negotiating theater designed to make Washington look like the obstacle. Three days of consultations could reflect deadlock, not convergence. Qatar's regular contact with Washington does not prove that Washington accepted the terms or even regarded them as workable.

**Required correction:** Headline and opening must say there is no accepted package. Describe the two-day sequence as Iran's conditional proposal after an agreement, not a promise to reopen within two days of the speech. Do not say talks are direct or that a breakthrough is near.

**Residual uncertainty:** High on substance; moderate on whether the channel is narrowing differences.

### *Al Mayeda*

**Strongest contrary case:** The event is two days older than its disclosure and may have involved debris, a minor collision, or an unrelated object rather than a deliberate attack. Qatar's use of attack language is an official characterization, not a forensic conclusion.

**Required correction:** Attribute “hit by unidentified objects” to the company and “attack” to Qatar. State that the crew was safe, no environmental effect was reported, and the vessel remained operational. Do not infer LNG supply loss or name an attacker.

**Residual uncertainty:** High on weapon and responsibility; lower on the vessel's reported condition.

### Ukraine's Patriot offer

**Strongest contrary case:** The proposal may be public diplomacy aimed at Washington, not a bargain designed for implementation. One month of refinery restraint and one month of interceptor supply are not directly commensurate, and no monitoring, quantity, or enforcement mechanism exists.

**Required correction:** Use “offered” and “proposed,” never “agreed.” Explain that an approved future sale does not guarantee near-term inventory. Keep the prior-pause chronology in Contested Claims rather than resolving it by inference.

**Residual uncertainty:** High on availability, acceptance, and compliance; moderate on the exact earlier understanding.

### MINURSO

**Strongest contrary case:** “Mission ends” can erase a potentially lengthy liquidation period and surviving UN functions. Conversely, emphasizing diplomacy can understate the practical loss of field monitoring.

**Required correction:** Say peacekeeping operations ended and liquidation began. Preserve both consequences: field capacity is being removed, while the UN political file and self-determination language continue. Do not convert a failed renewal into recognition of Moroccan sovereignty.

**Residual uncertainty:** Moderate on the operational gap and conflict risk; low on the Council votes and start of liquidation.

### OPEC+

**Strongest contrary case:** The decision may be largely symbolic because several members cannot reach their targets. Reuters' assessment of constraints is not a complete, independently audited production ledger either.

**Required correction:** Separate required-production levels from measured output, exports, and delivered supply. Avoid quantities not rendered in the inspected primary release. Do not claim the hold targeted US politics.

**Residual uncertainty:** Moderate on physical supply and capacity; low on the target decision.

### Israel

**Strongest contrary case:** Officials defending the election are interested in institutional stability and may not possess or disclose the full classified record. Their aggregate-validity statement cannot exonerate individuals or prove that foreign financing did not occur.

**Required correction:** Present two facts together: the result is institutionally defended and expected to stand; the financing inquiry continues. A 4,612-vote allocation margin is not evidence of 4,612 illegal votes. Avoid “fraud debunked” and “stolen election.”

**Residual uncertainty:** High on classified financing facts; low on the current institutional position and seat count.

### Bulgaria

**Strongest contrary case:** A decisive provisional margin may encourage the draft to announce a final certified result or a geopolitical realignment. The presidency's powers and both candidates' positions complicate a simple pro- or anti-West story.

**Required correction:** Label the 99.95% protocol count provisional and time-stamp it at 05:30 UTC. Do not use the later 100% update. Explain that the office is largely ceremonial and wait for concrete policy acts.

**Residual uncertainty:** Low on the winner implied by the cutoff-safe margin; moderate on policy consequence.
