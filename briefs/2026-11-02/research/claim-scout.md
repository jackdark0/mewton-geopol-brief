## C-01 — Claim: Foreign interference and fraud invalidated Israel's national election result

- **Exact atomic proposition / type:** Foreign interference or vote-buying changed the October 27 election sufficiently to invalidate the overall result. Empirical, causal, quantitative, and legal/process claims must remain separate. This is an analyst paraphrase *(approximate)* of the stolen-election narrative, not a verbatim quotation.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Benjamin Netanyahu and Likud. The strongest newly inspected version is Likud's November 1 response to the election chief: it contests declaring the result unaffected before the investigation concludes. Attribution confidence: high for the reported party statement; original party release/video not recovered.
- **Why it matters:** The claim could delegitimize coalition formation. Whether suspected financing occurred, whether it was unlawful, whether ballots were invalid, and whether seats changed are different propositions.
- **Evidence offered for it:** An actual Shin Bet inquiry into serious foreign-funding suspicions exists, according to the public statement reported by ToI. Likud invokes a narrow seat margin: “a mere 4,612 votes stood between a Knesset seat shifting from Ra’am to the right-wing bloc”. That quotation is from ToI's reproduction of the party statement, not independently recomputed arithmetic. A margin does not identify affected ballots.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** In a joint November 1 video with the Shin Bet chief and attorney general, election chairman Noam Sohlberg said the election properly reflected voters' will and that even substantiated suspicions would not undermine the overall result. He said many reports about the classified investigation were incorrect. No public transfer ledger, recipient-level findings, invalid-ballot total, or counterfactual seat calculation was inspected. This is a materially stronger official rebuttal than a generic assertion that nothing is known; the underlying classified basis still cannot be independently checked.
- **Independent corroboration:** Partial for the existence and wording of the official/party positions; no independent proof of the result-changing allegation. Reports of the same joint video are one underlying evidentiary lineage.
- **Current assessment:** **Weak / unsupported at the invalidated-result scope.** The narrower financing suspicion remains unresolved. Do not call every interference allegation false, and do not turn the public assurance into a final judicial judgment.
- **Confidence:** **Medium.** The public institutional position directly opposes the broad narrative, but the primary video, investigative record, and claimed 4,612-vote arithmetic require audit.
- **Sources:**
  - [ToI, election chief's joint statement](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/alongside-shin-bet-chief-election-committee-chairman-vows-results-will-not-change/) — inspected body, November 1, 19:47 displayed Israel time; approximately 17:47 UTC.   - [ToI, Likud response](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/likud-says-until-probe-completed-a-dark-cloud-hangs-over-israels-election-results/) — inspected body, November 1, 20:18 displayed Israel time; approximately 18:18 UTC. Establishes the party's counterargument and claimed margin. **Lineage:** secondary reproduction of an interested party statement; not corroboration of fraud or arithmetic.
- **Strongest fair-minded countercase / uncertainty:** A classified financing operation could exist despite valid overall results, and officials may know more than the public. That possibility preserves the narrower investigation; it does not supply the missing causal chain from money to invalid ballots to seats.
