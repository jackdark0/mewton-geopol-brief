# News Scout — 2026-11-02 — discovery only

**Frozen research cutoff:** 2026-11-02 02:00:00 America/New_York (EST, UTC-05:00) = 2026-11-02 07:00:00 UTC.

**Research window:** approximately 2026-10-31 21:00:00 America/New_York (EDT, UTC-04:00) = 2026-11-01 01:00:00 UTC, through the cutoff above: 30 elapsed hours crossing the daylight-saving transition.

**Leakage control:** Retrieval occurred for this edition after the frozen cutoff. Only dated events and attributable statements available by the cutoff are eligible. A November 2 dateline alone does not establish pre-cutoff publication. Mutable feeds, recommendations, current headlines, later counts and search snippets are not evidence. The dated article body is separated from its current surrounding page. Items with unresolved timing remain HOLD or excluded. The prior brief supplied continuity leads only; none of its wording is evidence for this edition.

**Scope and method:** Stage 1 discovery, not source audit or a publication verdict. Eighteen candidates were considered before pruning. FACT means an inspected record establishes the narrow event or statement; CLAIM means an interested source's underlying factual assertion; ASSESSMENT means the scout's explicitly provisional significance judgment. Candidate numbers do not measure world-event prevalence. No casualty, fraud, electorate or readiness percentage has been inferred from a convenience sample. Both Pangburn sibling standards were read separately: *Pangburn AI — Empirical Fact-Checking System*, v1.0 (2026-08-02), and *Pangburn Analysis — Forensic Fact-Checking Replication Standard*, v1.0 (2026-08-11). Their numbering is not interchangeable.

**Retrieval limits:** Direct AP pages repeatedly failed. Readable ABC and Washington Post copies retain AP bylines and count as the AP lineage, never separate confirmation. Direct UN pages often returned a robot challenge. Some accessible news pages expose only a calendar date, not a publication timezone. Official statements establish institutional positions; they do not independently establish responsibility for attacks. Quotes are deliberately avoided where original media or complete primary text was not recovered.
