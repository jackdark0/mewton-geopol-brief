### Source provenance

- AP directly reported the ministers' exchange. NATO and Ukraine are primary records for public positions, not independent proof of the positions' causal claims.

### Open questions

### Recommendation

- include major; carry the effectiveness dispute into contested claims.

**Priority:** high

- **[CONFIRMED]** OPCW-designated laboratories confirmed CS, related compounds and degradation products in a recovered RG-Vo grenade and soil sample from the September 20, 2024 incident. The Chemical Weapons Convention prohibits riot-control agents as a method of warfare. [OPCW topic page](https://www.opcw.org/media-centre/featured-topics/ukraine) [Secretariat note](https://www.opcw.org/sites/default/files/documents/2026/11/c31s01%28e%29.pdf)
- **[SUPPORTED]** Ukraine's national investigation attributes the grenade's drone delivery to Russian armed forces and says Russian industrial, logistics and training records indicate an organized supply and tactical-use system. It describes witness, physical, meteorological, intelligence, covert-investigation and open-source evidence. [Ukrainian report circulated by OPCW](https://www.opcw.org/sites/default/files/documents/2026/11/c31nat01%28e%29.pdf)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The OPCW Technical Secretariat provided technical expertise and circulated Ukraine's report to states parties; it did not issue the attribution conclusion as its own. [Secretariat note](https://www.opcw.org/sites/default/files/documents/2026/11/c31s01%28e%29.pdf)

### Material claims not established

- "The OPCW concluded Russia used tear gas" is false as an institutional-attribution statement.
- A command-wide Russian policy or the full Ukrainian claim of 14,000 similar incidents. The current public packet establishes chemistry in one investigated incident and describes, but does not fully expose, broader records.
- Russia's response to the current report was not located by cutoff; past Russian denials do not rebut this specific evidence.

### Source provenance

- OPCW laboratories independently establish chemistry. The delivery, actor and system attribution remains Ukraine's national finding, although circulated in an official OPCW document series.

### Open questions

- Public authenticity checks for military documents, responsible unit/command, and an incident-specific Russian response.

### Recommendation

- include major with a headline that preserves the provenance distinction; contested claim should correct OPCW over-attribution.
