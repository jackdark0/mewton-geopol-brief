## Claim: Ukrainian deep strikes are counterproductive and harden Russian resolve

- **Origin / principal promoters:** U.S. Under Secretary of Defense Elbridge Colby at the November 17 Ukraine Defense Contact Group.
- **Why it matters:** The claim could redirect allied weapons, intelligence and money away from long-range operations.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Colby distinguished failure to end the war or blunt front-line advances and cited attacks on Wildberries as possibly hardening public and Kremlin resolve.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Germany and Britain argue strikes degrade Russian military capacity and save lives; no inspected causal study or disclosed intelligence established the public-morale claim. Front-line effect, economic effect and bargaining effect are separate outcomes.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for the causal claim; yes for the public dispute.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** high that the claim was made; low on its causal truth.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/colby-ukraine-deep-long-nato-strikes-starshield-e570c549277a14f97ad24319f2f1bf7e); [Ukraine](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/shorazu-rosiya-obiraye-novu-eskalaciyu-tozh-ukrayinski-raket-107369).
- **Questions for auditor:** What observable measures would test military degradation, morale and negotiation effects separately?

## Claim: The OPCW concluded that Russian forces used tear gas

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Headline compression of Ukraine's national investigation and OPCW technical support.
- **Why it matters:** Misstating the institutional author would overstate an international body's attribution finding.
- **Evidence offered for it:** OPCW-designated laboratories confirmed CS-related compounds; Ukraine described physical evidence, witnesses, military documents and training materials tying an RG-Vo grenade to Russian forces.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The OPCW Secretariat note says it provided technical expertise and circulated Ukraine's report. It does not adopt the national attribution conclusion. Key military documents are described but not fully reproduced in the inspected public text.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for chemistry; no independent public attribution located.
- **Current assessment:** false if phrased as OPCW attribution; plausible-to-supported as a Ukrainian national attribution, with medium confidence.
- **Confidence:** high on the provenance distinction.
- **Sources:** [OPCW](https://www.opcw.org/sites/default/files/documents/2026/11/c31s01%28e%29.pdf); [Ukraine report](https://www.opcw.org/sites/default/files/documents/2026/11/c31nat01%28e%29.pdf); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ea32f272904869d3423de0246140584b).
- **Questions for auditor:** Which public exhibits identify the munition, delivery platform and unit?

## Claim: Iran's November 17 Bahrain attack caused no damage or casualties

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Bahrain and the U.S. military, reported by AP.
- **Why it matters:** Damage affects the escalation balance and credibility of both parties' narratives.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Bahrain said air defenses intercepted the attack; the U.S. said no equipment damage or personnel injuries occurred.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No independent battle-damage imagery or full intercept count was located. Iran's pattern of success claims in related attacks has not been independently substantiated.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial; Bahrain and the U.S. are aligned parties, not independent neutral confirmation.
- **Current assessment:** plausible.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-trump-israel-bahrain-attacks-582e0b5d51be06f0ed630fac24f39b72).
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek imagery, local reports and direct statements with precise target/munition counts.

## Claim: Kataib Hezbollah smuggled oil from Jurf al-Sakhar using 25 tankers

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Iraqi parliamentary and government investigation figures reported through state media and AP.
- **Why it matters:** The allegation supplies the legal and political rationale for entering a militia stronghold.
- **Evidence offered for it:** An investigating parliamentarian said thermal imaging showed 25 tankers carrying 36,000 liters each; the prime minister dismissed nine officials and ordered referrals.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Kataib Hezbollah acknowledges two refining facilities but says fuel serves its vehicles and generators. The underlying thermal images, documents, measurement date and custody chain were not inspected.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** low-to-medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iraq-iran-kataib-hezbollah-jurf-alsakhar-fed4063af6dbf61fd4fb6f5f5c3d278d).
- **Questions for auditor:** Is 900,000 liters a one-time observed stock/flow, and what evidence ties it to illicit sale?

## Claim: The Gaza ceasefire will collapse without immediate implementation

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Board of Peace November report.
- **Why it matters:** The claim frames the urgency of Security Council and party action.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Continued strikes, Hamas military activity, blocked aid inputs, no deployment of the technocratic administration or stabilization force, and earlier U.N. reporting of the same implementation gaps.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The ceasefire has survived previous noncompliance; "collapse" is a forecast without a specified time horizon or measurable threshold.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for fragility and implementation gaps; no for inevitability or timing.
- **Current assessment:** plausible risk, not established forecast.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/board-of-peace-us-israel-gaza-hamas-189d58d05fa984cc9573f98ec7d638be); [U.N. September report](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/unsco-report-to-the-ad-hoc-liaison-committee-september-2026/).
- **Questions for auditor:** What concrete trigger would constitute collapse rather than continued degraded compliance?

## Claim: The Houthis have solidified control of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait

- **Origin / principal promoters:** AP's Iran-war analysis used this unqualified formulation.
- **Why it matters:** Literal control of a strategic strait implies a much larger maritime-power shift than control of nearby land or capacity to threaten ships.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Recent Houthi territorial gains near the strait and continuing maritime-strike capability.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No inspected neutral maritime record established sovereign control of both shores, routine interdiction, or denial of passage. Shipping threat is not the same as physical control.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** weak as a literal control claim; supported only as a heightened threat to transit.
- **Confidence:** medium-high on the scope problem.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-trump-israel-bahrain-attacks-582e0b5d51be06f0ed630fac24f39b72); prior continuity check in the November 16 desk artifacts.
- **Questions for auditor:** Require a current territorial map and transit/interdiction data before repeating "control of the strait."
