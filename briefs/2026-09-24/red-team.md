## High-risk framing errors

### 1. “New Tigray war” can outrun the known battlefield picture

- **Problem:** Major fighting and airport seizures are verified, but a complete territorial map, casualty picture and agreed start point are absent.
- **Required correction:** Lead with the three named airports and multi-region fighting. Attribute claims about initiation. Do not state current Eritrean involvement as fact.
- **Residual risk:** Medium-high because the situation is active and reports may change after cutoff.

### 2. Continued U.S.-Iran talks can be mistaken for progress

- **Problem:** The only detailed Iranian terms come from one unnamed official; “still talking” does not establish convergence.
- **Required correction:** Say the channel survived, immediately add that the sides remain far apart, and separate named U.S. positions from the unnamed Iranian account.
- **Residual risk:** Medium.

### 3. Vessel-attack attribution is under-supported

- **Problem:** The strike and casualties are reported, but Iranian responsibility rests on Rubio's allegation in the inspected material.

- **Residual risk:** Low after attribution.

### 4. Gaza's blueprint can be misread as reconstruction underway

- **Problem:** A precise project count and price create false concreteness when funding, access and political sequencing are unresolved.
- **Required correction:** Put “not fully funded” and “no fixed implementation calendar” in the first paragraph. Use “blueprint,” “proposal” and “planned,” not “program underway.”
- **Residual risk:** Medium because official promotion may blur plan and execution.

### 5. Ukraine's offer can be turned into a bilateral ceasefire

- **Problem:** Kyiv accepted concepts; Moscow publicly rejected a pause. There is no operative text.
- **Required correction:** Make Russia's negative public response part of the headline and the first paragraph. State that no ceasefire exists.
- **Residual risk:** Low after correction.

### 6. Morocco's plurality can be inflated into control

- **Problem:** 97 seats sounds decisive without the 395-seat denominator.
- **Required correction:** Keep “97 of 395” in the first sentence, call the count provisional, and state that coalition bargaining is required.
- **Residual risk:** Low.

### 7. The Australia-Ukraine agreement can be mislabeled a guarantee

- **Problem:** Broad cooperation language can sound binding.

- **Residual risk:** Low.

### 8. The ICC verdict can be put in the wrong legal category

- **Problem:** War-crime charges were part of the case, but the convictions were for crimes against humanity.

- **Residual risk:** Low.
