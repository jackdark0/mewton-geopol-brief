## Story: ICJ finds Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **CONFIRMED:** On November 19, the International Court of Justice issued its final judgment in *The Gambia v. Myanmar*. By 9 votes to 6, it found that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in northern Rakhine State. The judgment is final and binding. [ICJ judgment](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-jud-01-00-en.pdf) [ICJ summary](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-sum-01-00-en.pdf)
- **CONFIRMED:** The Court also found failures to prevent and punish genocide, to enact required legislation, and to preserve evidence. It ordered legislation, investigation and prosecution where warranted, guarantees of non-repetition, restitution, and compensation; the amount and modalities of compensation remain to be settled. [ICJ summary](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-sum-01-00-en.pdf)
- **CONFIRMED:** By 14 votes to 1, the Court rejected The Gambia's allegation that Myanmar directly and publicly incited private persons to commit genocide. It also rejected all remaining submissions unanimously. [ICJ judgment](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-jud-01-00-en.pdf)
- **SUPPORTED:** This is the first time the Court has held a state responsible for genocide. The U.N. Secretary-General and AP independently described it that way. [U.N. statement](https://myanmar.un.org/en/326406-statement-secretary-general-international-court-justice-judgment-%E2%80%9C-gambia-v-myanmar%E2%80%9D) [AP](https://apnews.com/article/rohingya-myanmar-genocide-court-icj-30df77b12bf3f911f1100e77ebe3973a)

### Material claims not established

- A judgment does not establish that Myanmar will implement the remedies. The Court has no direct enforcement arm; AP notes the enforcement constraint.
- Myanmar's post-judgment government response was published after the fixed cutoff and is excluded from this edition.

### Source provenance

- The judgment and summary are the controlling primary records. The U.N. statement is an official institutional summary. AP independently reports reaction and enforcement limits but relies on the judgment for the legal disposition.

### Open questions

- What implementation measures, if any, will Myanmar report within the ordered two-year period?
- How will the parties settle compensation amounts and modalities?

### Recommendation

- include major; lead the brief.

[Agreement text](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20252/almdel/GRU/bilag/57/3189188.pdf)
- **CONFIRMED:** The agreement expressly preserves Greenland's right to self-determination and Denmark's sovereignty pending any constitutional change. [Agreement text](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20252/almdel/GRU/bilag/57/3189188.pdf)
