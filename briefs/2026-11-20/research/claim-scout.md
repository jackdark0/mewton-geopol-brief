## Claim: The ICJ made a final finding that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya

- **Origin / principal promoters:** ICJ judgment as described by the Court president, the U.N. Secretary-General, The Gambia and courtroom reporting.
- **Why it matters:** This is materially different from a provisional-measures order, an allegation or an investigative assessment.
- **Evidence offered for it:** AP reports the 9-6 merits disposition and quotes the Court president; the U.N. Secretary-General statement enumerates the Article II acts and genocidal intent finding.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The full judgment/official summary was not retrievable from the Court site during initial discovery; exact votes on each operative paragraph and dissent reasoning need primary-text inspection. Myanmar rejected the case's framing before judgment and may contest the ruling, but a party's rejection does not undo the Court's disposition.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial; multiple institutional and reporting sources confirm the judicial act, though all derive from the same judgment.
- **Current assessment:** supported.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/rohingya-myanmar-genocide-court-icj-30df77b12bf3f911f1100e77ebe3973a); [U.N. Secretary-General](https://myanmar.un.org/en/326406-statement-secretary-general-international-court-justice-judgment-%E2%80%9C-gambia-v-myanmar%E2%80%9D); [ICJ case page](https://www.icj-cij.org/case/178).
- **Questions for auditor:** Verify the exact operative clause and distinguish state responsibility, individual criminal responsibility and remedy.

## Claim: Russia accepted NATO Article 5-like security guarantees for Ukraine

- **Origin / principal promoters:** U.S. envoy Jared Kushner, speaking after Trump-Putin talks; amplified in headlines.
- **Why it matters:** Russian acceptance could unlock a ceasefire/security bargain; a mistaken claim could mislead audiences about the core negotiating gap.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Kushner said he believed the Russians were “comfortable” with the concept and that guarantees would be based on Article 5.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No inspected Russian text or named Russian official accepted the specific mechanism. Putin invited Europeans to Moscow to clarify their position and said the format would be decided afterward. The public coalition statement presents several possible guarantees, not a negotiated agreement.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** high that acceptance is not established; low on Russia's private position.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-kerch-bridge-putin-crimea-7b4a86809dcd466bba97d374045a1a78); [Reuters republication](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/putin-says-russia-invited-europeans-to-moscow-to-clarify-position-on-ukraine-talks-ce785ed2dc8ff220); [coalition statement](https://www.bundesregierung.de/resource/blob/992814/2461956/9c822e93b93aba0d75cba1ca91c20025/2026-11-19-cow-data.pdf?download=1).
- **Questions for auditor:** Was Kushner conveying a direct Russian commitment, a U.S. negotiating assessment or his own interpretation? Did the Kremlin clarify before cutoff?

## Claim: A Houthi missile directly struck Riyadh's PIF tower

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Houthi media and reporting based on visible impact footage; Reuters factbox calls it a strike.
- **Why it matters:** A direct warhead impact would imply a more serious Saudi interception failure than damage from falling debris.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Visible building damage and footage described as showing a projectile hitting the tower; residents heard an explosion after warnings.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The Saudi-led coalition said debris from an intercepted missile fell; no inspected forensic debris or damage assessment distinguishes the mechanisms.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for damage, no for mechanism.
- **Current assessment:** disputed.
- **Confidence:** high on attack-caused damage; low on direct-hit mechanism.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/saudi-yemen-war-iran-november-19-2026-e72472c557057943cab32010666b29bf); [Reuters factbox republication](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Factbox-Houthi%2Battacks%2Bon%2BSaudi%2BArabia/27375061.html); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/saudi-led-coalition-intercepts-houthi-missiles-targeting-riyadh-and-south).
- **Questions for auditor:** Inspect original imagery, timing, trajectory and official debris evidence; avoid using “intercepted” to imply no damage.

## Claim: The Iran war has cost the United States $48.3 billion in total

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Shorthand based on Pentagon budget-official testimony.
- **Why it matters:** Cost framing shapes congressional authorization and public debate; the wrong denominator can understate the burden.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The official supplied $48.3 billion through November 13 for operations plus equipment and munitions replacement.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The number excludes damage to U.S. bases and may exclude other categories, classified costs, partner costs and future obligations. It is date-limited.
- **Independent corroboration:** no independent audit inspected; AP directly reports the hearing.
- **Current assessment:** qualified.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-costs-base-damage-congress-effe3c444d5a5d83a506e47d28add19b).
- **Questions for auditor:** Preserve “estimated,” “through November 13” and the explicit exclusions; obtain hearing material if available.

## Claim: China's November 19 measures cut Japan off from critical minerals

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Broad summaries of a year-long China-Japan dispute.
- **Why it matters:** A comprehensive cutoff would be a major supply-chain shock with defense and semiconductor effects.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Reuters reports prior months with no Chinese shipments of gallium, dysprosium, terbium or yttrium to Japan and continuing targeted dual-use restrictions. A renewed seafood halt was signaled November 19.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The mineral measures predate this window and are targeted by item/end user; the new November 19 step concerned seafood procedures. No inspected source established a new universal minerals cutoff that day.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for the historical customs data; no for a new blanket cutoff.
- **Current assessment:** weak as stated; supported only in a narrower, pre-existing form.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters republication](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/how-japan-pm-s-taiwan-remarks-sparked-a-year-long-rift-with-china-ce785eddd08cf32c).
- **Questions for auditor:** Inspect current Chinese orders and customs denominators; separate zero shipments, formal bans, licensing delays and end-user controls.

## Claim: The Saudi strike wave destroyed a major share of Houthi missile and drone capacity

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Saudi-led coalition target descriptions and pro-coalition commentary.
- **Why it matters:** The claim bears on campaign effectiveness, escalation duration and U.S. support decisions.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The coalition says it struck missile warehouses and drone workshops across Houthi-held territory.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No inspected imagery, inventory denominator, launch-rate trend or independent battle-damage assessment shows how much capacity was destroyed. The next-day Riyadh damage shows residual reach, not necessarily the survival rate of any specific targeted system.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** high that the claimed effect is not established.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/saudi-yemen-war-iran-november-19-2026-e72472c557057943cab32010666b29bf); prior campaign description in [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-news-iran-bahrain-us-november-18-2026-b71b1d25f3cff987b4b125196ecb4c3a).
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek imagery, named sites, repeat-strike evidence, pre/post launch tempo and independent casualty attribution.

## Claim: The UAE is arming Somali forces outside federal control

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and the federal government's expulsion rationale.
- **Why it matters:** If established, the conduct would implicate sovereignty, internal conflict and Gulf competition around ports and security partnerships.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Mogadishu cites UAE ties with Somaliland, Puntland and Jubbaland and earlier cancelled port/security agreements.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The inspected AP report does not provide transfers, unit names, dates or independent documentation; the UAE rejects the federal government's account and regional authorities reject Mogadishu's authority over their agreements.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** medium that the diplomatic rupture is real; low on the underlying arming claim.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/somalia-uae-ambassador-expulsion-sovereignty-somaliland-mohamud-3cdeb27a0a08d365d6a04de46500d842).
- **Questions for auditor:** Locate the Somali cabinet statement, UAE response and any documented aid, equipment or command relationship.
