## Candidate: ICJ finds Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya

- **Event time/date:** Judgment delivered 2026-11-19 at 10:00 The Hague time.
- **Region / actors:** Myanmar, Rohingya, The Gambia, International Court of Justice.
- **What changed:** The ICJ issued a final merits judgment finding, by 9-6, that Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya in northern Rakhine State during the 2017 operations. It ordered safe, voluntary and dignified return, investigation and punishment of perpetrators, and compensation to be set later.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It is the first ICJ judgment holding a state responsible for committing genocide.
  - The judgment is legally binding but enforcement depends heavily on state compliance and the U.N. Security Council.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The U.N. Secretary-General statement independently confirms the Court's disposition; AP reports from the courtroom. Both ultimately describe the same judicial act, but the U.N. statement is an official institutional record rather than independent fact-finding.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/rohingya-myanmar-genocide-court-icj-30df77b12bf3f911f1100e77ebe3973a) supports the vote, findings and remedies; [U.N. Secretary-General](https://myanmar.un.org/en/326406-statement-secretary-general-international-court-justice-judgment-%E2%80%9C-gambia-v-myanmar%E2%80%9D) confirms the judgment's core legal findings; [ICJ case page](https://www.icj-cij.org/case/178) establishes the docket and procedural record.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain the judgment or official summary; separate the acts, intent finding, remedial orders and enforcement mechanism; check Myanmar's response by cutoff.

## Candidate: European coalition tests deployment for a post-ceasefire Ukraine force

- **Event time/date:** Berlin leaders' meeting and public details on 2026-11-19; Koalicja-2026 exercise began 2026-11-09.
- **Region / actors:** Ukraine, France, United Kingdom, Germany, Poland, United States, roughly 37 Coalition of the Willing states.
- **What changed:** Leaders publicly tied an ongoing 2,800-person deployment exercise in Poland to a proposed European-led Multinational Force Ukraine. Their statement described possible air, sea and land reassurance measures, regeneration of Ukraine's armed forces, U.S.-led ceasefire monitoring and a legally binding response commitment after a future Russian attack, subject to national procedures.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - A previously political guarantee concept now has an exercised deployment and command-and-control mechanism.
  - The force would deploy only after a ceasefire; its size, contributors, mandate and U.S. support remain unsettled.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The German-hosted joint statement is the primary political record; AP independently observed and described the exercise but relies on coalition officials for purpose and readiness.
- **Sources:** [German-hosted joint statement](https://www.bundesregierung.de/resource/blob/992814/2461956/9c822e93b93aba0d75cba1ca91c20025/2026-11-19-cow-data.pdf?download=1) supports the guarantee architecture; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/poland-ukraine-military-exercise-peace-france-britain-a28d60d1b37093199dd017472028d7ac) supports exercise scale and activities; [Ukraine presidency](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/spilna-zayava-lideriv-nimechchini-franciyi-velikoyi-britaniy-107409) republishes the leaders' language.
- **Questions for auditor:** Which commitments are adopted rather than illustrative? Which states would provide troops? Is Russia's position documented beyond U.S. envoy characterization?

## Candidate: Denmark completes parliamentary approval of U.S.-Greenland defense deal

- **Event time/date:** Folketing vote on 2026-11-19.
- **Region / actors:** Denmark, Greenland, United States, NATO.
- **What changed:** Denmark's parliament approved the September agreement after Greenland's parliament approved it on November 3. The updated framework permits modernization at Pituffik, defense areas at Narsarsuaq and Mestersvig, possible additional mutually agreed areas, expanded military access and support for Golden Dome while affirming sovereignty and Greenlandic self-determination.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It creates the legal framework for a larger U.S. Arctic military footprint.
  - Only NATO members may establish enduring military installations, directly structuring Russian and Chinese access competition.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** Danish parliament and foreign ministry are direct primary records; AP independently reports the vote and political context.
- **Sources:** [Folketing](https://www.ft.dk/da/aktuelt/nyheder/2026/11/folketinget-godkender-aftale-om-forsvaret-af-groenland) supports approval and entry-into-force sequence; [agreement text](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20252/almdel/GRU/bilag/57/3189188.pdf) supports authorities and limits; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/denmark-greenland-us-security-arctic-north-atlantic-a392abbba5c2f1bccbbb372e3d38769d) supports the 109-0 vote and context.
- **Questions for auditor:** Has diplomatic notification occurred by cutoff? Which projects are authorized immediately versus requiring later mutual agreement?

## Candidate: Houthi attack damages Riyadh's Public Investment Fund tower

- **Region / actors:** Houthis, Saudi Arabia, Saudi-led coalition.

- **Source chain:** AP and Reuters-based imagery/witness reporting offer distinct reporting routes, but official attribution and Houthi claims are interested sources.
