## Candidate: Bahrain intercepts Iranian aerial attacks

- **Event time/date:** 2026-11-15, beginning before 00:00 ET; Reuters published at 23:25 ET on November 14.
- **Region / actors:** Bahrain, Iran, United States.
- **What changed:** Bahrain issued two public threat warnings and said its air defenses intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks before they reached their targets. Iran's Revolutionary Guard said six drones struck U.S. air-defense radar at Al-Rifaa helicopter base; neither Bahrain nor the United States confirmed that claimed impact.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It was Bahrain's first announced interception of Iranian attacks since September.
  - The attack tested the still-undefined U.S. condition that further Iranian or Houthi “provocations” could reopen U.S. strikes.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high that Bahrain activated warnings and announced interceptions; low on Iranian target-impact claims.
- **Source chain:** Bahrain's and Iran's accounts are opposing interested-source chains. Reuters independently established the public alerts but did not independently verify impact.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Bahrain%2Bissues%2Bfirst%2Bthreat%2Balerts%2Bsince%2BSeptember%2Bas%2BIran%2Bsays%2Bit%2Battacked%2BUS%2Bbase/27346239.html) reports the alerts, Bahrain's interception account, Iran's six-drone claim, and absence of U.S./Bahraini impact confirmation.
- **Questions for auditor:** What air vehicles were intercepted? Did debris or independent sensors confirm origin, target, or damage? Did Washington classify the attack as a “provocation” under Trump's reported threshold?

## Candidate: Yemeni government forces retreat from Bab el-Mandeb approaches

- **Event time/date:** announced 2026-11-15; Reuters updated at 00:48 ET on November 16.
- **Region / actors:** Yemen, Houthis, Saudi-backed internationally recognized government forces.
- **What changed:** The main southern government force announced a redeployment from Dhubab Airport and the Bab el-Mandeb area toward Aden. Reuters reported that Houthis were left in control of the area and that the move apparently ended a six-week counteroffensive, while government-linked forces said they were still contesting nearby coastal ground.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It reverses recent Saudi-backed gains near one of the world's central maritime chokepoints.
  - The stated need to defend Aden indicates simultaneous Houthi pressure on the coastal and highland fronts.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on the announced redeployment; medium on the exact territorial-control map.
- **Source chain:** Reuters combines the force's public statement with named-by-role Yemeni military sources. UKMTO/JMIC is a separate maritime-monitoring stream and cautions that stable control had not been independently confirmed before the retreat.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/yemen-government-forces-withdraw-from-area-near-bab-elmandeb-strait-houthis-in-control-5027740) reports the withdrawal, continuing nearby fighting, and Houthi positions; [JMIC Update 114](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/update-114_jmic_advisory_15-nov.pdf?rev=be65cfbd755a41c4816feba6cef4401c) reports roughly 22 daily Bab el-Mandeb transits on November 12-14 versus roughly 25 on November 5-11 and no Houthi shipping claim in the preceding 72 hours.
- **Questions for auditor:** Which positions changed hands, and which remain contested? Does the retreat change the ability to threaten shipping, or chiefly change land-force leverage? Is Aden under imminent operational threat or only under increased pressure?

## Candidate: Estonia announces Russia-border closure after GRU-attributed arson attempt

- **Event time/date:** attempted attack at 03:06 local time on 2026-11-14; attribution and closure announced 2026-11-15.
- **Region / actors:** Estonia, Latvia, Russia, Threod Systems.
- **What changed:** Estonia's Internal Security Service attributed an attempted explosion and arson at drone maker Threod Systems to Russia's GRU. Eight Latvian residents were detained, the company suffered no significant damage, and Estonia announced that its road border crossings with Russia would close from December 1.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - A NATO member is converting a sabotage attribution into a material border measure.
  - The target is part of the defense-industrial base relevant to Ukraine and European drone capability.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on the attempted arson, detentions, lack of major damage, and closure announcement; medium on GRU command because the public evidentiary file is incomplete.
- **Source chain:** Estonian government, foreign ministry, and security service form one official investigative chain. ERR and international reporting add no independent access to the intelligence basis.
- **Sources:** [Estonian Foreign Ministry](https://www.vm.ee/en/news/foreign-minister-tsahkna-estonias-rapid-and-decisive-action-makes-it-increasingly-difficult) states the attribution, detentions, investigation, and damage assessment; [Estonian Foreign Ministry border announcement](https://vm.ee/uudised/valisminister-tsahkna-sulgeme-eesti-venemaa-maanteepiiripunktid) states the planned December 1 road-crossing closure; [ERR](https://news.err.ee/1610196794/estonia-to-prepare-to-close-border-with-russia-after-sabotage-attacks) reports the three affected crossings and official rationale.
- **Questions for auditor:** Has the cabinet issued the operative legal order, and does it cover rail or only road/pedestrian traffic? What public evidence connects the detained suspects to GRU tasking? What humanitarian or trade exceptions remain?

## Candidate: ASEAN retains Myanmar leadership ban but opens a phased route back

- **Event time/date:** adopted 2026-11-15.
- **Region / actors:** ASEAN, Myanmar.
- **What changed:** ASEAN leaders kept Myanmar to non-political representation at leaders' and foreign-ministers' meetings until “meaningful progress” on the Five-Point Consensus. They simultaneously removed restrictions from other sectoral bodies and said full political representation could return gradually after sustained and concrete progress.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - The bloc rejected immediate normalization after Myanmar's military-backed electoral process.
  - The unrestricted sectoral channel and phased pathway create a clearer engagement ladder than a blanket ban.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The adopted ASEAN decision is the controlling primary record. AP and CNA interpret the same document and summit statements rather than independently proving implementation.
- **Sources:** [ASEAN adopted decision](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/FINAL-ASEAN-Leaders-Review-and-Decision-on-the-Implementation-of-the-Five-Point-Consensus-15-November-2026.pdf) sets the representation rules and phased pathway; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/660b4a8df06082f79b673912b2f3e655) reports summit context and continued violence; [CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/asean-myanmar-engagement-peace-plan-6552716) reports internal pressure for engagement.
- **Questions for auditor:** What concrete measures will count as progress? Which sectoral meetings now admit political-level representatives? Does the staged route change leverage over Myanmar or merely regularize existing engagement?

## Candidate: José Maria Neves wins Cape Verde reelection in provisional count

- **Event time/date:** polls closed 2026-11-15; Reuters published at 00:58 ET on November 16.
- **Region / actors:** Cape Verde.
- **What changed:** Provisional results from 97% of polling stations gave President José Maria Neves 63.8%, enough to avoid a runoff; Joana Rosa received 31.6% and conceded. Turnout was under one-third, and final proclamation remained pending.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It preserves unified PAICV-backed control of the presidency and government after the party's May parliamentary victory.
  - The very low turnout is a domestic legitimacy signal, although the president has limited executive authority in Cape Verde's parliamentary system.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on the provisional outcome; final certification pending.
- **Source chain:** Reuters observed the election and cites the state-television provisional count. The election commission is the source of the underlying tally.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Cape%2BVerde%2BPresident%2BNeves%2Bwins%2Bsecond%2Bterm,%2Bchallenger%2BRosa%2Bconcedes%2Bdefeat/27346480.html) gives the in-window provisional count and concession; [Cape Verde CNE](https://cne.cv/) is the election authority and final-proclamation source.
- **Questions for auditor:** What was the last result timestamp before 02:00 ET? Did remaining stations have any mathematical path to change the winner? When is the final proclamation?
