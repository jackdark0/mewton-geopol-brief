## Claim: Iranian drones struck and damaged a U.S. air-defense radar at Al-Rifaa base

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, reported by Fars and state-run Press TV.
- **Why it matters:** Confirmed damage to a U.S. military installation could trigger the reported U.S. “new provocation” condition and reopen major strikes.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The IRGC said it launched six drones at the radar in retaliation for U.S. actions.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Bahrain said aerial attacks were intercepted before reaching targets. Neither Bahrain nor the United States confirmed the claimed target or damage. No inspected imagery, debris record, casualty report, or base-status notice establishes impact.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for target impact; partial for an attack, warnings, and interceptions.
- **Current assessment:** unclear on attempted target; unsupported on successful strike or damage.
- **Confidence:** high that impact is not established; medium on the exact attempted target.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Bahrain%2Bissues%2Bfirst%2Bthreat%2Balerts%2Bsince%2BSeptember%2Bas%2BIran%2Bsays%2Bit%2Battacked%2BUS%2Bbase/27346239.html) records both official accounts and the absence of confirmation.
- **Questions for auditor:** Check CENTCOM, Bahrain Defence Force, satellite imagery, flight notices, and post-attack radar operations. Rate “Iran launched aerial attacks,” “the target was Al-Rifaa radar,” and “the radar was hit” separately.

## Claim: Houthis now control the Bab el-Mandeb Strait

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Strong headline formulations following the Saudi-backed force's withdrawal; Reuters more narrowly says Houthis were left in control of the area near the strait.
- **Why it matters:** Literal control of an international chokepoint implies a much larger power to stop shipping than control of adjacent land.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Government forces withdrew from Dhubab Airport and the Bab el-Mandeb area; Houthis hold positions near the coast and highlands.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Reuters says government units remained at al-Suqyah and were contesting nearby coastal areas. JMIC recorded continued commercial transits and no recent Houthi shipping-attack claim. No evidence establishes legal or physical control of the international waterway.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for the withdrawal and Houthi land gains; no for literal control of the strait or closure.
- **Current assessment:** qualified / misleading.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/yemen-government-forces-withdraw-from-area-near-bab-elmandeb-strait-houthis-in-control-5027740) supports the withdrawal and remaining contest; [JMIC Update 114](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/update-114_jmic_advisory_15-nov.pdf?rev=be65cfbd755a41c4816feba6cef4401c) supports ongoing transit and current maritime conditions.
- **Questions for auditor:** Use “terrain overlooking/near the strait” unless evidence establishes effective maritime control. Verify which islands, batteries, and observation points are held.

## Claim: Russia's GRU ordered the Threod Systems arson attempt

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Estonian Internal Security Service, repeated by the prime minister and foreign minister.
- **Why it matters:** A state-directed attack on a NATO member's defense industry is a serious hybrid-action attribution and the stated basis for closing road crossings.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Estonia says counterintelligence confirmed GRU tasking; eight Latvian residents were detained after cross-border cooperation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The public statements do not disclose communications, payment trails, tasking documents, admissions, or charging evidence. Russia's response was not found in the inspected record.
- **Independent corroboration:** no independent access to the attribution evidence; yes for the attempt, detentions, and lack of major damage.
- **Current assessment:** supported as Estonia's official investigative finding; unverified as an independently established command link.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [Estonian Foreign Ministry](https://www.vm.ee/en/news/foreign-minister-tsahkna-estonias-rapid-and-decisive-action-makes-it-increasingly-difficult) states the official finding and case status; [Estonian government](https://www.valitsus.ee/uudised/peaminister-michal-valmisolek-ja-koostoo-nurjasid-venemaa-eriteenistuste-jarjekordse-sabotaazikatse) provides the prime minister's account.
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek KAPO/prosecutor documents and the suspects' alleged role. Attribute the finding in final prose rather than stating state command without qualification.

## Claim: ASEAN has normalized Myanmar after its military-backed election

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A broad reading of ASEAN's decision to permit unrestricted participation in sectoral bodies and consider phased restoration.
- **Why it matters:** Normalization would alter the military-backed government's regional legitimacy and ASEAN leverage.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The adopted text removes restrictions at sectoral ministerial bodies and creates a route back to full political representation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The same decision retains non-political representation at summits and foreign-ministers' meetings until meaningful progress on the Five-Point Consensus. Full restoration is conditional on sustained and concrete progress.
- **Independent corroboration:** primary text resolves the institutional rules; implementation has not yet been demonstrated.
- **Current assessment:** false as a blanket claim; qualified if limited to expanded sectoral engagement.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [ASEAN decision](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/FINAL-ASEAN-Leaders-Review-and-Decision-on-the-Implementation-of-the-Five-Point-Consensus-15-November-2026.pdf), especially paragraphs 24-25.
- **Questions for auditor:** Identify which bodies and representation levels are affected; do not treat technical access as full political recognition.

## Claim: Neves won 64% with a strong national mandate

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Victory framing around Cape Verde's provisional presidential result.
- **Why it matters:** Vote share and mandate are distinct. The office's limited powers and low turnout constrain the strategic interpretation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** In-window provisional results from 97% of polling stations showed 63.8%; Rosa conceded.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Fewer than one-third of eligible voters participated, final certification was pending, and the president does not direct government policy in Cape Verde's parliamentary system.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for the provisional outcome; no objective test for “strong mandate” without a defined denominator and institutional scope.
- **Current assessment:** supported on provisional victory and share; non-rateable/qualified on “strong mandate.”
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Cape%2BVerde%2BPresident%2BNeves%2Bwins%2Bsecond%2Bterm,%2Bchallenger%2BRosa%2Bconcedes%2Bdefeat/27346480.html) provides the in-window count, turnout, and institutional context; [CNE](https://cne.cv/) is the final authority.
- **Questions for auditor:** State the numerator and denominator; do not convert share of valid votes into share of the electorate.

## Claim: Starshield restrictions were imposed to recognize Russian control of occupied Ukraine

- **Origin / principal promoters:** An inference from Zelenskyy's warning that the geographic restriction sends a signal treating occupied territory as non-Ukrainian.
- **Why it matters:** If deliberate, it would link operational service policy to territorial concessions under negotiation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The 100-mph rule reportedly applies along the front and in occupied territory; Ukraine says the U.S. side controls or can explain the service and senators have requested authorization records.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No U.S. agency or SpaceX rationale was found. Contractual limits, anti-escalation policy, system design, technical configuration, or other explanations remain possible. A political effect does not establish political intent.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for the reported restrictions; no for intent or authorizing actor.
- **Current assessment:** speculative.
- **Confidence:** high that intent is not established; medium on the precise technical scope.
- **Sources:** [Zelenskyy address](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/rozrahovuyemo-sho-bude-spravdi-velikij-obmin-polonenimi-zver-107329) states the reported scope and political interpretation; [Senate letter](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/11132026%20Letter%20to%20Elon%20Musk%20and%20SpaceX%20on%20Starshield%20and%20Starlink.pdf) shows that authorization and rationale remained unresolved.
- **Questions for auditor:** Separate service geography, operational effect, decision authority, and intent. Do not promote a sovereignty signal into proof of a territorial-recognition policy.
