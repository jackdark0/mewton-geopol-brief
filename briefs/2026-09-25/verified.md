[Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/four-takeaways-from-trump-s-summit-with-xi-in-washington-ce785adfd889f22d)

### Source provenance

- Bessent's public remarks are the origin for the January 10 date; SCMP and Reuters are independent reports of that public statement, not two independent agreements.
- China's Foreign Ministry is primary for Beijing's account and for what it says Xi proposed, but not independent proof of U.S. acceptance.

### Open questions

- Will delayed U.S. agricultural purchases and rare-earth deliveries be completed?

### Recommendation

- Include major. The verified change is a short extension with no durable settlement, not a strategic reset.
