## Audit rules applied

- Each proposition below is atomic. A source that proves one part of a story does not prove the rest.
- **Verdict** describes what the evidence supports. **Confidence** describes how likely that verdict is to survive further checking.
- Official statements establish what an institution said or did; they do not independently prove disputed attribution, intent, or operational effect.
- Multiple reports based on the same government statement or wire copy count as one evidence lineage.
- Later developments were excluded even when they appeared during this 07:00 finishing run.

## 1. Merchant-vessel attacks reached Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone

**Priority:** High — a new geographic spread of Black Sea maritime attacks with direct NATO-member exposure.

### Verified propositions

- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** The merchant vessels *Alfa Watan* and *Able* were struck on 6 October in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone. *Alfa Watan* sank; *Able* burned. Bulgaria mounted rescue and response operations. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/bulgaria-prime-minister-ships-drones-economic-zone-59f61af472f0379ebe7b1eb39b768c45) and [Bulgarian National Television](https://bntnews.bg/news/drone-attack-ship-sunk-sailors-rescued-authorities-mobilised-after-black-sea-incident-overview-1415692news.html) independently describe the Bulgarian response.
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** All 18 people aboard *Able* were rescued; two had serious injuries. BNT is the direct national public-broadcaster record for this count.
- **CONFIRMED — medium-high confidence:** Maritime-security firm Ambrey recorded three merchant-vessel attacks in Romanian or Bulgarian exclusive economic zones across 5–6 October, involving aerial or surface drones; it said two vessels sank. [Ambrey threat update](https://ambrey.com/operations/event/treat-update-black-sea-merchant-vessel-attacks/).
- **SUPPORTED AS AN ASSESSMENT — medium confidence:** Ambrey assessed Russia was “highly likely” responsible. Ukraine's president also attributed the attacks to Russia. These are two attribution claims, not two independent public demonstrations of the underlying technical evidence.
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** Bulgarian authorities had not publicly identified the attacker by the cutoff. NATO referred attribution questions to Bulgaria. AP and BNT both preserve that uncertainty.

### Material claims not established

- Public evidence inspected in this run does not establish the launch point, operator, weapon ownership, or chain of command.
- Cargo association with Ukraine may bear on motive, but it is not attribution evidence.
- Ambrey's public summary does not disclose the sensor, forensic, or intelligence basis for its assessment.

### Provenance and independence

AP and BNT report the incident and official response through distinct newsrooms. Ambrey is a commercial risk assessor and supplies an analytical attribution. President Volodymyr Zelensky's statement is a participant-government claim. The latter two may have access to nonpublic information, but their public claims cannot substitute for disclosed evidence.

### Open questions

- Will Bulgaria release debris, radar, electronic, or trajectory evidence?
- Were the Romanian- and Bulgarian-zone incidents one coordinated operation?
- Will NATO change escort, air-policing, or maritime-surveillance posture?

**Recommendation:** Lead story. State the incident as fact and Russian responsibility as an unresolved, evidence-backed assessment.

## 2. Lithuania advanced a constitutional change while courting a permanent US base

**Priority:** High — constitutional change and alliance basing would materially alter Baltic deterrence, but neither is complete.

### Verified propositions

- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** Article 137 of Lithuania's Constitution bars foreign military bases. [Official English Constitution](https://www.lrs.lt/home/Konstitucija/Constitution.htm).
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** On 6 October the Seimas passed the first vote on amendment XVP-1729(2), 106–18 with six abstentions, according to the Seimas roll-call record directly inspected in the Deep Scout pass. [Seimas vote record](https://www.lrs.lt/sip/portal.show?p_r=37067&p_bals_id=-57873).
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** The constitutional ban remained in force at the cutoff. Article 148 requires two votes at least three months apart, each supported by at least two-thirds of all members, for this type of amendment. The expected final vote was reported for 12 January. [Official Constitution](https://www.lrs.lt/home/Konstitucija/Constitution.htm) and [European Pravda](https://www.eurointegration.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/6/7247047/).
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** Prime Minister Inga Ruginienė said Lithuania would cover the costs if the United States agreed to establish a permanent base. President Donald Trump said he would consider the proposal. [Reuters report](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/lithuania-says-it-would-pay-the-cost-if-us-establishes-military-base-ce785dd8df81fe27).
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** The existing US armored battalion presence is rotational, about 1,000 personnel, and dates to 2019. Reuters reports this context.
- **CONFIRMED — high confidence:** No US agreement to establish a permanent base had been announced by the cutoff.

### Material claims not established

- Lithuania had not repealed Article 137.
- A permanent US base had not been agreed, funded, scheduled, or built.
- A constitutional amendment would remove a domestic legal barrier; it would not itself produce a US basing decision.

### Provenance and independence

The constitutional text and roll call are primary legal records. European Pravda supplies timing and political context. Reuters reports the Lithuanian and US political statements. The amendment vote and the basing overture are related but distinct facts and must not be collapsed into one completed policy.

### Open questions

- Does the amendment retain two-thirds support at the January second vote?
- Would a US offer be permanent, rotational under another name, or limited to infrastructure?
- How will Moscow answer the legal and political signal before any force posture changes?

**Recommendation:** Major story. Make the “first of two votes” and “no US acceptance” qualifiers unavoidable.
