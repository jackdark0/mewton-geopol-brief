## Claim: CS-01 — Russian forces conducted the three merchant-vessel attacks reported in Romanian and Bulgarian exclusive economic zones on October 5–6

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy attributes the Bulgarian attacks to Russia in his October 6 evening address. Ambrey Analytics makes a separate attribution assessment covering all three incidents. Neither is treated as a public forensic finding.
- **Why it matters:** Attribution governs the escalation narrative, maritime protection choices, and whether the incidents demonstrate deliberate Russian pressure on Ukrainian trade beyond Ukraine's waters. Location in a NATO member's EEZ alone does not settle the legal or alliance implications.
- **Evidence offered for it:** **CLAIM:** Zelenskyy says Ukrainian and Bulgarian naval contact established Russian responsibility and says Ukrainian forces conducted no operation in that area. **ASSESSMENT by source:** Ambrey grades Russian responsibility for all three incidents highly likely and identifies Danube trade links.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** BNT's October 6 evening reporting says no official information identifies the attacker. It includes a rescued sailor's Russia inference based on Ukrainian cargo, which does not establish launch origin. No inspected wreckage analysis, weapon serials, radar tracks, authenticated launch imagery, or Bulgarian attribution statement resolves responsibility. Cargo association cannot by itself establish perpetrator or motive. Ukrainian non-involvement is also an attributed assertion.
- **Independent corroboration:** **Partial** for the occurrence and damage; **unknown** for attribution. Ambrey is institutionally separate from Ukraine, but its public summary does not disclose whether its attribution depends on independent technical evidence or on the same reports. BNT has a crew-interview stream, but that sailor's nationality inference is not technical corroboration.
- **Current assessment:** **Unclear** as an established fact; Russia attribution has substantive support as an attributed assessment. **Unsupported** is the disposition for claiming that the inspected public record conclusively proves Russian responsibility; that does not mean Russia has been disproved.
- **Confidence:** **Medium** in this provisional classification. A specific official attribution and specialist assessment exist, but their underlying evidence is unavailable and Bulgaria's public reporting remains unresolved.

- **Evidence offered for it:** Reuters reports Trump attributing a threat to Iran and citing known people; it also records official concern about an Iran-backed plot. A bomber redeployment is described in the same report, but relocation does not independently prove the actor behind the danger.
