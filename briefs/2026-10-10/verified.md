## Story: Washington licenses Russian diesel while Ukraine talks continue

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED | High]** On October 9, OFAC issued [General License 135](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937216/download?inline=). It authorizes transactions otherwise prohibited by the two named Russia sanctions regulations that relate to the sale, delivery, offloading, or importation of Russian-origin diesel, including importation into the United States, through 12:01 a.m. EDT on April 7, 2027.
- **[CONFIRMED | High]** The license withholds authorization for debits to US financial-institution accounts of Russia's central bank, National Wealth Fund, or finance ministry. It does not contain an October 9 loading-date restriction. OFAC's [issuance notice](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20261009_33) authenticates the instrument.
- **[CLAIMED | Moderate]** Trump announced more than 300,000 metric tons immediately, another 500,000 in November, and one million afterward, with later supply conditional on refinery condition. [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trump-says-russia-to-supply-diesel-to-us-and-global-market-ce785ddcd881f12d) reported that US diesel futures fell almost 5% after the announcement, while named analysts disputed durable price relief.
- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate]** US, Ukrainian, European, and NATO representatives met in Miami during the announcement. [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/us-ukraine-european-negotiators-meet-in-miami-as-trump-announces-diesel-deal-with-russia-ce785ddcda8df322) attributed a six-hour duration and a planned unified proposal to one person familiar with the talks; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/12baccc7aade559bf329079b0548c9da) independently reported the meeting, not every agenda detail.

### Material claims not established

- No inspected cargo manifest, Russian export order, contract, or delivery record establishes that the announced tranches will arrive.
- The announcement and futures move do not establish durable retail-price relief, net additional global supply, or a measured revenue gain for Moscow.
- The Miami meeting does not establish agreement on territory, security guarantees, a ceasefire, or a common written plan.

### Source provenance

- OFAC notice and license are one controlling US legal lineage.
- Trump's quantities, Russian praise, and the immediate market response are distinct propositions in the same Reuters report; repeated syndications are not independent confirmation.
- AP independently confirms that the meeting occurred. The detailed Miami agenda and duration remain one Reuters anonymous-source lineage.

### Open questions

- Which promised volumes are ready, which are conditional, and which are net new rather than rerouted?
- Will Ukraine alter refinery strikes, and will the US condition intelligence or military support? No pre-cutoff evidence answers either question.
- Will Miami participants publish or jointly acknowledge a proposal?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Lead with the legal authorization and the sanctions-versus-prices tradeoff. Treat delivery, price relief, and diplomatic convergence as unproven.

- **[CONFIRMED | High]** [General License 13](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937186/download?inline=) permits ordinary maintenance or wind-down through 12:01 a.m. EDT on April 7, 2027. Its examples include state-party payments, salaries, health coverage, rent, utilities, taxes, insurance, and routine commercial services.
- **[CONFIRMED | High]** [General License 14](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937191/download?inline=) authorizes specified telecommunications, internet communications, enterprise software, cloud storage, backup, cybersecurity, and related services, subject to its exceptions and other federal requirements. The document states no expiration date.
- **[CONFIRMED | High]** [General License 15](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937196/download?inline=) covers bounded pension transactions. GL 16 addresses services needed for continued detention of named people. These are exceptions, not removal of the institutional designation.
- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate]** [Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Exclusive-US%2Bimposes%2Bsanctions%2Bon%2BInternational%2BCriminal%2BCourt%2C%2Bhours%2Bafter%2Bformer%2Bjudge%2Bwins%2BNobel/27170029.html) recorded US pressure, ICC opposition, EU calls for protection, and the six-month dialogue window. ### Material claims not established

- The licenses contradict any universal claim that all US financial and technology services stopped immediately.
- Formal permissions do not establish that every bank or vendor will continue service; de-risking could create effects beyond the legal minimum.
- Same-day timing does not prove the ICC action was retaliation for the prize. Reporting says sanctions preparations predated the award.
- This policy dispute does not adjudicate the ICC's jurisdiction or the merits of any pending criminal allegation.

### Source provenance

- OFAC documents form one primary US legal source chain. Each license governs a different atomic proposition.
- Reuters supplies reactions and reported policy intent, not independent legal adjudication.
- The Nobel Committee establishes its own award and rationale, not the correctness of every legal position associated with Pillay or the ICC.

### Open questions

- Which banks or service providers change their conduct despite the licenses?
- Will the EU activate a blocking measure or only discuss one?
- What conditions would cause Washington to narrow, extend, or intensify the licenses after April 7?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Frame this as staged institutional pressure with substantial continuity exceptions, not an immediate court shutdown.

Covered operators are advised not to fly in two defined portions of the Jeddah FIR and to exercise caution elsewhere; the bulletin is not a closure of all Saudi airspace.

[Reuters](https://www.aol.ca/articles/houthi-strikes-riyadh-airport-killed-091811000.html) and AP describe different operational measures rather than a clean factual contradiction.
- **[CLAIMED | Moderate]** Pakistan's Foreign Office spokesperson described the Makkah arrangement as defensive, said its forces would not cross the border to attack Yemen, and rejected reports of Pakistani fighter operations. The [briefing account](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistan-says-troop-deployments-under-makkah-pact-to-depend-on-saudi-security-needs-3003674) establishes the declared policy, not field compliance.

### Material claims not established

- Houthi responsibility for the fatal airport impacts has not been established through an inspected forensic record.
- Saudi claims that Bab el-Mandeb was cleared of Houthis remain unverified.
- Pakistani presence in Saudi Arabia does not establish offensive sorties, and official denial does not independently prove no classified operation occurred.
- No new Pakistani unit, command arrangement, rules of engagement, or deployed capability was publicly verified.

### Source provenance

- AP and Reuters casualty accounts both trace the toll to GACA; they are not independent death counts.
- EASA is an independent operational-risk source for covered aviation, not for weapon attribution.
- Pakistan's role limitation is one official statement lineage; press repetition is not operational corroboration.

### Open questions

- What weapon caused the fatal impact, and was the aircraft struck directly or by debris?
- Which carriers restore Riyadh, Yanbu, and Abha service, and when?
- Does Pakistan publish a force package limited to Saudi territorial defense?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Combine the casualty disclosure, EASA's measurable risk response, and Pakistan's stated defensive boundary.
