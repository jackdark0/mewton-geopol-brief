## Candidate: Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone come down in Poland

- **Event time/date:** Overnight 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** Russia, Ukraine, Poland, NATO, Coalition of the Willing.
- **What changed:** Polish authorities identified three Russian objects that crossed into Poland during a large Russian attack on Ukraine: a Kh-101 cruise missile, a second suspected missile and a likely jet-powered drone. No injuries or critical-infrastructure damage were reported. One object landed about 35 kilometers from the Coalition of the Willing exercise at Nowa Deba.
- **Why this may matter:** It is a direct NATO-airspace violation during a force-deployment exercise. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk called intentional provocation likely, but the Russian origin of the objects does not by itself establish deliberate targeting of Poland or the exercise.
- **Initial priority:** very high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high; medium-low on intent.
- **Source chain:** The Polish defense ministry, National Security Bureau and prime minister's office are primary government accounts based on recovery and military tracking. AP independently reported from the exercise and crash-area context, but relied on Polish authorities for identification.
- **Sources:** [Polish defense ministry](https://www.gov.pl/web/obrona-narodowa/wicepremier-w-kosiniak-kamysz-o-naruszeniu-polskiej-przestrzeni-powietrznej); [Polish National Security Bureau](https://www.bbn.gov.pl/pl/wydarzenia/10584%2CNaruszenie-polskiej-przestrzeni-powietrznej-Komentarz-Szefa-BBN.html); [Polish prime minister's office](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/rosyjskie-obiekty-naruszyly-polska-przestrzen-powietrzna-kolejna-odslona-rosyjskiej-eskalacji); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-kyiv-attacks-putin-crimea-433e18f00cbd37a2a9da94e60fce0e07).
- **Questions for auditor:** Did Poland request Article 4 consultations or added deployments? Is the second object confirmed as a missile? What trajectory or targeting evidence bears on intent?

## Candidate: Moscow embraces a U.S. opening on European security and revives its 2021 demands

- **Event time/date:** Russian and U.S. positions reported 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** Russia, United States, Ukraine, NATO and European allies.
- **What changed:** The Kremlin endorsed a U.S. proposal to discuss a broader postwar European security framework and linked that opening to Russia's December 2021 draft demands: no further NATO enlargement to former Soviet states, no allied military activity in Ukraine, and a rollback of NATO deployments toward 1997 lines. Russia's deputy foreign minister separately rejected any Ukrainian "quasi-membership" in NATO.
- **Why this may matter:** The negotiating object may be expanding from a Ukraine ceasefire to NATO's post-Cold War posture. That can create bargaining space on risk reduction, but it also lets Moscow reintroduce demands NATO previously rejected.
- **Initial priority:** very high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on public positions; low on any agreed negotiating mandate.
- **Source chain:** AP synthesized Kremlin statements, U.S. envoy remarks and the public 2021 drafts. The 2021 documents are the primary baseline; no new jointly authenticated negotiating text was found.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-putin-trump-security-europe-60752c921b031be80b1f7a9e83455a0e); [European leaders' November 19 statement](https://www.bundesregierung.de/resource/blob/992814/2461956/d8ca129d517686c27bc6f3fb06ca0bca/2026-11-19-cow-data.pdf?download=1).
- **Questions for auditor:** Did U.S. envoys formally propose NATO-wide negotiations or only discuss them? Which Russian demands were restated verbatim? What authority would Ukraine and European allies have over the agenda?

## Candidate: U.S. and Iran state incompatible public terms while mediation continues

- **Event time/date:** 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** United States, Iran, Qatar, Gulf states.
- **What changed:** Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said messages still move through mediators and that Iran seeks guarantees against renewed war while keeping its highly enriched uranium in Iran. President Donald Trump said Iran wants a deal but not one acceptable to him. Qatar and Iran had met November 17 to narrow proposals; U.S. envoys met Qatari mediators on November 20. Treasury simultaneously sanctioned four alleged oil-revenue trustees.
- **Why this may matter:** The channel is active, but the uranium disposition and confidence-building sequence remain unresolved while economic pressure and regional attacks continue. Public willingness to negotiate is not evidence of convergence.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on public positions and sanctions; medium on private channel content.
- **Source chain:** Reuters separately reported the Iranian and U.S. statements; the White House and Treasury provide primary U.S. records. Anonymous diplomatic accounts establish that mediation is described as active, not that an agreement is close.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/araqchi-says-iran-wants-diplomacy-to-end-war-trump-says-terms-unacceptable-5042853); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-trump-diplomacy-attacks-8fd6dcd6dc4a1a77437cedc4567fbee8); [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/at-press-briefing-president-trump-touts-historic-wins-for-americans-signals-deal-making-on-healthcare/); [U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0697); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/20/witkoff-kushner-meet-qatari-mediators-iran).
- **Questions for auditor:** What did Araqchi mean by keeping enriched material "in the country"? Is dilution under IAEA supervision an authorized option? Did Qatar report any agreed sequencing or merely continued contact?

## Candidate: EU and UK agree concrete post-Brexit defense reintegration steps

- **Event time/date:** EU-UK summit, Brussels, 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** European Union, United Kingdom, Ukraine and NATO allies.
- **What changed:** The summit concluded negotiations on a framework for UK participation in EU civilian CSDP missions, advanced UK participation in two PESCO military-mobility projects, committed to joint naval activity and shadow-fleet enforcement coordination, and linked UK and EU training of Ukrainian forces to the Multinational Force Ukraine. The UK also joined the EU Ukraine Support Loan.
- **Why this may matter:** The relationship is moving from consultation toward operational access, logistics and sanctions enforcement. Several commitments still require signature, accession or later implementation.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The EU-UK joint statement is the authoritative record and is published by both parties; the two publication sites are not independent evidence.
- **Sources:** [EU-UK joint statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukeu-summit-2026-key-documents/uk-eu-summit-2026-joint-statement-html); [Council PDF](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/media/ccpczdvj/joint-statement-eu-uk-summit-20-november-2026.pdf); [UK summit release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/costs-cut-prime-minister-secures-deal-with-eu-to-cut-costs-keep-prices-down-and-create-opportunities-for-young-people-worth-up-to-17-billion-per-year).
- **Questions for auditor:** Which instruments are signed, provisionally applied or only intended? Does PESCO participation give operational rights now? What enforcement actions against the shadow fleet are newly authorized?

## Candidate: Myanmar rejects the ICJ genocide judgment without committing to remedies

- **Event time/date:** Official response 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** Myanmar, Rohingya, The Gambia, ICJ, ASEAN.
- **What changed:** Myanmar's government called the 9-6 genocide finding unfair, repeated its counterterrorism account of the 2017 operations and said it would study the judgment. Its foreign ministry statement did not commit to the ordered legislation, investigation, prosecution, non-repetition or reparation measures.
- **Why this may matter:** The first official response moves the story from legal disposition to expected resistance on implementation. Rejection does not alter the binding judgment, but it makes outside pressure and monitoring more consequential.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** Myanmar's foreign ministry statement is primary for the government's position; AP independently describes the press conference and notes the absence of a remedy commitment.
- **Sources:** [Myanmar foreign ministry](https://www.mofa.gov.mm/en/press-statement-by-the-ministry-of-foreign-affairs-of-the-republic-of-the-union-of-myanmar-on-the-judgment-of-the-international-court-of-justice-delivered-on-19-november-2026/); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/c76dcb3f97c1077262f87e0cba01bb78); [ICJ summary](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-sum-01-00-en.pdf).
- **Questions for auditor:** Did Myanmar explicitly reject compliance or only criticize the reasoning? Which remedy has the earliest observable deadline? What concrete ASEAN or third-state action followed by cutoff?

## Candidate: Saudi-led coalition strikes Hodeidah as Houthis continue attacks on Saudi Arabia

- **Event time/date:** 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** Saudi Arabia, Yemen's Houthis, Red Sea shipping.
- **What changed:** The Saudi-led coalition said it struck alleged weapon-smuggling and ballistic-missile transport operations at Hodeidah port and military capabilities in Sanaa. Saudi Arabia said it intercepted five drones and one ballistic missile; the Houthis claimed attacks on Jazan port and an Al-Shuqaiq power station. Saudi authorities acknowledged debris near Al-Shuqaiq but reported no damage.

- **Confidence in basic event:** medium-high on official actions; low on target effects.
- **Source chain:** AP reports both official accounts but does not independently verify target characterization or damage. Saudi and Houthi statements are interested sources.
- **Sources:** [AP republication](https://cmg-cmg-rd-20021-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/world/saudi-arabia/Z5LP6WO6P46LVBK3NOCZKL3KWM/); [AP Iran diplomacy report](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-trump-diplomacy-attacks-8fd6dcd6dc4a1a77437cedc4567fbee8).

## Candidate: Iraq implements a compromise entry into Jurf al-Sakhar

- **Event time/date:** Headquarters established 2026-11-20.
- **Region / actors:** Iraqi government, Kataib Hezbollah, Popular Mobilization Forces, Iran.
- **What changed:** Iraqi army, federal police and energy police established a command headquarters outside the militia stronghold. Two anonymous Iraqi officials told AP that Kataib Hezbollah would not leave immediately but would withdraw within a month; PMF personnel remain in the deployment. Iran's Quds Force commander reportedly mediated the compromise.
- **Why this may matter:** Baghdad gained a physical state presence without an immediate clash. The test is whether one-month withdrawal and investigation promises change real command, access and smuggling control.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on deployment; medium-low on the unpublished agreement and withdrawal schedule.
- **Source chain:** AP observed or confirmed the deployment but relied on two unnamed officials for the agreement and Qaani mediation.
- **Sources:** [AP republication](https://cmg-cmg-rd-20021-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/world/saudi-arabia/Z5LP6WO6P46LVBK3NOCZKL3KWM/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Is the one-month deadline in a written Iraqi instrument? Who commands the mixed force? Has the government gained unrestricted entry or investigative authority?

## Candidate: U.S.-China-Russia nuclear dialogue starts with an unsupported parity forecast

- **Event time/date:** Trump remarks 2026-11-20 after the November 19 trilateral.
- **Region / actors:** United States, China, Russia.
- **What changed:** Trump said China would catch the United States in nuclear weapons within ten years. The three leaders' declaration commits only to dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and proliferation; it names no negotiators, ceilings, verification rules or timetable.
- **Why this may matter:** China entering three-way nuclear-risk dialogue is new and potentially useful. The parity forecast is not supported by the Pentagon estimate AP cited: China's stockpile was in the low 600s through 2024 and projected above 1,000 by 2030, while U.S. and Russian stockpiles remain in the thousands.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on the statement; low on the forecast.
- **Source chain:** The White House and joint declaration establish what was said and agreed; AP supplies the Pentagon comparison.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/trump-xi-china-nuclear-weapons-286391edaacc7e8544dc0dc023e253fb); [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/at-press-briefing-president-trump-touts-historic-wins-for-americans-signals-deal-making-on-healthcare/).
- **Questions for auditor:** What denominator did Trump mean: total inventory, deployed strategic warheads or production capacity? Has China agreed to arms limits or only dialogue?
