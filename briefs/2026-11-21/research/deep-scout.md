## Candidate: The Poland incursion tests intent attribution and NATO restraint at once

- **Underlying development:** Two Russian missiles and a likely jet-powered drone crossed into Poland during a large strike on Ukraine, near an exercise rehearsing a possible European force for postwar Ukraine.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Even accidental or spillover incursions force NATO to activate surveillance, air defense and consultation procedures. A deliberate test would probe detection and political thresholds; repeated spillover without intent can still create escalation through miscalculation.
- **Observable now:** Recovered Kh-101; two other Russian-origin objects under examination; Polish and allied aircraft and air defenses activated; no reported casualties or critical-infrastructure damage.
- **What remains inference:** Deliberate targeting, selection of the exercise area, Russian command intent, and whether NATO changes posture.
- **Initial priority:** very high.
- **Sources:** [Polish defense ministry](https://www.gov.pl/web/obrona-narodowa/wicepremier-w-kosiniak-kamysz-o-naruszeniu-polskiej-przestrzeni-powietrznej); [Polish National Security Bureau](https://www.bbn.gov.pl/pl/wydarzenia/10584%2CNaruszenie-polskiej-przestrzeni-powietrznej-Komentarz-Szefa-BBN.html); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-kyiv-attacks-putin-crimea-433e18f00cbd37a2a9da94e60fce0e07).
- **Questions for auditor:** Look for radar/trajectory evidence and NATO actions, not rhetoric alone.

## Candidate: A Ukraine deal is becoming a contest over Europe's security architecture

- **Underlying development:** Washington floated wider European-security talks; Moscow welcomed them and pointed back to its 2021 NATO rollback proposals, while European leaders said EU- or NATO-related terms require their consent.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Expanding the agenda can enable reciprocal risk-reduction measures, but it also changes bargaining leverage by placing allied deployments and enlargement policy on the table alongside occupied Ukrainian territory and ceasefire terms.
- **Observable now:** Kremlin endorsement; public restatement of 2021 demands; Russian rejection of Ukrainian quasi-NATO status; European consent condition.
- **What remains inference:** A U.S. commitment to negotiate alliance posture, an agreed forum, or European acceptance of the agenda.
- **Initial priority:** very high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-putin-trump-security-europe-60752c921b031be80b1f7a9e83455a0e); [European leaders' statement](https://www.bundesregierung.de/resource/blob/992814/2461956/d8ca129d517686c27bc6f3fb06ca0bca/2026-11-19-cow-data.pdf?download=1).
- **Questions for auditor:** Separate discussion of security architecture from acceptance of Russian terms.

## Candidate: EU-UK defense ties now have operational interfaces, not only consultation

- **Underlying development:** The second summit concluded or advanced mechanisms for civilian CSDP missions, PESCO military mobility, logistics hubs, maritime activity, shadow-fleet enforcement, training and the Ukraine Support Loan.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Access to EU mission frameworks and movement networks can reduce friction for British forces, trainers and sanctions enforcement. Durable effects depend on legal completion, funding and repeated operations.
- **Observable now:** Completed negotiation of the civilian-mission participation agreement; progress toward two PESCO projects; new joint-activity and coordination commitments.
- **What remains inference:** Immediate access, specific deployments, new enforcement authorities or procurement orders.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [EU-UK joint statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukeu-summit-2026-key-documents/uk-eu-summit-2026-joint-statement-html); [Council PDF](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/media/ccpczdvj/joint-statement-eu-uk-summit-20-november-2026.pdf).
- **Questions for auditor:** Identify signatures and entry-into-force steps for each mechanism.

## Candidate: Iran diplomacy is blocked on sequence and custody, not on whether messages exist

- **Underlying development:** Both sides acknowledge continued indirect contact. Iran demands credible no-war guarantees and insists enriched material remain in country; Trump says the terms are unacceptable. Treasury adds sanctions rather than offering public relief.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Custody of enriched material bears on breakout risk and verification; sequencing sanctions relief, blockade changes and inspections determines who gives up leverage first. Public statements can be bargaining positions rather than final offers.
- **Observable now:** Araqchi's stated red line; Trump's rejection; Qatar-Iran contact; fresh Treasury designations.
- **What remains inference:** Exact proposals, authorized fallback positions, whether dilution is acceptable, and whether renewed strikes are imminent.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/araqchi-says-iran-wants-diplomacy-to-end-war-trump-says-terms-unacceptable-5042853); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-trump-diplomacy-attacks-8fd6dcd6dc4a1a77437cedc4567fbee8); [Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0697).
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek primary Iranian wording and avoid treating mediator activity as convergence.

## Candidate: Myanmar's first response makes noncompliance the near-term baseline

- **Underlying development:** The government attacks the judgment's reasoning and repeats the military's counterterrorism narrative while offering no remedy timetable.
- **Strategic mechanism:** The ICJ cannot directly execute its order; implementation depends on Myanmar institutions and external diplomatic, financial or legal pressure. A public refusal to commit increases the value of measurable remedy deadlines.
- **Observable now:** Official criticism; promise to study the judgment; no stated legislation, investigation, prosecution or compensation plan.
- **What remains inference:** Formal defiance, future partial compliance and third-state coercive action.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [Myanmar foreign ministry](https://www.mofa.gov.mm/en/press-statement-by-the-ministry-of-foreign-affairs-of-the-republic-of-the-union-of-myanmar-on-the-judgment-of-the-international-court-of-justice-delivered-on-19-november-2026/); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/c76dcb3f97c1077262f87e0cba01bb78); [ICJ summary](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-sum-01-00-en.pdf).
- **Questions for auditor:** Distinguish criticism of judgment from legal noncompliance, and publish the first concrete deadline.

## Candidate: Jurf al-Sakhar may become a timed test of militia subordination

- **Underlying development:** Iraqi state forces established a headquarters under a compromise that reportedly gives Kataib Hezbollah one month to withdraw while PMF personnel remain.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A timed withdrawal could convert nominal state presence into control. A mixed deployment can also absorb the militia into a state label without changing command, access or illicit revenue.
- **Observable now:** Headquarters established; army, federal and energy police deployed; mixed PMF presence.
- **What remains inference:** Written deadline, real command authority, investigation access, compliance and Quds Force control.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [AP republication](https://cmg-cmg-rd-20021-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/world/saudi-arabia/Z5LP6WO6P46LVBK3NOCZKL3KWM/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Treat the one-month withdrawal as a sourced claim until a public Iraqi instrument appears.
