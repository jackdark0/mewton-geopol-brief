## Candidate: USTR opens broad Section 301 tariff proposals against 14 economies

- **Event time/date:** November 25, 2026.
- **Region / actors:** United States; China, the EU, India, Vietnam, Taiwan and nine other economies.
- **What changed:** USTR made formal Section 301 determinations against 14 economies and proposed economy-specific tariffs for public comment. The measures are not yet imposed: comments are due January 5, 2027, and hearings begin January 25.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - The proposals create bargaining deadlines across several major US trade relationships.
  - The contemplated rates vary materially, including up to 35% for Vietnam and up to 25% for India.
  - Misreporting proposals as already imposed would overstate the immediate shock.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The primary USTR notice controls. AP and other reporting derive the rates and legal posture from it.
- **Sources:** [USTR](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural) establishes the determinations, proposed rates, comment deadline and hearing date; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/us-trump-tariffs-china-europe-india-overcapacity-367083ea32f71fd7f70029ba7df66bf0) provides independent policy and political context.
- **Questions for auditor:** Which rates are ceilings, which are net of other duties, what exemptions apply, and when could final actions take effect?

## Candidate: Baghdad orders federal control over Kurdish oil and border functions

- **Event time/date:** November 24-25, 2026.
- **Region / actors:** Iraq; federal government, Kurdistan Regional Government, KDP and PUK.
- **What changed:** Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi ordered the federal Oil Ministry to manage specified fields under Kurdish security control, directed federal control of Kurdistan-region border crossings, and limited oil-export approvals to the federal ministry. Kurdish leaders across party lines rejected the measures as unconstitutional and called for dialogue.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - Implementation would shift fiscal and territorial leverage from Erbil to Baghdad.
  - The order links a smuggling investigation to a much wider assertion of federal power.
  - Kurdish cross-party resistance raises the risk of a constitutional and security confrontation.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high on the orders and rejection; medium on implementation.
- **Source chain:** Reuters inspected the federal statement; KRG and Kurdistan presidency statements are primary for the Kurdish response.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/iraq-orders-takeover-of-oilfields-in-areas-under-kurdish-control) gives the scope of the federal order; [Kurdistan presidency](https://presidency.gov.krd/en/statement-by-president-nechirvan-barzani-1) and [KRG representation](https://us.gov.krd/kurdish-leaders-reaffirm-unity-constitutional-rights-and-call-for-dialogue-with-baghdad/) establish the rejection and cross-party position.
- **Questions for auditor:** Has federal control actually changed at any crossing or field? What is the precise constitutional and court posture as of cutoff?

## Candidate: BBC publishes a 490,334 floor from leaked Russian death records

- **Event time/date:** BBC publication November 25, based on data through September 29, 2026.
- **Region / actors:** Russia and Ukraine; BBC Russian, Mediazona, Meduza, Rosstat, Cyber Partisans and Silent Crow.
- **What changed:** BBC analysis reported at least 490,334 Russian military deaths recorded in leaked Rosstat material. Mediazona's related methodology found roughly 490,000 registered deaths and separately estimated about 700,000 total deaths after accounting for registration delays and missing cases.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - The registered-record floor is far above Moscow's last public official toll.
  - The records could materially change assessments of Russia's manpower cost and mobilization burden.
  - The 490,334 record count and the roughly 700,000 modeled estimate require different confidence labels.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** medium-high on authenticity and the registered floor; medium on the extrapolated total.
- **Source chain:** BBC and Mediazona collaborated on the named list, so they are not wholly independent. Separate investigators using the same leaked corpus reached broadly similar registered totals.
- **Sources:** [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmx27g533zwyo) reports the 490,334 count; [Mediazona](https://en.zona.media/article/2026/11/23/rosstat_leaked) explains the filters, cross-checks and extrapolation; [iStories](https://istories.media/en/stories/2026/11/23/a-russian-soldier-dies-every-five-minutes-in-the-war-with-ukraine/) reports a separately derived total from the same leak.
- **Questions for auditor:** Which records count foreign fighters, occupied-region residents, missing soldiers declared dead, and noncombat deaths? What exactly does Rosstat deny?

## Candidate: Council makes EUR45 billion from the existing Ukraine loan accessible for 2027

- **Event time/date:** Decision adopted November 23 and published in the Official Journal on November 25.
- **Region / actors:** European Union and Ukraine.
- **What changed:** A Council implementing decision approved Ukraine's 2027 financing strategy and made up to EUR45 billion from the previously established EUR90 billion Ukraine Support Loan accessible for 2027, including EUR31.7 billion for defense-industrial capacity and EUR13.3 billion in budget and macro-financial support.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - This is an adopted legal implementation step, not merely a summit pledge.
  - The defense/budget split shows where the existing facility is intended to deliver capability.
  - It is distinct from the Commission's separate proposal for an additional EUR45 billion loan now before leaders.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The Official Journal decision is controlling; the Commission proposal concerns a separate instrument.
- **Sources:** [Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2623](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623) establishes the approved 2027 allocations; [Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf) describes the separate proposed additional loan.
- **Questions for auditor:** Which disbursements remain conditional? Confirm that the adopted EUR45 billion is not the extra instrument being debated at the November 26-27 summit.

## Candidate: Houthis claim Ras Tanura attack as Saudi Arabia reports four interceptions

## Candidate: China extends the US critical-minerals control pause

- **Event time/date:** November 24, 2026.
- **Region / actors:** China and United States.
- **What changed:** China's Ministry of Commerce extended to January 27, 2027 the suspension of controls that would otherwise presumptively block US-bound dual-use items related to gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard materials and tighten graphite end-user review.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - The extension avoids a November 28 supply-chain cliff.
  - It preserves Chinese leverage because the underlying control framework remains in place.
  - The two-month horizon creates another near-term trade deadline.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** Chinese official republication controls; Reuters independently confirms the date and scope.
- **Sources:** [Chinese embassy republication of the Ministry of Commerce notice](https://la.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/news/202611/t20261125_12066919.htm) establishes scope and dates; [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/china-extends-suspension-of-ban-on-us-bound-gallium-germanium-antimony-exports-ce7851dad08efe22) provides independent context.
