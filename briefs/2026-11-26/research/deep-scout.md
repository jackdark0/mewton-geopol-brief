## Candidate: USTR's tariff package is a negotiating calendar, not an enacted shock

- **Underlying development:** USTR completed Section 301 determinations for 14 economies and opened proposed responses for comment through January 5, with hearings beginning January 25.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Headlines often collapse the legal finding, proposed tariff ceiling, negotiated alternative rate and final action into one event.
- **Strategic mechanism:** The proposals create leverage before implementation. Trading partners can bargain over agreements and exemptions while US importers reprice political risk. Because rates are stated net of other tariffs in different ways, the marginal burden cannot be inferred from the headline ceiling alone.
- **What evidence is observable now:** USTR's determinations, economy-specific contemplated rates, exemptions, comment deadline and hearing date.
- **What remains inference:** Final rates, effective dates, product coverage, retaliation and whether the process survives domestic political resistance.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [USTR](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural) is the primary notice; [AP](https://apnews.com/article/us-trump-tariffs-china-europe-india-overcapacity-367083ea32f71fd7f70029ba7df66bf0) supplies context without changing the proposal's status.
- **Questions for auditor:** Build a clean table of proposed ceilings and negotiated alternatives only if the final brief needs numbers; avoid implying immediate collection.

## Candidate: Baghdad is testing whether federal legal authority can become physical control

- **Underlying development:** The Iraqi prime minister ordered federal management of specified oilfields, export approvals and Kurdistan-region border crossings after a smuggling inquiry.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The order is more consequential than another budget dispute because it touches security control, customs, oil marketing and disputed territory at once.
- **Strategic mechanism:** If implemented, Baghdad gains revenue visibility and coercive leverage over Erbil. If resisted, the government must choose between negotiated coordination and security deployment, with risks to oil flows and Iraq's internal balance.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The written scope reported from the federal statement and a rare unified rejection by major Kurdish actors.
- **What remains inference:** Whether federal personnel can take control without confrontation, whether the orders apply inside the constitutionally administered region or only disputed areas, and whether smuggling evidence justifies the full scope.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/iraq-orders-takeover-of-oilfields-in-areas-under-kurdish-control); [Kurdistan presidency](https://presidency.gov.krd/en/statement-by-president-nechirvan-barzani-1); [KRG representation](https://us.gov.krd/kurdish-leaders-reaffirm-unity-constitutional-rights-and-call-for-dialogue-with-baghdad/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Verify any court ruling before using it; distinguish an order, a legal authorization and effective control.

## Candidate: The Rosstat leak provides a floor and a model, not one casualty number

- **Underlying development:** Analysts identified about 490,000 registered Russian military deaths in leaked Rosstat records through late September and estimated a higher total after modeling registration delays and missing cases.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Public discussion gravitates to the largest number, while the evidentiary status differs sharply between observed records and extrapolation.
- **Strategic mechanism:** The floor informs Russia's replacement burden, regional demographic losses and the fiscal cost of recruitment and death benefits. The extrapolation may better approximate total loss but carries more model risk.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Cross-matches with a named-dead list, the probate registry, civil-registry leaks and published Rosstat totals; explicit filtering rules for war-coded deaths, court declarations and combat indicators.
- **What remains inference:** Completeness, classification errors, occupied-territory coverage, foreigners and the lag adjustment behind the roughly 700,000 estimate.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmx27g533zwyo), [Mediazona methodology](https://en.zona.media/article/2026/11/23/rosstat_leaked), [iStories independent analysis](https://istories.media/en/stories/2026/11/23/a-russian-soldier-dies-every-five-minutes-in-the-war-with-ukraine/).
- **Questions for auditor:** State the denominator and cutoff for every number; treat Rosstat's leak denial as a provenance dispute, not a rebuttal of the cross-checks.

## Candidate: Two different EUR45 billion Ukraine actions are at risk of being blended

- **Underlying development:** The Council adopted a decision allocating the 2027 half of the existing EUR90 billion Ukraine Support Loan; separately, the Commission proposed another EUR45 billion instrument for the same year.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Both amounts are EUR45 billion and appear in the same summit cycle, inviting double counting or false claims that the extra loan has already passed.
- **Strategic mechanism:** The adopted decision turns a previously authorized envelope into a 2027 defense/budget allocation. The new proposal would add borrowing capacity but still needs political and legislative agreement.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The Official Journal makes up to EUR31.7 billion available for defense-industrial capacity and EUR13.3 billion for budget/macro-financial support under the existing loan. The Commission's separate proposal is up to EUR25 billion military and EUR20 billion general budget support.
- **What remains inference:** Disbursement timing, conditionality outcomes, member-state agreement on the extra loan and the ultimate financing cost.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Council Implementing Decision](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623); [Commission proposal](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf); [special European Council agenda](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/european-council/2026/11/26-27/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Confirm which legal instrument each figure belongs to and do not combine them as EUR90 billion in new support.

## Candidate: Ras Tanura shows how an unverified target-effect claim can move markets

- **Underlying development:** Houthis claimed a successful refinery attack; Saudi Arabia reported four drones intercepted at Ras Tanura; Brent rose 3.5% on the day.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The strategic event before cutoff may be the attempt and market repricing, not verified physical damage.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Repeated attempts against Saudi energy nodes can raise insurance, air-defense and pricing costs even when interception rates are high. Conversely, treating every claim as a successful strike exaggerates Houthi coercive capacity.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The Saudi interception statement, the Houthi claim and the settlement move.
- **What remains inference:** Penetration, damage, refinery throughput, Aramco response and causal attribution for the entire oil move.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [SPA](https://spa.gov.sa/N2735707); [Reuters market report](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/oil-prices-rise-after-houthis-claim-attack-saudi-refinery).
- **Questions for auditor:** Freeze evidence at 07:00 UTC and exclude later satellite assessments.

## Candidate: China's mineral pause preserves both supply and leverage

- **Underlying development:** Beijing extended through January 27 the suspension of a stricter US-specific export-control clause covering gallium, germanium, antimony, superhard materials and graphite review.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** It is a non-event in shipment terms but a meaningful avoided escalation and a deliberately short renewal.
- **Strategic mechanism:** US users retain access under the existing licensing environment, while Beijing keeps a legal switch it can reactivate during the next trade negotiation.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The official dates and the suspended clause's scope.
- **What remains inference:** Actual license approval volumes, inventory effects, military-user treatment and whether the pause is renewed again.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Chinese official republication](https://la.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/news/202611/t20261125_12066919.htm); [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/china-extends-suspension-of-ban-on-us-bound-gallium-germanium-antimony-exports-ce7851dad08efe22).
- **Questions for auditor:** Keep "suspension of controls" distinct from unrestricted exports.

## Candidate: Golden Dragon makes Ream's dual-use military value observable

- **Underlying development:** China and Cambodia opened the largest edition of Golden Dragon at the Chinese-funded Ream Naval Base with joint land, sea and air activity.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The useful evidence is the scale and repeatable access pattern, not speculation that a secret permanent base has been proved.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Large recurring exercises can build familiarity, logistics and access without a formal exclusive-basing agreement. Cambodia can also point to a recent US ship visit and planned US exercise to contest an exclusivity narrative.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Exercise dates, missions, venue, three Chinese vessels and Cambodian characterization of record scale.
- **What remains inference:** Permanent access, exclusive privileges, contingency use and command arrangements.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Sources:** [China's defense ministry](https://eng.mod.gov.cn/2025xb/N/T/16501985.html); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/cambodia-china-military-army-naval-base-45c1eb364c82c346340916e6174ea8e3).
- **Questions for auditor:** Do not infer permanent basing from exercise access.
