# Claim scout - 2026-11-26

**Cutoff:** 2026-11-26 02:00:00 America/New_York (07:00:00 UTC)
**Window:** approximately 2026-11-24 20:00:00 to 2026-11-26 02:00:00 America/New_York

## Claim: The United States imposed new tariffs on 14 economies on November 25

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Headline and shorthand descriptions of USTR's announcement.
- **Why it matters:** It changes the immediate trade shock from a future risk into a present cost if stated incorrectly.
- **Evidence offered for it:** USTR completed actionable Section 301 determinations and published proposed tariff rates.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** USTR expressly labels the responsive actions proposals for public comment; comments run to January 5 and hearings begin January 25. No universal effective date appears in the inspected primary notice.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for the announcement; no for the stronger "already imposed" formulation.
- **Current assessment:** false as stated; supported only as proposed tariffs following completed determinations.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [USTR](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/us-trump-tariffs-china-europe-india-overcapacity-367083ea32f71fd7f70029ba7df66bf0).
- **Questions for auditor:** Does any economy-specific notice set a provisional collection date not shown in the overview?

## Claim: Baghdad has taken control of Kurdistan's oilfields and border crossings

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Maximalist summaries of the Iraqi prime minister's November 24 orders.
- **Why it matters:** Effective physical control would be a larger constitutional and security change than the issuance of orders.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The prime minister directed federal management, border control and export approval; federal authorities framed the measures as sovereign and anti-smuggling actions.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Kurdish authorities rejected the measures, and no inspected pre-cutoff evidence shows completed handovers at all named fields or crossings.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial.
- **Current assessment:** supported as an order; unclear as completed control.
- **Confidence:** high on the order, low on implementation.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/iraq-orders-takeover-of-oilfields-in-areas-under-kurdish-control); [Kurdistan presidency](https://presidency.gov.krd/en/statement-by-president-nechirvan-barzani-1); [KRG representation](https://us.gov.krd/kurdish-leaders-reaffirm-unity-constitutional-rights-and-call-for-dialogue-with-baghdad/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Which sites, if any, changed command or staffing before cutoff?

## Claim: Leaked Rosstat data proves exactly 700,000 Russian soldiers were killed

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Compressed summaries of Mediazona/Meduza's registered records and lag-adjusted estimate.
- **Why it matters:** The number affects assessments of Russian manpower, war sustainability and demographic damage.
- **Evidence offered for it:** About 490,000 registered military deaths were identified using explicit war codes, court declarations and secondary combat indicators, with strong cross-matches to several external registers. Analysts modeled delayed and missing registrations to reach roughly 700,000.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The 700,000 figure is an estimate rather than a direct count. The stolen corpus is not an official public release; Rosstat acknowledged a cyberattack but denied protected data leaked. Coverage and classification assumptions remain.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial. Multiple analysts used the same leaked corpus, reducing independence despite separate methods.
- **Current assessment:** supported as a roughly 490,000 registered-record floor; plausible but less certain as a roughly 700,000 total estimate; false if presented as an exact proved count.
- **Confidence:** medium-high on the floor; medium on the model.
- **Sources:** [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmx27g533zwyo); [Mediazona](https://en.zona.media/article/2026/11/23/rosstat_leaked); [iStories](https://istories.media/en/stories/2026/11/23/a-russian-soldier-dies-every-five-minutes-in-the-war-with-ukraine/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Publish the observed and modeled denominators separately.

## Claim: The EU approved a new extra EUR45 billion Ukraine loan

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Coverage blending the November 25 Official Journal decision with the November 18 Commission proposal.
- **Why it matters:** It can double-count support and erase the remaining political decision.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The Council decision makes EUR45 billion available for 2027; the Commission has proposed another EUR45 billion instrument.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The Council decision allocates the 2027 share of the already established EUR90 billion Ukraine Support Loan. The additional EUR45 billion proposal remained before leaders at cutoff.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes on both separate instruments.
- **Current assessment:** false as a single adopted new extra loan; supported as two distinct EUR45 billion actions with different legal status.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Official Journal decision](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623); [Commission proposal](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf); [Council summit agenda](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/european-council/2026/11/26-27/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Use labels such as "existing facility" and "additional proposal" every time the amounts appear together.

## Claim: Houthi drones successfully struck and damaged Ras Tanura refinery

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Houthi military spokesperson Yahya Saree and derivative reports.
- **Why it matters:** Verified damage at Saudi Arabia's largest refinery would show penetration of air defenses and could affect supply.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The Houthis claimed a successful attack and fires; the target was under attack, according to Saudi reporting of four interceptions; oil prices rose on the claim.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Saudi Arabia said four drones were intercepted and reported no initial casualties. No Aramco outage, throughput change, damage report or independently verified target effect was inspected before the cutoff. Later satellite reporting is out of scope for this edition.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for an attempted attack; no for successful target effect.
- **Current assessment:** supported that an attack was attempted and interceptions occurred; unclear whether the refinery was hit or damaged.
- **Confidence:** high on attempt/interception; low on effect.
- **Sources:** [SPA](https://spa.gov.sa/N2735707); [Reuters market report](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/oil-prices-rise-after-houthis-claim-attack-saudi-refinery).
- **Questions for auditor:** Preserve the edition freeze even if later imagery clarifies the event.

## Claim: Golden Dragon 2026 proves China has a permanent exclusive base at Ream

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Strategic-base narratives attached to Chinese-funded expansion and repeat PLA use.
- **Why it matters:** Permanent exclusive access would change regional force-posture assessment.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The largest Golden Dragon exercise opened at Ream with Chinese ships and combined forces; China funded the base's expansion.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Exercise access is not a basing treaty. Cambodia denies exclusive privileges, a US warship visited in January, and a US-Cambodia exercise is scheduled for February 2027.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for the exercise; no for permanent exclusivity.
- **Current assessment:** weak as proof of a permanent exclusive base; supported as evidence of substantial repeat Chinese access and military cooperation.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [China's defense ministry](https://eng.mod.gov.cn/2025xb/N/T/16501985.html); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/cambodia-china-military-army-naval-base-45c1eb364c82c346340916e6174ea8e3).
- **Questions for auditor:** Look for treaty text, exclusive-support arrangements or persistent deployment evidence before upgrading.
