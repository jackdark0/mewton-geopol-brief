## Story: USTR proposes new Section 301 tariffs against 14 economies

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **CONFIRMED:** On November 25, USTR made affirmative Section 301 determinations covering China, the European Union, Singapore, Switzerland, Indonesia, Malaysia, Cambodia, Thailand, South Korea, Vietnam, Taiwan, Bangladesh, Japan and India. It did not make determinations on Mexico or Norway. [USTR](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural)
- **CONFIRMED:** The responsive tariffs are proposals for public comment, not duties already imposed. Written comments and hearing requests are due January 5, 2027; hearings begin January 25. [USTR](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural)
- **CONFIRMED:** The proposed ceilings and structures differ by economy. Examples include up to 35% for Vietnam, up to 25% for India, up to 20% for most covered Chinese goods, 10% for certain Chinese Annex A goods, and 15% for EU goods net of most-favored-nation rates and existing Section 301 tariffs. Product exemptions and offsets matter. [USTR](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural)

### Material claims not established

- New duties took effect on November 25. They did not; the action is at the proposal and comment stage.
- Every import from each economy would face the headline rate. The notices contain exclusions and, for several economies, rate offsets or possible negotiated adjustments.

### Source provenance

- USTR's release and linked Federal Register notices are the controlling primary record. Secondary reports interpret the same notices and are not independent proof of legal effect.

### Open questions

- Which product exclusions survive the comment process, and which economies negotiate lower rates before final action?
- When, if at all, will USTR adopt final tariffs and specify an effective date?

### Recommendation

- Include major. Lead with the bargaining calendar and legal status, not the maximum rates alone.

### Source provenance

### Open questions

- Include major, with “ordered” rather than “took control.”

## Story: Leaked Russian records support a roughly 490,000 registered-death floor

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **SUPPORTED:** Mediazona's analysis of leaked Rosstat-linked civil-registry material found at least roughly 490,000 registered Russian military deaths through late September 2026. Its filtered records were cross-checked against a named-dead list, the national probate registry, court rulings, other civil-registry leaks and previously published Rosstat statistics. [Mediazona](https://en.zona.media/article/2026/11/23/rosstat_leaked)
- **SUPPORTED:** BBC Russian's November 25 publication reported a floor of 490,334. The BBC and Mediazona cooperate on the named list, so their results are not wholly independent. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmx27g533zwyo) [Mediazona](https://en.zona.media/article/2026/11/23/rosstat_leaked)
- **SUPPORTED:** iStories, using the same leaked corpus but its own filtering, found more than 499,000 death records. That methodological convergence is useful, but it is not independent confirmation of the underlying leak. [iStories](https://istories.media/en/stories/2026/11/23/a-russian-soldier-dies-every-five-minutes-in-the-war-with-ukraine/)
- **CLAIMED:** Mediazona estimates the true total was around 700,000 by September after accounting for delayed registrations and missing cases. This is a model, not a count in the leaked records. [Mediazona](https://en.zona.media/article/2026/11/23/rosstat_leaked)

### Material claims not established

- Exactly 700,000 Russian soldiers had been killed. The estimate has model uncertainty and a different denominator from the registered-record floor.
- The leak's authenticity has been independently authenticated by Rosstat. Rosstat acknowledged a cyberattack but disputed the leak; the newsroom cross-checks are the basis for confidence.

### Source provenance

- BBC Russian and Mediazona share the named-list project. iStories used the same hacker-supplied source corpus. The outlets provide partially independent filtering and validation, not independent acquisition.

### Open questions

- How many foreign recruits, occupied-territory residents, missing soldiers later declared dead and noncombat deaths are captured by each filter?
- Will a verifiable official dataset or demographic release corroborate the leak at the aggregate level?

### Recommendation

- Include major. Keep the registered floor, the modeled total and the leak-authenticity dispute separate.

## Story: EU adopts the 2027 allocation from its existing Ukraine Support Loan

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **CONFIRMED:** Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2623 was adopted November 23 and published November 25. It makes up to EUR45 billion from the existing EUR90 billion Ukraine Support Loan accessible for 2027. [Official Journal](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623)
- **CONFIRMED:** The decision allocates EUR31.7 billion to defence-industrial capacity and EUR13.3 billion to budget and macro-financial assistance, split into two EUR6.65 billion components. [Official Journal](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623)
- **CONFIRMED:** This adopted allocation is distinct from the Commission's November 18 proposal for an additional loan of up to EUR45 billion for 2027, split up to EUR25 billion for military assistance and EUR20 billion for budget support. The latter still needs legislative approval. [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf)
- **CONFIRMED:** EU leaders were scheduled to discuss Ukraine's 2027 financing needs at the November 26-27 special European Council, after this edition's cutoff. [European Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/european-council/2026/11/26-27/)

### Material claims not established

- The EU adopted a new extra EUR45 billion loan on November 25. It implemented the 2027 tranche of the existing loan; the extra instrument remained a proposal at cutoff.
- All EUR45 billion will be disbursed immediately. Instalments, modalities and conditions still govern access.

### Source provenance

- The Official Journal decision controls the adopted action. The Commission release controls the separate proposal. Coverage that calls both actions “the EUR45 billion loan” risks conflating them.

### Open questions

- What agreement, if any, will leaders reach on the additional proposal?
- What conditions and procurement rules will govern actual 2027 disbursements?

### Recommendation

- Include major. Use the two-instrument distinction as the organizing fact.
