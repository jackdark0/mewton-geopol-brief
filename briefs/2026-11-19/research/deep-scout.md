## Candidate: The trilateral summit created political cover, not yet arms-control architecture

- **Underlying development:** Trump, Xi and Putin held a first three-way meeting and were reported to endorse nuclear-risk dialogue, energy-supply cooperation, U.N. reform and limits on ICC jurisdiction.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The visual novelty can obscure the missing implementation layer.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A durable channel could reduce miscalculation and start successor arms-control work after New START. A leader-only declaration with no common public U.S. text, officials, timetable or verification mechanism instead gives each side diplomatic benefits without binding constraints.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The meeting occurred; AP reported the declaration through Chinese and Russian officials; the declaration omitted Ukraine and Iran; the United States had not released it by cutoff.
- **What remains inference:** Whether the three sides agreed on identical language, whether negotiations will start and whether any energy commitment changes supply.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/trump-xi-putin-nuclear-ukraine-iran-a04dc76ed8e786b087e2c1ea9598783a) supports the observable outcome and missing U.S. release.
- **Questions for auditor:** Was a negotiating mandate created? Which agencies own follow-up? Does the ICC language purport to change law or only state a political position?

## Candidate: Europe's Ukraine financing proposal is a burden-sharing test disguised as a headline number

- **Underlying development:** The European Commission proposed up to EUR 45 billion in additional 2027 borrowing, including EUR 25 billion for defense and EUR 20 billion for the state budget.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Reporting can merge the existing EUR 90 billion 2026-27 loan with the new EUR 45 billion proposal or treat a proposal as committed money.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Predictable financing sustains Ukraine's government and defense production through 2027. The package also shifts bargaining pressure onto reluctant EU states and non-EU partners asked to fill roughly one-third of the estimated gap.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The Commission adopted legislative proposals; Ukraine's estimated 2027 gap is EUR 70 billion; repayment is limited until Russian reparations; co-legislator approval is pending.
- **What remains inference:** Final member-state participation, adoption timing, borrowing cost and whether non-EU partners fill the balance.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [European Commission](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-proposes-eur45-billion-additional-financial-support-ukraine-2027-2026-11-18_en) is the primary proposal summary. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/europe-ukraine-loan-russia-summit-8c1baf00b55973a3534096aae2cc73a2) reports the political resistance.
- **Questions for auditor:** Map each approval threshold and opt-out. Keep the old and new EUR 45 billion tranches distinct.

## Candidate: Ethiopia-Eritrea escalation risk rests on force geometry, not a settled clash narrative

- **Underlying development:** Conflict monitors and militia-linked sources describe a force build-up that puts Ethiopian and Eritrean forces within striking distance near Badme; evidence about direct clashes conflicts.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Attention is concentrated on the Gulf and APEC, while the Horn could move from internal conflict to interstate war.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Dense forces near a politically loaded border shorten decision time. Ethiopian operations against Tigrayan rebels can draw Eritrean forces in even without either state choosing a general war.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Reuters recorded ACLED's November 12-13 incident claims, ICG's warning and a direct contradiction from a pro-government militia source.
- **What remains inference:** Force size, command intent, border crossings and whether the reported direct state clash occurred.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters republication](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Ethiopia%2Band%2BEritrea%2Bamass%2Bforces%2Balong%2Bborder,%2Bheightening%2Bwar%2Bfears,%2Csources%2Bsay/27366323.html) preserves the source conflict.
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek imagery, official military statements and independent local reporting; do not turn “within striking distance” into proof of offensive intent.

## Candidate: The U.S.-Venezuela accord may transform influence into institutional dependence

- **Underlying development:** Hegseth announced a signed agreement for joint training and intelligence sharing with Venezuela's interim authorities.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Coverage focuses on the January capture of Maduro and oil, while military institutional integration could last longer than either.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Training, intelligence systems and operational routines can create path dependence, shape leadership networks and give Washington leverage over Venezuelan internal security and regional policy.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Hegseth announced the signed deal and named two cooperation fields; the next presidential election has no date.
- **What remains inference:** Basing, deployments, operational authority, data access, duration and domestic legal approval.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/hegseth-venezuela-maduro-trump-e02dd6173e575c644a37dd417d84296d) provides the direct announcement and context.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain the text and signatories before describing an alliance or basing relationship.

## Candidate: Hormuz disruption is serious, but two denominators produce different headlines

- **Underlying development:** Kpler estimated strait-only crude flows at 8.5 million barrels per day, 50% of a prewar baseline, and total regional crude exports by all routes at 15 million barrels per day, 64% of that baseline.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** “Hormuz flows halved” is accurate only for one route definition and can imply a larger total export loss than the same source reports.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Alternative pipelines and a protected tanker route cushion supply while raising transport, insurance and transfer costs. That can weaken Iran's choke-point leverage without restoring prewar commerce.
- **What evidence is observable now:** AP reported Kpler's preliminary through-Monday estimates and cautioned that they could rise.
- **What remains inference:** Final November volumes, product-specific disruption, freight pass-through and the causal share attributable to Iranian attacks rather than commercial risk choices.
- **Initial priority:** medium-high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-news-iran-bahrain-us-november-18-2026-b71b1d25f3cff987b4b125196ecb4c3a) gives both numerators, baselines and the preliminary-data warning.
- **Questions for auditor:** Verify Kpler's prewar denominator and avoid combining strait-only and all-route percentages.

## Candidate: Treasury is applying secondary-sanctions leverage to Hezbollah's civilian cover and logistics

- **Underlying development:** OFAC designated a Lebanese charity and a gold-cash network it says support Hezbollah and IRGC-QF activity.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The legal action is small in headcount but touches the politically sensitive relief/militancy boundary and cross-border bullion finance.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Banks, insurers and traders may over-comply because secondary-sanctions exposure extends beyond U.S. persons, potentially isolating the network. Poorly calibrated enforcement could also deter legitimate aid.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The designations, legal authorities, named entities and U.S. allegations are public.
- **What remains inference:** Network scale, transaction volume, allied enforcement, humanitarian spillover and whether public evidence proves the allegations.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Sources:** [U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0692) is the controlling designation record.
- **Questions for auditor:** Separate the legal fact of designation from the government's factual allegations and predicted economic effect.

## Candidate: Myanmar's ASEAN threat may be a divide-and-bypass strategy

- **Underlying development:** Min Aung Hlaing said the government would review ASEAN ties while continuing discussions with individual members.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** “Myanmar may leave ASEAN” is more dramatic than the observed statement and may be wrong.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Bilateral engagement can weaken a consensus-based bloc's restrictions even if Myanmar never withdraws. Conversely, a real institutional downgrade would deprive Naypyitaw of a regional channel.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The review statement, continued bilateral-contact promise and ASEAN's renewed top-level restriction.
- **What remains inference:** Withdrawal, suspended dues, reduced attendance or other concrete consequences.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/min-aung-hlaing-asean-peace-plan-turmoil-97e863b842b3a3ad805635b11a7aea8c); [ASEAN review](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/FINAL-ASEAN-Leaders-Review-and-Decision-on-the-Implementation-of-the-Five-Point-Consensus-15-November-2026.pdf).
- **Questions for auditor:** Look for a cabinet, foreign-ministry or parliamentary instrument that operationalizes “review.”
