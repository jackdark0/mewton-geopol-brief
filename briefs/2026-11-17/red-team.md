# Adversarial review - 2026-11-17

**Scope:** `verified.md` and `analysis.md`, tested against the scout files, fixed cutoff, source policy, template, and the two separate Pangburn standards.

## Bottom line

The five-story slate is supportable if the editor preserves attribution and denominator limits. No source establishes renewed U.S. strike authorization, a sustained three-carrier baseline, Chinese coordination with the Russian flight, 1,336 actual interceptions, an adopted EU 22nd sanctions package, or an imminent Iranian homeland attack. Those are the principal overclaim traps.

The Serbian parliament item must not appear: its 10:00 CET opening was 04:00 America/New_York, after the fixed 02:00 cutoff. The Mozambique trial and COP31 draft target should also remain out because event timing or primary text was not secured.

## Story-level stress tests

### Three-carrier overlap

**Strongest challenge:** The headline can turn a momentary rotation overlap into a strategic buildup. The source base confirms presence, not duration, tasking, or strike intent.

**Required correction:** Use “three carriers overlap” or “temporary surge,” then immediately state that USNI expects *Theodore Roosevelt* to relieve *George Washington*. Do not call the three-carrier posture permanent or even sustained.

**Residual uncertainty:** The expected relief is informed reporting, not an official published schedule. Even a short overlap can be intentional, but the editor cannot assign motive.

**Verdict:** survives as a major story.

### BIS remote-compute rule

**Strongest challenge:** Secondary coverage incorrectly says the rule took effect immediately. This is a material legal error, not a stylistic quibble.

**Required correction:** Say announced/released November 16 and scheduled to take effect on Federal Register publication November 19. Keep the 90-day transition attached to existing providers. Do not convert 12,000,000 TPP into a chip count without a source.

**Residual uncertainty:** The final published notice could correct the public-inspection copy, but that had not happened by cutoff. Provider compliance and enforcement effects are wholly prospective.

**Verdict:** survives as a major story and should carry the correction prominently.

### Japanese airspace violation

**Strongest challenge:** The regional route invites a dramatic “Russia and China probe allied defenses” narrative that the evidence does not support. ADIZ activity is also easily mislabeled as sovereign-airspace violation.

**Required correction:** Attribute the territorial-airspace finding to Japan; call the aircraft “suspected Russian”; separate Japan's airspace from South Korean and Taiwanese ADIZ reporting; say the inspected record does not prove a common formation or Chinese coordination.

**Residual uncertainty:** No Russian account, aircraft type, or mission evidence was found.

**Verdict:** survives as a major story.

### EU Ukraine package

**Strongest challenge:** Three separate actions can be collapsed into a nonexistent “new sanctions package,” and approval can be mislabeled as cash already transferred.

**Required correction:** Separate (1) just under EUR 8 billion authorized under the Ukraine Facility, (2) 113 listings, and (3) 30 people plus two entities under FIMI/cyber regimes. Say the 22nd package was requested, not adopted. Say the money was approved for receipt “soon,” not fully settled by cutoff.

**Residual uncertainty:** Economic effect depends on exposed assets and third-country compliance. “Largest-ever” must remain scoped to one Ukraine Facility disbursement.

**Verdict:** survives as a major story.

### Ukraine drone wave and Kerch Bridge

**Strongest challenge:** The 1,336 figure is a single-source belligerent claim with no complete launch denominator. It can make the brief look precise while being the least certain number in it.

**Required correction:** Lead with the observed outcome—bridge damage and a temporary road halt. Put “Russia claimed” directly before 1,336 and state that AP could not verify it. Do not calculate an interception rate or cost ratio. Exclude later November 17 reports of a second strike or collapsed span.

**Residual uncertainty:** AP's observable-effect reporting is stronger than the numerical claim but still does not furnish a full independent battle-damage assessment.

**Verdict:** survives as a major story with medium confidence and restrained language.
