## Claim: The new BIS remote-compute rule took effect immediately on November 16

- **Origin / principal promoters:** AP's November 16 report said the rule “took effect immediately”; some derivative coverage repeated that formulation.
- **Why it matters:** Effective date controls when exporters and data centers face legal obligations, when the 90-day transition begins, and whether conduct on November 16-18 falls under the new rule.
- **Evidence offered for it:** BIS announced the rule on November 16 and described it as an interim final rule.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The public-inspection text says the rule is effective on the date of Federal Register publication. The notice says it is scheduled for publication on November 19, 2026. The “INSERT DATE” placeholders remained in the pre-publication copy, but the scheduling banner fixes the expected publication date.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for the contrary evidence; the Federal Register public-inspection text is the controlling primary document.
- **Current assessment:** false as stated. Announced November 16; scheduled to become effective on publication November 19.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:**
  - https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf - inspected primary rule, dates, and transition provisions.
  - https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-issues-rule-addressing-remote-access-american-super-intelligence-technology - inspected announcement.
  - https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-export-controls-chips-china-remote-access-60f868d3bb430b85a8657b27d421096e - inspected AP wording.
- **Questions for auditor:** Confirm the document was not published early under a separate Federal Register record. Cite the final Federal Register URL if it exists by audit time, without moving the research cutoff.

## Claim: Three U.S. carriers in the Middle East establish a sustained third-carrier buildup or a decision to resume strikes

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Dramatic headline and social-media interpretations of USS *Theodore Roosevelt* entering CENTCOM.
- **Why it matters:** A sustained third carrier would represent a major global force-posture choice and stronger escalation signal than a short relief overlap.
- **Evidence offered for it:** CENTCOM confirmed the *Roosevelt* arrival. AP counted three carrier groups and a large escort force. Trump has not ruled out renewed strikes.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** USNI reports the *Roosevelt* is expected to relieve USS *George Washington*. CENTCOM would not say whether all three will stay. No inspected order authorized renewed large-scale strikes or established the duration of the overlap.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial. Multiple credible sources confirm three carriers at cutoff; the sustained-posture and strike-decision propositions are not independently established.
- **Current assessment:** qualified / misleading. Three were present at cutoff; persistence and strike intent are unclear.
- **Confidence:** high on the correction; medium on the expected rotation because no public Navy schedule was inspected.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/us-iran-war-aircraft-carrier-middle-east-9858fc82c61c7a1d159e7bba72afe99a - inspected theater count and policy context.
  - https://news.usni.org/2026/11/16/uss-theodore-roosevelt-now-in-the-middle-east-lincolns-last-escorts-finally-home - inspected expected-relief report.
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek an official departure or relief statement. Do not infer a strike order from capability alone.

## Claim: Russia shot down 1,336 Ukrainian drones in one overnight attack

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Russian Defense Ministry, repeated by AP with an explicit verification caveat.
- **Why it matters:** If accurate, it would be the largest reported drone attack/interception event of the war and a major indicator of Ukrainian production and Russian defensive load.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Russia's military published the number; extensive regional alerts and a damaged Kerch Bridge establish a large attack.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No complete launch denominator, weapon-type breakdown, radar record, debris inventory, or independent geolocation set supports 1,336 successful interceptions. Russian statements did not say how many weapons got through. Kerch damage shows at least some leakage.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for the exact total; yes for a very large attack and some effects.
- **Current assessment:** claimed / unclear.
- **Confidence:** high that the exact count is unverified; low on the true count.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-drones-air-defenses-f6eb7059bcaf5097f89f3665a823ee63 - inspected report and caveat.
- **Questions for auditor:** Locate the original Russian releases, avoid summing overlapping reporting periods, and freeze evidence before later November 17 strikes.

## Claim: Iran has decided to detonate a dirty bomb or use a chemical weapon in Washington

- **Origin / principal promoters:** CNN reported new U.S. intelligence based on seven U.S. officials; headline and political discussion can collapse “developing options” into a decided or imminent attack.
- **Why it matters:** The allegation could affect U.S. retaliation, domestic security, and the risk of rapid escalation with Iran.
- **Evidence offered for it:** CNN's sourced report says intelligence indicates Iranian planning involving specific locations and targets, and U.S. officials held contingency meetings. An October 23 declassified DNI memo independently says Iranian lethal plotting against U.S. personnel had reached advanced stages in the Middle East and “certain other countries,” but it does not mention Washington, dirty bombs, or chemical weapons.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** CNN's own sources described the work as option development, not evidence of an imminent attack or a decision to execute. The White House said there were no credible threats to the homeland at that time. ODNI declined comment. Iran denied chemical-weapon allegations and did not directly answer the dirty-bomb claim. No intelligence, material acquisition, operative, or executive order is public.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for a broader rise in Iranian lethal plotting; no independent public corroboration of the specific Washington dirty-bomb/chemical plan.
- **Current assessment:** plausible as an attributed U.S.-intelligence concern; unsupported as a decided or imminent Iranian attack.
- **Confidence:** medium on the existence of an intelligence concern; low on the underlying plan and Iranian command decision.
- **Sources:**
  - https://d3nqdp0e3r32g8.cloudfront.net/news/us-intel-indicates-iran-developing-plans-to-potentially-detonate-a-dirty-bomb-in-washington-sources-say - inspected CNN-syndicated full report.
  - https://www.wlbt.com/2026/11/17/us-monitoring-reported-iran-dirty-bomb-plans-white-house-says-no-credible-threats/ - inspected CNN Newsource summary with White House statement.
  - https://www.iranwatch.org/sites/default/files/dni-memo-to-dos-20261023.pdf - inspected declassified October 23 DNI memo; supports only broader plotting.
  - https://apnews.com/article/trump-asia-manila-asean-xi-putin-c94386cd092482d94dca38fc8b8d51a7 - inspected Trump's conditional public response.
- **Questions for auditor:** Preserve every modal: “indicates,” “developing plans,” “potentially,” “options,” and “no credible threats.” Check whether the White House statement temporally followed the same intelligence. Do not treat seven anonymous officials as seven independent evidence streams.
