## Candidate: Third U.S. aircraft carrier enters the Middle East

- **Event time/date:** Announced 2026-11-16.
- **Region / actors:** United States, Iran, Gulf states.
- **What changed:** CENTCOM announced that the USS *Theodore Roosevelt* carrier strike group had entered the Middle East, joining the USS *George H.W. Bush* and USS *George Washington*. AP reported 13 destroyers, two cruisers, and a littoral combat ship in the Arabian Sea, with two more destroyers in the Red Sea. Three simultaneous carrier groups in the theater are rare.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - Increases U.S. strike, air-defense, surveillance, and blockade capacity while negotiations remain stalled.
  - Makes Trump's description of the war as militarily “effectively over” sit beside a large, observable force buildup.
  - Creates an escalation signal even if one carrier later rotates out; the duration of the three-carrier overlap is not yet established.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** CENTCOM social-media announcement is the primary origin; AP adds independently compiled force-composition context but relies on that announcement for arrival.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/us-iran-war-aircraft-carrier-middle-east-9858fc82c61c7a1d159e7bba72afe99a - inspected AP report with the CENTCOM announcement, ship count, and context.
- **Questions for auditor:** Is this a net long-duration force increase or a short overlap before a carrier rotation? Is an official carrier-departure schedule available? What forces are actually inside the CENTCOM area at cutoff?

## Candidate: U.S. closes the remote-compute gap in AI-chip export controls

Which compliance duties attach to exporters versus data-center operators? Is the “effective immediately” language subject to a comment or delayed-compliance period?

## Candidate: Ukraine launches the largest drone wave Russia says it has faced

- **Event time/date:** Overnight 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-16.
- **Region / actors:** Ukraine, Russia, occupied Crimea.
- **What changed:** Russia's military said it shot down 1,336 Ukrainian drones, its largest claimed interception total of the war. AP could not independently verify the number. A Ukrainian drone damaged the Kerch Bridge and temporarily disrupted road traffic, an observable effect despite the claimed interception scale.

  - Demonstrates Ukraine's expanding capacity for mass long-range saturation attacks.
  - Forces Russia to spread air defenses across strategic infrastructure and a vast territory.
  - The gap between claimed interceptions and verified impacts is itself important; neither launch total nor interception rate is established.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high for a very large attack and Kerch disruption; low-medium for 1,336 interceptions.
- **Source chain:** The 1,336 figure comes from Russia's military; AP independently reports observable effects and explicitly says the number is unverified.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-drones-air-defenses-f6eb7059bcaf5097f89f3665a823ee63 - inspected AP report.
- **Questions for auditor:** Find Russian Defense Ministry release(s) and Ukrainian operational statements. What launch-period denominator produced 1,336? How many impacts are independently geolocated? What damage to the Kerch Bridge was established by cutoff, before later second-day strikes?

## Candidate: Suspected Russian aircraft violate Japanese airspace

- **Event time/date:** 2026-11-17 04:07-04:12 Japan Standard Time, within the cutoff.
- **Region / actors:** Russia, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan.

- **Confidence in basic event:** high for Japan's detection and response; medium on aircraft identity pending Russian acknowledgment.
- **Source chain:** Japan's Defense Ministry is primary for the airspace finding and route; AP adds statements from Japanese, South Korean, and Taiwanese authorities.
