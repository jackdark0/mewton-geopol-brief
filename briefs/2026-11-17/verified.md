## Story: BIS extends advanced-compute controls to remote access

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED | high confidence]** The Bureau of Industry and Security released an interim final rule that creates a worldwide end-use restriction for covered advanced-computing items knowingly destined for data centers that provide more than 12,000,000 aggregate total processing performance, at one site or digitally linked sites, to or for companies headquartered or ultimately parented in Macau or Country Group D:5. [Federal Register public-inspection rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf)
- **[CONFIRMED | high confidence]** The rule creates destination-based licensing requirements involving Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil, while providing a reporting-based exclusion for certain U.S.-headquartered end users. [Federal Register public-inspection rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf)
- **[CONFIRMED | high confidence]** It adds ownership, control, end-use, indirect-access, and reporting duties intended to reach remote use and circumvention through third-country data centers. [Federal Register public-inspection rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf) and [BIS release](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-issues-rule-addressing-remote-access-american-super-intelligence-technology)
- **[FALSE AS STATED | high confidence]** The rule did **not** take effect immediately on November 16. The controlling public-inspection text says it becomes effective on publication, scheduled for November 19; existing providers then receive a 90-day licensing period. AP's “took effect immediately” wording conflicts with the primary legal text. [Federal Register public-inspection rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf) and [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-export-controls-chips-china-remote-access-60f868d3bb430b85a8657b27d421096e)
- **[CONFIRMED | high confidence]** The rule rescinds the previous AI Diffusion Rule and includes an exception for some commercial recommendation-system uses. [Federal Register public-inspection rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf)

### Material claims not established

- The share of Chinese or other D:5 training capacity that will be blocked.
- An Nvidia-equivalent chip count for the 12,000,000-TPP threshold.
- That beneficial ownership and indirect access can be detected reliably.
- The magnitude of compliance cost, evasion, retaliation, or third-country alignment.

### Source provenance

- The Federal Register public-inspection copy controls legal timing and scope. The BIS release explains intent. AP supplies reactions and commercial context but is not controlling on effective date.

### Open questions

- How will BIS test corporate control and digitally linked capacity in practice?
- Which providers seek licenses during the 90-day transition?
- Will the final text or implementation guidance narrow the commercial exception?

### Recommendation

- **include major**; emphasize the shift from controlling shipped hardware to controlling access to compute, and state the November 19 effective date.
