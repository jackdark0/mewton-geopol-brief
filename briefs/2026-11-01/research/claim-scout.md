## C01 — Claim: Rezaei said Iran was debating its nuclear doctrine but had taken no new decision

- **Atomic proposition / type:** Attribution and policy-status claim: on October 31, Iran's security chief publicly described a conditional debate about retaining the nuclear-weapons prohibition if war resumes, while denying that a new state decision had been made. This does not establish a weapons order, a deployed capability, withdrawal from the NPT, or independently observable internal deliberations.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Mohsen Rezaei's IRIB television interview, reported by Reuters and Rudaw. Attribution confidence: high for the reported speaker, medium for translation/context without original audio.
- **Why it matters:** It is a consequential escalation signal within active mediation. Calling it an adopted weapons policy would cross several unsupported inferential steps.
- **Evidence offered for it:** [Reuters via Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913416), article body paragraphs 1–4, reports both the debate and absence of a decision. [Rudaw](https://rudaw.net/english/categories/iran/1079969), nuclear-posture paragraphs, reproduces the same qualification while identifying IRIB as the originating interview.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No full Persian transcript, original interview audio, authenticated policy instrument, or independent evidence of internal decision-making was retrieved. Both reporters may be translating the same broadcast. A denial of a new decision cannot prove the complete absence of secret action. Conversely, a debate is not proof of adoption.
- **Independent corroboration:** **Partial** for the public statement; **no** for the underlying internal debate or absence of a secret decision. Separate outlets are not separate witnesses to the internal process.
- **Current assessment:** **Supported as an attributed public statement; unclear as independently established policy-process fact.** A completed-policy-change reading is unsupported by this packet. Preserve the true core: the conditional debate itself is significant.
- **Confidence:** **Medium**, because the original-language record is missing and the accounts share a broadcast origin. High confidence that the inspected reports retain the no-decision qualification.
- **Sources / provenance / timestamps:** Reuters/JPost displays **2026-10-31 21:44**, zone not printed; it is safely before cutoff under either Israel or UTC interpretation. Rudaw displays **2026-10-31**, no clock/zone; date-only October 31 remains pre-cutoff under its apparent regional publication setting, but auditor should confirm metadata. **Lineage NUC-IRIB:** IRIB interview → translations/reporting; not independent proof of the debated policy. A [Press TV primary-adjacent page](https://www.presstv.media/Detail/2026/10/31/779646/irans-security-chief-us-tone-has-completely-changed-no-decision-yet-on-nuclear-doctrine) was discovered but **two opens timed out**; its search text is not evidence here.
- **Denominator / scope:** No quantity or polling inference. No claim about public support is carried from the interview.
- **Questions for auditor:** Retrieve the original Persian passage and surrounding question; distinguish nuclear doctrine, fatwa, NPT membership, and weapons acquisition. Was any operative decision independently documented before 07:00Z? Establish broadcast and page UTC times.
- **Publication-safe correction / uncertainty:** Rezaei publicly raised a conditional reconsideration of doctrine and said no new decision had been made; the desk has not independently verified Iran's internal deliberations. **Status:** discovery packet, primary/audio retrieval hold.
