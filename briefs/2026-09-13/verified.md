## Method note

The propositions below are atomic: attack, launch location, perpetrator, command, territorial possession, operational control, statement, and implementation are treated separately. Official records establish what an institution announced or did; they do not automatically verify the institution's account of contested facts. Verdict and confidence are separate. No rate or percentage is calculated from a selected claim set.

## Story: Baghdad traces the Saudi pipeline attack to Maysan and removes commanders

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** Saudi Arabia said its East-West pipeline was attacked on September 10 and shut as a precaution. The Saudi Energy Ministry did not identify a perpetrator in the first statement. [Saudi Energy Ministry via SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2674017); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-yemen-iran-saudi-houthis-476237dd3bf568d946d2b16c7b45a687)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry later alleged that several drones launched from Iraq struck pipeline sites in the Riyadh and Madinah regions, causing injuries and damage. This is a Saudi attribution, not independent proof. [Saudi Foreign Ministry via SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2674116)
- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate confidence]** Iraq said its authorities determined that the attacks originated from a site in Maysan province; the prime minister dismissed the Maysan operations commander, later removed the provincial police chief, and referred security officials for investigation. The personnel actions are directly documented; the evidentiary basis for the launch-site finding is not public. [Iraqi News Agency record](https://video-agencyia.iq/EN/Detail/4255); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-yemen-iran-saudi-houthis-476237dd3bf568d946d2b16c7b45a687)

### Material claims not established

- Which group launched the drones; whether an Iraqi militia conducted the attack; whether Iran supplied, ordered, approved, or directed it; the exact damage and repair time; and whether the pipeline had resumed operation by the cutoff.

### Source provenance

- SPA is the primary Saudi record for the shutdown and Saudi accusation. The Iraqi record is a participant-side account of Baghdad's investigation and personnel actions. AP independently reported both governments' accounts but did not supply a separate physical attribution stream.

### Open questions

- Will Baghdad publish launch-site evidence, identify suspects, or prosecute commanders? Will Riyadh distinguish launch location, perpetrator, sponsor, and command responsibility? How long does the pipeline remain constrained?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Lead on the cross-border sovereignty and export-route change, not an unproved Iran attribution.

## Story: Houthis seize Perim/Mayun after Mokha

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate-to-high confidence]** AP reported that a senior official from Yemen's internationally recognized government and a Houthi official separately confirmed the Houthi seizure of Mayun, also called Perim, on September 11. Reuters separately reported the arrival through multiple Yemeni-government sources. The two reporting efforts are independent, but their evidence remains participant-source reporting rather than public imagery. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-yemen-iran-saudi-houthis-476237dd3bf568d946d2b16c7b45a687); [Reuters](https://ca.investing.com/news/commodities-news/yemens-houthis-reach-strategic-island-at-mouth-of-vital-shipping-lane-4836948)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** Houthi spokesperson Yahya Saree announced that navigation was safe for all companies except Saudi vessels. This confirms the declared policy only. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-yemen-iran-saudi-houthis-476237dd3bf568d946d2b16c7b45a687); [Reuters](https://ca.investing.com/news/commodities-news/yemens-houthis-reach-strategic-island-at-mouth-of-vital-shipping-lane-4836948)
- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate confidence]** The island seizure followed the reported capture of Mokha and expands the Houthis' positions at the entrance to the Red Sea. That changes capacity to threaten or selectively interdict shipping; it does not establish effective control over every navigation lane. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-houthis-iran-strait-mokha-shipping-ab2e69eb2959979bae44eb4d45ae637e)

### Material claims not established

- That the Houthis have closed or fully control Bab el-Mandeb; that non-Saudi shipping is safe; control of every relevant island, shore position, or lane; and the practical effect on traffic, insurance, or rerouting before the cutoff.
- Reuters reported allegations of direct IRGC guidance. Those allegations remain inside one anonymous-source reporting chain; no public command record or independent evidentiary stream was recovered.

### Source provenance

- AP and Reuters are separate reporting efforts, but both depend heavily on conflict participants. The Houthi safety assurance is one participant statement, not corroboration of actual safety.

### Open questions

- Who holds the island's facilities and both traffic approaches? Do UKMTO/JMIC alerts, ship tracks, attacks, or insurers show that positional leverage became operational disruption?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Describe a second territorial step and greater coercive capacity; reject shorthand that the strait is already closed.

## Story: North Korea fires several short-range ballistic missiles after Freedom Edge

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[SUPPORTED | High confidence]** South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said it detected multiple short-range ballistic missiles launched from the Wonsan area at about 05:20 KST on September 12; it reported flights of about 250 kilometres into the sea. Yonhap and AP both carry this same JCS sensor lineage, so they are not two independent detections. [Yonhap](https://m-en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20260912000353315?section=national%2Fdefense); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/north-south-korea-projectile-fired-ccf51bb9cd9abfda6c0b0f36c4b639e1)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** The five-day U.S.-South Korea-Japan Freedom Edge exercise concluded September 11. The U.S. Pacific Fleet described training in air, maritime, missile-defence, cyber, medical, command-and-control, and real-time information-sharing functions. This is a participant account of exercise activity and purpose. [U.S. Pacific Fleet](https://www.cpf.navy.mil/Newsroom/News/Article/4599379/freedom-edge-26-concludes-advancing-trilateral-readiness-across-indo-pacific/)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** North Korea's defence minister had threatened “reflective countermeasures” before the launch, as recorded by Yonhap. This makes the sequence relevant but does not prove the launch's purpose.

### Material claims not established

- Exact missile count, model, payload, technical performance, operational objective, or a causal finding that Freedom Edge triggered the launch. Yonhap's Hwasong-11/600mm assessment is attributed to unnamed sources, not the JCS.

### Source provenance

- The launch facts share a JCS origin. The U.S. Navy record is independent only for the exercise, not for the North Korean launch. North Korea had not supplied an inspected technical account by the cutoff.

### Open questions

- Will Japan publish an independent detection record? Will Pyongyang identify the systems or purpose? Did the launch test a new configuration or repeat a familiar signalling pattern?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Focus on the quick exercise-response cycle and allied detection sharing, not an alleged new capability.

### Open questions

## Story: Niger replaces its armed-forces chief after the August mutiny

**Priority:** medium

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** Niger's state news agency reported decrees dated September 11 appointing Brig. Gen. Mamane Sani Kiaou as armed-forces chief of staff, replacing Gen. Moussa Salaou Barmou, and changing other senior posts. [Agence Nigérienne de Presse](https://anp.ne/niger-le-president-tiani-renouvelle-le-commandement-militaire/)
- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate confidence]** AP places the reshuffle less than two weeks after an August 28-29 mutiny at Base 101 and reports that the Defence Ministry described soldiers taking hostages and firing at sensitive sites. The decree gave no reason, so a causal claim that the mutiny directly caused the reshuffle remains an inference. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/12ecef2a363e00f825274ca712ef948b)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** Kiaou had previously served as army chief; the change is a top-command turnover, not simply a renamed post.

### Material claims not established

- The government's rationale; whether the mutiny reflected a wider coup network; whether Russia's Africa Corps or the Niger-Mali-Burkina Faso force took part; or whether the reshuffle improves regime or battlefield stability.

### Source provenance

- ANP is the state primary record for the appointment. AP independently contextualizes the mutiny but relies partly on Niger's Defence Ministry for incident details.

### Open questions

- Do further arrests, promotions, deployments, or defections show that the reshuffle consolidated control or exposed deeper military division?

### Recommendation

- **Include major if space permits.** It is a meaningful regime-stability signal; keep causal and external-actor claims qualified.
