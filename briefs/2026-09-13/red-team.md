## Finding: “Baghdad traced” can sound more conclusive than the record

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** Iraq-Saudi pipeline story; launch-site and accountability analysis.
- **Problem:** Iraq's public claim that authorities determined a Maysan launch site is not accompanied by radar, debris, imagery, flight paths, arrests, or a disclosed method. Calling the location “documented” without attribution can let an official conclusion masquerade as independently verified physical evidence.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** The Iraqi personnel actions are directly documented. The origin finding remains a government account also repeated by AP. Saudi Arabia separately alleged Iraqi origin; those accounts may converge, but they are not independent forensic streams.
- **Best competing interpretation:** Baghdad may be acting quickly to manage relations with Riyadh or punish a security failure while the exact launch evidence remains preliminary.
- **Required editorial action:** qualify. State that Baghdad said its authorities located the origin in Maysan; reserve “confirmed” for the dismissals, investigation, Saudi shutdown, and official accusations. Do not name a militia or Iran.
