## Candidate: Iraq confirms Saudi pipeline attacks originated from its territory

- **Event time/date:** Iraqi government action reported early 2026-09-12 EDT (AP published 2026-09-12 06:59:44 UTC; underlying attack was 2026-09-10, outside the window, while Iraq's confirmation and command action are inside it).
- **Region / actors:** Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Iran; Iraqi government; Saudi Energy and Foreign Ministries.
- **What changed:** Iraq said the attacks on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline were launched from a Maysan Province site, ordered an investigation into the local operations command, and dismissed its commander. Saudi Arabia had already shut the pipeline as a precaution; its September 12 statement said multiple drones launched from Iraq caused injuries and damage. This establishes a cross-border provenance finding by Baghdad and a Saudi legal/diplomatic accusation, not Iranian direction of the attack.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It turns a Saudi infrastructure strike into an Iraq-sovereignty and militia-control problem at a time the pipeline is a bypass for disrupted Hormuz traffic.
  - The Iraqi action may create pressure for militia accountability, Saudi restraint, or wider escalation; none is yet demonstrated.
  - A source-location finding is not an attribution of command, sponsorship, or Iranian responsibility.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high for Saudi shutdown and the Iraqi government's stated investigation/dismissal; medium for the precise origin finding; low for responsibility beyond the launch site.
- **Source chain:** Saudi Press Agency is the participant/official record of Saudi claims and shutdown. AP independently reports the Iraqi government's stated action, but its Iraq account is still official-source-derived. Trump's “probably” Iran attribution is a separate political claim, not corroboration.
- **Sources:**
  - [Saudi Ministry of Energy statement via SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2674017) — official record that the pipeline was attacked on September 10 and shut as a precaution.
  - [Saudi Foreign Ministry statement via SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2674116) — official claim that drones were launched from Iraq and caused injuries/damage.
  - [AP, September 12](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-yemen-iran-saudi-houthis-12-september-2026-324485fad65647d4a897d5bfdcb7849c) — reports Iraq's stated territorial-origin finding and command dismissal; separately records Trump's unproven Iran attribution.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain Baghdad's underlying release; establish whether the pipeline remains shut, what equipment was hit, whether launch-origin evidence is public, and whether Iraq attributes a perpetrator rather than only a location.
