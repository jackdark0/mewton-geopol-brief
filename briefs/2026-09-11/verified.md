## Story: Houthi forces took Mokha; the port seizure adds pressure on Red Sea approaches

**Priority:** High  
**Recommendation:** Include major.

### Verified propositions

- **SUPPORTED — High confidence:** Houthi forces took the Yemeni Red Sea port city of Mokha on September 10. AP reported the takeover from Houthi and government-aligned sources and residents. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-iran-houthi-rebels-red-sea-mokha-e4e799701b382799a955969c212800ea)
- **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** The UN said eight UN vehicles were seized from its humanitarian hub in Mokha that morning. [UN briefing](https://www.un.org/sg/fr/node/401528)
- **SUPPORTED — High confidence:** Mokha is strategically relevant to the Bab el-Mandeb approaches; that does not establish operational control of the strait.

### Material claims not established

- **CLAIMED:** Iranian officers directly commanded the offensive. Reuters reported confidential-source allegations, but no orders, intercepts, travel record, or other inspectable evidence was available.
- **UNSUPPORTED:** Mokha's capture means the Houthis now control Bab el-Mandeb, its navigation lanes, or both shores. The territorial result supports increased leverage and risk, not completed chokepoint control.

### Source provenance

- AP's local-source reporting and the UN's vehicle report are independent only for their respective propositions. Reuters republications about Iranian direction are one confidential-source lineage.

### Open questions

- Who controls Mokha's port facilities, nearby islands, Dhubab, and Perim at the cutoff?
- Does the takeover change actual transit, insurance, or attacks on shipping after September 10?
