## Claim C1: US negotiators threatened to withdraw intelligence sharing unless Ukraine stopped attacks on Russian refineries

- **Type / atomic scope:** CLAIM about a conditional threat during negotiations, not a claim that assistance actually stopped. Working wording is a paraphrase, not a quotation.
- **Event clock:** Talks preceding the Oct 9 diesel announcement; precise meeting time and proposed intelligence scope require verification. Source clocks: S1–S3 above.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Anonymous Ukrainian participants/officials in original reporting; social accounts and Townhall amplify versions of the allegation.
- **Why it matters:** Such a condition would change Ukraine's leverage and expose tension between an alliance's security objectives and US fuel-price policy.
- **Evidence offered for it:** S2 identifies an interviewee as present at the talks. That improves proximity, but anonymity prevents checking access and incentives. The allegation concerns intelligence useful for targeting, which should not silently become “all aid.”
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** There is a contrary US account in S1. No transcript, contemporaneous written condition, named participant confirmation, or documented interruption was inspected. Anonymous-source overlap is unknown. A threat, discussion, negotiated condition, and implemented cutoff must be tested separately.
- **Independent corroboration:** **unknown**. Multiple publications are not proof of separate witnesses; S3 is explicitly derivative.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear**. **Publication gate: HOLD** pending version-clock validation and stronger event evidence.
- **Confidence:** **low** in the underlying threat proposition; higher confidence only that a consequential dispute exists in inspected reporting.
- **Sources:** S1 ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/10/trump-zelensky-putin-diesel-deal-war)); S2 ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/exclusive-it-felt-like-a-trap-how-ukraine-was-blindsided-by-trumps-putin-pivot/)); S3 ([Townhall](https://townhall.com/news/josephchalfant/2026/10/10/the-trump-administration-is-drawing-this-red-line-with-zelensky-n2684466)). Entailment and clocks are in the register.
- **Source-chain / adversarial caution:** Original anonymous interviews → outlet story → social paraphrase → commentary. Amplification can strip the denial and widen “targeting intelligence” into “US support.” Neither side's negotiating incentives settle the facts.
- **Questions for auditor:** Validate exact pre-cutoff versions; retrieve the original FT report rather than its social summary; identify source overlap; distinguish intelligence categories and proposed versus implemented action. Seek primary US/Ukrainian records.
- **Safe treatment if unresolved:** Present a disputed allegation, with the denial adjacent; do not state an intelligence cutoff occurred.

## Claim C2: Ukrainian refinery attacks explain the high US diesel price that the Russian diesel arrangement is intended to address

- **Type / atomic scope:** Causal CLAIM, scoped to explanation of the US diesel-price problem. Do not strengthen it into an exact percentage or exclusive cause without a source.
- **Event clock:** Trump's remarks and market dispute reported Oct 10; S5 estimates concern September exports, not the day's delivered supply.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Trump, as reported by Reuters; favorable commentary treats the arrangement as a price remedy.
- **Why it matters:** An economic explanation is being used to justify a consequential change in sanctions and pressure on a wartime partner.
- **Evidence offered for it:** S5 estimates September diesel/gasoil exports at 144,000 barrels/day, down 28% month-on-month, and links constraints partly to refinery attacks. This supports a possible supply channel, not a measured US price contribution.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** S4 also identifies the Iran war as a major market context and reports analysts doubting sustained relief from the deal. No counterfactual US price model, refinery-capacity bridge, substitution estimate, or delivered-US-volume record was inspected.
- **Independent corroboration:** **partial** for a supply mechanism; **no** for a quantified causal share of US prices.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear** for the broad price attribution. The narrower proposition that refinery disruption can constrain supply is **plausible**, but cannot carry the broader conclusion by itself.
- **Confidence:** **medium** in the evidentiary limitation; **low** in any claimed share of US price changes.
- **Sources:** [Reuters / S4](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trump-calls-for-replacing-ukraine-s-zelenskiy-in-tussle-over-diesel-deal-with-russia-ce785ddcdd80f523) — identifies political blame and competing explanations; [Bloomberg–Vortexa / S5](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/russias_oil_product_exports_rise-10-oct-2026-184813-article/) — estimates an export constraint; [OFAC / S6](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937216/download?inline=) — establishes permission, not a price effect.
- **Source-chain / denominator caution:** Vortexa → Bloomberg → Rigzone is one estimate. The 1.17 million barrels/day figure is **all reported refined products**, not diesel/gasoil. Do not substitute it for 144,000, turn monthly exports into US deliveries, or equate a regulatory waiver with incremental output.
- **Questions for auditor:** Inspect primary US price/stock data and the agreement's actual contracted supply; establish baseline, unit, period, destination, incremental versus diverted barrels, and the causal bridge. Keep estimates separate from observations.
- **Safe treatment if unresolved:** The claimed benefit is uncertain; refinery damage is one possible supply factor, not a demonstrated explanation of the entire price problem.

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Saudi military spokesman Turki Al-Maliki; US official statements reinforce the attribution.

- **Evidence offered for it:** S9 relays Al-Maliki's named attribution. Its 20:30 EDT entry reports Rubio's statement that an American was killed. Those are official assertions, not two forensic investigations.

- **Independent corroboration:** **partial** for the incident; **no** independently inspected forensic attribution.
- **Current assessment:** **plausible**, as a named official attribution with an unresolved evidentiary gap, not merely because disproof is difficult.
- **Confidence:** **medium** in the attribution candidate; do not imply confidence in a settled forensic finding.
- **Sources:** [ABC / S9](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-trump-us-attack-iran-ahead/?id=137063637) — pre-cutoff Saudi and US statements; [Reuters / S8](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Houthi%2Battack%2Bon%2BSaudi%2Bairport%2Bkills%2B12,%2Bover%2B300%2Bwounded,%2Bauthorities%2Bsay/27172684.html) — reports responsibility caveats but later-body timing is on hold.

- **Event clock:** Oct 10 operation in the Gulf of Oman, reported by ABC at 21:19 EDT and Reuters at 21:35 EDT.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** CENTCOM.
- **Why it matters:** The distinction between disabling propulsion without injury and a lethal attack affects escalation, accountability, and public interpretation of enforcement.
- **Evidence offered for it:** S9 and S10 relay CENTCOM's account of warnings and propulsion-disabling fire. The “unharmed crew” assurance comes from the military conducting the operation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** S10 reports no available operator comment. No medical record, crew interview, independent ship inspection, or independently authenticated damage assessment was inspected. An official handout image cannot establish every person's condition.
- **Independent corroboration:** **no** for the no-harm proposition.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear**. Lack of independent confirmation is not evidence of injury or proof that the assurance is false.
- **Confidence:** **low** in the complete no-harm claim; **high** that this is the official account reported in pre-cutoff sources.
- **Sources:** [Reuters / S10](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/US%2Bmilitary%2Bsays%2Bit%2Bdisabled%2Bcargo%2Bvessel%2Btrying%2Bto%2Brun%2BIran%2Bblockade/27172764.html) — attributes the outcome and documents missing operator comment; [ABC / S9](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-trump-us-attack-iran-ahead/?id=137063637) — relays the military account.

## Claim C6: The Oct 9 Russian diesel waiver emboldened Russia to attack Ukraine on Oct 10

- **Type / atomic scope:** ASSESSMENT of a causal effect on Russian behavior; not a factual inference established by the ordering of two events.
- **Event clock:** Waiver dated Oct 9; attack and reaction reported Oct 10. S4 version updated at 22:10 EDT, before cutoff.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Zelensky, as reported by Reuters.
- **Why it matters:** The argument concerns whether economic relief undermines deterrence and encourages escalation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The waiver and later attack occurred in that sequence. S4 reports Zelensky's causal interpretation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** S4 also reports earlier mass-casualty attacks and an existing bombardment pattern. No inspected Russian decision record, planning timeline, changed target selection, or counterfactual comparison ties this operation to the waiver. An attack can have been planned before a policy announcement.
- **Independent corroboration:** **no** for the causal mechanism; occurrence of the policy and attacks does not independently verify motive.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear**.
- **Confidence:** **low** in causation; **medium** that the hypothesis merits strategic discussion rather than factual narration.
- **Sources:** [Reuters / S4](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trump-calls-for-replacing-ukraine-s-zelenskiy-in-tussle-over-diesel-deal-with-russia-ce785ddcdd80f523) — records the argument and preceding attack context; [OFAC / S6](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937216/download?inline=) — establishes the waiver itself, not its behavioral consequence.
- **Source-chain / inference bridge:** Legal text + attack reporting → political causal interpretation. The arrow is the disputed part. Neither the policy's desirability nor sympathy for the victims fills that evidence gap.
- **Questions for auditor:** Check planning and launch chronology, changes relative to the previous attack pattern, and any primary Russian response. What observable result would distinguish emboldenment from continued previously planned operations?
- **Safe treatment if unresolved:** “Zelensky argues the waiver emboldened Moscow”; frame strategic plausibility as analysis, not proven causation.

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Mahgoub al-Sheikh, governor of Wad al-Mahi, in AFP reporting.

S11 mentions Ethiopia's denial of broader RSF-base allegations, which is not an event-specific rebuttal. Exact casualty counts need their own verification and cannot authenticate launch origin.
- **Independent corroboration:** **no** for Ethiopian launch origin.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear**. **Publication gate: HOLD** until the source clock and event eligibility are validated.
- **Confidence:** **low**.
- **Sources:** [AFP / S11](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1442070-26-women-4-children-killed-in-sudan-rsf-drone-strike) — supports that a named governor made the allegation and reports witness accounts; does not independently establish the launch point.

Independent casualty witnesses provide a different evidentiary function, not confirmation of the governor's geographic attribution.
