# News Scout — 2026-10-11

## Frozen research boundary and method

- **Research cutoff:** 2026-10-11 02:00:00 America/New_York (EDT; 2026-10-11 06:00:00 UTC).
- **Approximate 30-hour window:** 2026-10-09 20:00:00 through 2026-10-11 02:00:00 America/New_York; 2026-10-10 00:00:00 through 2026-10-11 06:00:00 UTC.
- **No-post-cutoff leakage rule:** No report published or updated after that cutoff is evidence here. A date without a reliable clock, an unstated publisher timezone, or a mutable page without an update stamp is marked timing-uncertain. Those components need auditor clearance before final use. An older event can qualify as newly reported information, but its event date remains explicit.
- **Scope:** Stage 1 discovery only. Fifteen serious candidates are documented below; an eleven-candidate ranked shortlist ends the file. These are candidates for verification, not fifteen established developments or a recommendation to print eleven major stories.
- **Continuity:** The 2026-10-10 convenience brief was inspected for ongoing threads only. Today's proposed deltas are Russian domestic export implementation, Trump's stronger demand, a new Saudi airport attack, new legal/diplomatic positions, a Palestinian decree rather than a recommendation, and newly reported maritime actions. Yesterday's wording is not evidence.
- **Standards:** Pangburn EFCS and Pangburn Replication Standard were read as separate controlling sibling documents, not merged or renumbered. This scout applies retrieval grounding, atomic claims, entailment, source lineage, disconfirming evidence, confidence distinct from verdict, denominator discipline, and explicit uncertainty. No numerical confidence or population-level reliability rate is inferred from this small candidate set.
- **Provenance labels:** PRIMARY-STATEMENT means a directly inspected official account of its own position; WIRE-OBSERVATION means reporter observation; WIRE-ATTRIBUTION means a wire relaying a named or unnamed source; SHARED-LINEAGE means different websites reproduce the same wire or underlying statement. Official assertions do not independently prove outcomes, attribution, legal entitlement, or motive.
