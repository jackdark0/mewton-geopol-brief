**No-post-cutoff leakage rule:** Admit only developments and source versions demonstrably available by the frozen cutoff. Later retrieval is permitted; later first availability is not. Undated additions, unclear timezones near the cutoff, and inaccessible search-only results remain quarantined. Page sidebars are not evidence. Older material supplies context only.

**Method / status:** Stage 1 discovery, not final adjudication. Search snippets, automated summaries and newsletter roundups were discovery only. A successful page open is not sufficient if the body is missing: Alberta result totals are held for that reason.
