## Story: Washington broadens pressure on Iran's industry and sanctions-evasion finance

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED | high confidence]** Treasury issued October 1 sectoral determinations for Iran's automotive and rail sectors and designated major automakers, rail firms, and foreign suppliers under Executive Order 13902. Eligibility for future designation and the actual named designations are separate legal acts. [Treasury industrial action](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0643), [OFAC Iran program index](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/iran-sanctions)
- **[CONFIRMED | high confidence]** OFAC designated the A7 Network as a significant transnational criminal organization, while FinCEN issued an alert and a notice of proposed rulemaking aimed at A7-controlled foreign sub-agents. The FinCEN prohibition was proposed, not final, at the cutoff. [Treasury A7 action](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0644/), [FinCEN proposed rule](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-10/9714-A7-NETWORK-NPRM.pdf), [FinCEN alert](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-10/FinCEN-Alert-A7-Network.pdf)
- **[SUPPORTED | moderate confidence]** FinCEN assessed more than $17 billion in globally aggregated sub-agent transactions from January 2025 through June 2026. The public proposed rule says it draws on Bank Secrecy Act data, law-enforcement information, and public sources; the figure is an agency assessment rather than an independently reproducible public dataset. [FinCEN proposed rule](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-10/9714-A7-NETWORK-NPRM.pdf)
- **[SUPPORTED | moderate confidence]** The separate $179.1 billion figure concerns gross A7A5 token transfers from February 2025 through June 2026. It is a different population and may include internal circulation; it must not be added to or directly compared with the $17 billion fiat/sub-agent assessment. [FinCEN proposed rule](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-10/9714-A7-NETWORK-NPRM.pdf)

### Material claims not established

- Treasury's claims that Iranian oil revenue is literally zero or that the industrial actions will materially change Tehran's war decisions.
- The share of A7 flows tied specifically to sanctions evasion, Iran, weapons procurement, or oil.
- Immediate compliance, blocked volumes, or economic effect from the proposed FinCEN rule.
- A blanket prohibition on every Iranian automotive or rail transaction worldwide.

### Source provenance

- Treasury, OFAC, and FinCEN are one U.S.-government family for the legal acts and most network allegations.
- Reuters independently confirmed the industrial announcement and reported an outside expert's warning about civilian costs, but its entity list remains derivative of Treasury. [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/us-sanctions-target-iran-s-auto-rail-sectors-as-blockade-chokes-ship-lanes-ce785ddad880f123)

### Open questions

- Which foreign banks identify and reject A7-linked payments before a final rule?
- How much trade shifts to new sub-agents or non-U.S. settlement channels?
- Do automotive and passenger-rail restrictions impose visible civilian costs without changing military behavior?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Explain the two legal tracks and firewall the two financial denominators.
