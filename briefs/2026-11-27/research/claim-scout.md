## Claim: Iran carried out the lethal November 26 drone attack in Kuwait

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Kuwait's Defence Ministry and Kuwaiti officials; repeated by several Gulf governments and some headlines.
- **Why it matters:** Attribution could drive Kuwaiti, Italian, EU or allied retaliation during an active regional war.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Kuwait said its forces detected hostile drones in its airspace and described the event as Iranian aggression. Kuwait has experienced prior acknowledged or alleged Iranian attacks in 2026.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Iran's armed-forces spokesperson denied that Iranian forces conducted an operation in the area, and Araghchi offered a joint investigation. No inspected public radar track, wreckage identification, launch evidence or forensic report ties this incident to an Iranian unit or command.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for the attack and deaths; no for attribution.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** high that attribution remains unresolved; low in either categorical attribution or exoneration.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.kuna.net.kw/ar/News?newsId=3340245 - Kuwaiti military account and attribution.
  - https://www.esteri.it/it/sala_stampa/archivionotizie/comunicati/2026/11/attacco-con-droni-in-kuwait-due-cittadini-italiani-deceduti/ - Italian confirmation of deaths and site context, without public attribution evidence.
  - https://irna.com/en/allnews/ - official Iranian denial and investigation offer.
  - https://new.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Two%2BItalians%2Bkilled%2Bin%2BIranian%2Bdrone%2Battack%2Bin%2BKuwait%2C%2BItaly%2Bsays/27404719.html - Reuters account of both claims.
- **Questions for auditor:** What exact evidence did Kuwait use? Did Italy inspect wreckage or independently endorse attribution? Was the site struck directly or by interception debris?

## Claim: The Houthis struck Jazan refinery and the Saudi-led coalition destroyed 50 military targets in response

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Saudi-led coalition and Saudi Energy Ministry; Houthi responsibility is asserted by the coalition.
- **Why it matters:** It frames a verified refinery impact as a Houthi success and a large coalition response as effective counterforce action.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The Energy Ministry confirmed a hostile aerial projectile, fire, death, injuries and partial operational suspension. The coalition attributed the attack to the Houthis and announced 50 target destructions across three provinces.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The inspected Energy Ministry wording does not itself identify the attacker. No independent weapon identification, launch evidence or battle-damage assessment for 50 targets was found. Houthi and civilian-casualty accounts require separate inspection.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for effects at Jazan; no for perpetrator and 50-target damage.
- **Current assessment:** supported for a lethal hostile-projectile impact and temporary interruption; plausible but not independently established for Houthi responsibility; claimed for 50 destroyed targets.
- **Confidence:** high on target effects; medium-low on attribution; low on counterstrike effects.
- **Sources:**
  - https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/666565/saudi-arabia/al-maliki-jazan-refinery-was-attacked-by-houthi-militia - Energy Ministry effects.
  - https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2737028 - coalition attribution.

- **Questions for auditor:** Can imagery or operator data establish the weapon and damage? What does "destroyed" mean across the 50 targets, and is there independent or Houthi reporting?

## Claim: North Korea suspended missile, ammunition, airspace and additional troop support for Russia

- **Why it matters:** If accurate, it would constrain a material Russian supply source and reveal a serious alliance dispute.
- **Evidence offered for it:** HUR attributes the decision to Kim Jong Un and gives specific categories: KN-23 missiles, artillery ammunition, Russian military flights and a proposed troop expansion.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** North Korea, Russia and South Korea had not publicly confirmed the pause by cutoff. No shipping, rail, aviation or inventory data were presented. The claimed motive relies on HUR's account of internal diplomatic exchanges.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear; a consequential belligerent-intelligence claim.
- **Confidence:** high that the claim exists and lacks public corroboration; low in the underlying proposition.
- **Sources:**

## Claim: Ethiopian and Eritrean state forces fought directly near Badme and Ethiopia captured Gual Gemhalo

- **Origin / principal promoters:** ACLED event reports based on local sources; repeated by regional outlets.
- **Why it matters:** Direct state-on-state fighting near Badme could mark the start of another Ethiopia-Eritrea war.
- **Evidence offered for it:** ACLED reported heavy infantry fighting through the night of November 25-26 among Ethiopian federal/pro-government Tigrayan forces and Eritrean/Tigrayan forces, followed by Ethiopian-aligned control of Gual Gemhalo. Residents reported shelling and air activity.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No inspected official statement, geolocated imagery or independent map confirms the combatant identities, boundary, control line or duration. Earlier reports sometimes disagreed over who held nearby positions.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for serious fighting in the area; no for the exact order of battle and durable control.
- **Current assessment:** plausible but not sufficiently established for categorical publication.
- **Confidence:** medium-low.
- **Sources:**
  - https://acleddata.com/ethiopia-alerts-archive - main event and control claim.
  - https://apnews.com/article/427358084fef2eb567e38acd81c3eacd - independent reporting on preceding deployments and exchanges.
  - https://www.criticalthreats.org/briefs/ethiopia-war-security-review/ethiopia-war-security-review-november-24-2026 - explicit source conflicts before the claimed capture.

## Claim: The EU approved another EUR45 billion for Ukraine in 2027

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Compressed summaries of the special European Council and the Commission proposal.
- **Why it matters:** It would materially change Ukraine's 2027 financing outlook and can double-count separate instruments.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Twenty-four member states backed the additional proposal; the Commission proposed up to EUR45 billion; the EU separately adopted a EUR45 billion 2027 allocation from an existing EUR90 billion loan.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The European Council's official record says leaders will return to the additional proposal in December. Broad political support is not adoption. The existing allocation and additional proposal are legally separate.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for legal status through primary EU records.
- **Current assessment:** false if phrased as approval of the additional loan; supported if phrased as 24-state political support without adoption.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/european-council/2026/11/26-27/ - official outcome and December deferral.
  - https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf - additional proposal.
  - https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623 - separate adopted 2027 allocation.
- **Questions for auditor:** Identify the exact proposal supported by 24 states and whether any non-unanimous workaround was agreed before cutoff.

- **Evidence offered for it:** The Federal Supreme Court backed federal border authority; military deployments and road closures were ordered; Border Forces announced implementation; federal control of export approvals and specified fields was directed.

No inspected pre-cutoff evidence showed federal occupation or staffing of a named KRG-administered crossing or oilfield. The court ruling reported by AP concerns borders, not necessarily oilfield management.

- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/iraq-krg-baghdad-oil-borders-kurds-protest-irbil-erbil-1b0e8cb02a09ce16b8885c60f4733a44 - court, deployments, protests and scope.
