## Story: Congress clears Russia–Iran sanctions bill with broad tariff authority

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED]** The House passed H.R. 5334 at 18:28 EDT on September 16 by 262–159: 203 Republicans, 58 Democrats and one independent voted yes. [House Clerk](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026308?Page=1)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The bill targets Russian officials, financial institutions and shadow-fleet activity, extends Iran sanctions for five years, and creates tariff authorities tied to Russian trade and energy purchases. [House Rules](https://rules.house.gov/bill/119/hr-5334) [AP](https://apnews.com/article/sanctions-russia-ukraine-house-tariffs-china-f377fc956c442195dfcf50571b01a1c4)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The statutory design does not establish an automatic, immediate 100% tariff on named countries. CRS identifies rates “up to” 100% for certain secondary tariffs, a possible 500% ceiling for primary Russia tariffs, undefined or ambiguous triggers, and national-interest waiver authority. [CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/LSB11474.html)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Attempts in the House Rules process to narrow tariffs, name countries, tighten waivers, add Ukraine financing or remove open-ended waiver language were submitted but not made in order. [House Rules](https://rules.house.gov/bill/119/hr-5334)

### Material claims not established

- That China or India will immediately face a 100% rate. The administration must still make consequential findings and choices, and the law contains broad discretion.
- That the measure will reduce Russian revenue rather than reroute trade or trigger retaliation.

### Source provenance

- The Clerk and House Rules Committee provide independent primary records of the vote and legislative text/process.
- CRS supplies independent legal analysis. AP independently reports political reaction but is not the authority for exact statutory meaning.

### Open questions

- Which importers, rates and implementation date will the administration select after enactment?
- How will the executive interpret “knowingly made,” “country” and the top-five evasion criteria?

### Recommendation

- **Include major** and carry the automatic-tariff formulation into contested claims.
