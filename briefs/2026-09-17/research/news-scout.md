## Candidate: House clears Russia–Iran sanctions bill with broad tariff authority

- **Event time/date:** 2026-09-16, 18:28 EDT.
- **Region / actors:** United States, Russia, Iran, major Russian-energy importers.
- **What changed:** The House passed H.R. 5334 by 262–159 and sent it to the president. The bill targets Russian officials, financial institutions and the shadow fleet, extends Iran sanctions for five years, and creates primary and secondary tariff authorities tied to Russian trade and energy purchases.
- **Why this may matter:** It gives the executive a powerful coercive instrument while leaving major choices—rates, targets, waivers and implementation—inside the administration.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** The Clerk is the primary vote record; AP reports the political coalition and bill scope; CRS independently analyzes the statutory language.
- **Sources:** [House Clerk vote 308](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026308?Page=1); [House Rules bill page and Senate amendment](https://rules.house.gov/bill/119/hr-5334); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/sanctions-russia-ukraine-house-tariffs-china-f377fc956c442195dfcf50571b01a1c4); [CRS legal analysis](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/LSB11474.html).
- **Questions for auditor:** Does the enacted language mandate immediate 100% tariffs on China or India? What discretion and waiver authority remain?
