**Method:** Scout claims were decomposed into atomic propositions and checked against direct records, official statements, or reporting whose publication time fell within the fixed window. Search snippets and post-cutoff updates were not used as evidence. Multiple reports carrying the same wire or official statement were treated as one provenance lineage.

## Audit key

- **CONFIRMED:** directly supported by a primary record or by independent, mutually consistent evidence.
- **SUPPORTED:** credible evidence supports the proposition, but an important detail, attribution, or independent confirmation remains missing.
- **CLAIMED:** an identified actor made the claim; the claim itself is verified as having been made, but its substance is not independently established.
- **DISPUTED:** credible sources conflict or a consequential party rejects the claim.
- **UNSUPPORTED:** the inspected evidence does not entail the proposition.
- **Confidence:** confidence in the verdict, not confidence that the underlying allegation is true.

## 1. No verified Russia–Ukraine energy ceasefire existed by the cutoff

### Atomic findings

1. **CLAIMED — High confidence:** U.S. President Donald Trump publicly announced that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to stop attacks on energy infrastructure, effective immediately. The announcement is direct evidence of the U.S. claim, not evidence of mutual assent. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/11/russia-ukraine-oil-energy-ceasefire-trump-putin-zelensky), [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-yandex-trump-zelenskyy-f621fa788196f204448e7f75fcc78c68)
2. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Ukraine was prepared to stop attacks on Russian energy facilities if Russia did the same, while saying Kyiv still needed clear details. That is a conditional commitment, not confirmation of agreed terms. [President of Ukraine](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/ukrayina-gotova-ne-zavdavati-udariv-po-rosijskij-energetici-106845), [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-yandex-trump-zelenskyy-f621fa788196f204448e7f75fcc78c68)
3. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** No inspected Russian statement accepted a ceasefire or published common terms before the cutoff. Axios reported a White House official saying Moscow was considering a refinery-for-refinery arrangement, while the AP reported no immediate Russian comment. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/11/russia-ukraine-oil-energy-ceasefire-trump-putin-zelensky), [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-yandex-trump-zelenskyy-f621fa788196f204448e7f75fcc78c68)
4. **SUPPORTED — Medium confidence:** The working U.S. concept was narrower than Trump's public wording: reciprocal restraint against refineries. Ukraine sought protection for the wider power grid and power plants. This scope mismatch was reported by Axios through officials; no signed or jointly published text was available. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/11/russia-ukraine-oil-energy-ceasefire-trump-putin-zelensky)
5. **CLAIMED — High confidence:** Ukrainian authorities continued to report Russian strikes after Trump's announcement. These are attributable wartime claims; the inspected evidence did not independently verify every strike or establish whether any unpublished agreement was in force. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-yandex-trump-zelenskyy-f621fa788196f204448e7f75fcc78c68)
6. **SUPPORTED — Medium confidence:** European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called for a ceasefire protecting energy and food supplies in both countries after speaking with Zelensky. This shows European support for a defined reciprocal arrangement, not proof that one already existed. [European Commission](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/read-out-president-von-der-leyen-following-her-call-president-zelenskyy-2026-10-11_en)
7. **CLAIMED — Medium confidence:** The Financial Times lineage reported that Washington threatened to restrict intelligence or arms supplied through the PURL mechanism if Ukraine continued refinery attacks. The Reuters relay could not independently verify the report, and no implementation was identified before the cutoff. [Reuters relay](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/usthreatens-to-block-arms-for-ukraine-over-refinery-attacks-ft-reports-4942801)

### Audit verdict

**Verdict:** Trump's announcement outran the verified diplomatic record. By the cutoff, Ukraine had made a conditional commitment, Russia had not publicly accepted, and the scope remained unsettled. The reported U.S. leverage threat is consequential but remains a single-lineage claim without evidence of implementation.  
**Confidence:** High on the absence of verified mutual assent; medium on the precise U.S. pressure mechanism.

### Denominator and provenance note

No count of attacks was used to infer compliance because there was no published start time, common target definition, monitoring mechanism, or denominator of alleged incidents. The AP, Axios, Ukrainian presidency, and European Commission provide distinct records, but several details in secondary reports ultimately depend on anonymous officials.

[Radio Pakistan](https://radio.gov.pk/11-10-2026/president-promulgates-emergency-governors-rule-in-kp), [Radio Pakistan](https://radio.gov.pk/11-10-2026/president-zardari-signs-pms-advice-on-kps-security-situation)

[Radio Pakistan](https://radio.gov.pk/11-10-2026/president-promulgates-emergency-governors-rule-in-kp)
3. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** The federal government said military support would be deployed under Article 245 and framed the action as necessary because of militancy, governance failure, and risks to national integrity. The underlying security assessment is the government's rationale, not an independently proven legal necessity. [Radio Pakistan](https://radio.gov.pk/11-10-2026/president-promulgates-emergency-governors-rule-in-kp)
4. **CLAIMED — High confidence:** Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf described the move as an unconstitutional or disguised coup against its provincial mandate and announced resistance. This verifies the opposition position, not the legal merits. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/pakistan-khan-march-khyber-pakhtunkhwa-3fee40be73c3f5632f2be3204e9c47f8)
5. **SUPPORTED — Medium confidence:** The measure creates a serious confrontation between the federal government and PTI in a strategically sensitive province bordering Afghanistan. The confrontation follows directly from the institutional displacement and announced opposition, while its trajectory remains uncertain. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/pakistan-khan-march-khyber-pakhtunkhwa-3fee40be73c3f5632f2be3204e9c47f8)

### Audit verdict

**Verdict:** The institutional action is verified and strategically important. Whether it is constitutionally justified, a necessary counterterrorism measure, or a partisan power seizure is contested and cannot be settled from the available public record.  
**Confidence:** High on what the order did; low on competing claims of necessity and legality.

### Denominator and provenance note

No incident or casualty series was found that could independently validate the federal government's broad security rationale. The government and PTI statements are adversarial participant evidence. The AP provides procedural context but not a judicial ruling.

## 4. Two separate tanker incidents near Oman left two Indian seafarers missing

### Atomic findings

1. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** UK Maritime Trade Operations issued Warning 164-26 after the company security officer of an outbound tanker reported that an unidentified projectile struck the vessel's port side near the Strait of Hormuz. The vessel was damaged; the crew was safe and no environmental impact was reported at that time. [UKMTO](https://www.ukmto.org/), [Saudi Press Agency relay](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2698664)
2. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** India's government identified that vessel as the Marshall Islands-flagged *MT Valthera* and said all 11 Indian crew members were safe. The identity is therefore sourced to India rather than inferred from proximity alone. [Akashvani News](https://newsonair.gov.in/two-tankers-attacked-near-strait-of-hormuz-and-oman-two-indian-seafarers-missing/)
3. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** A separate vessel, *MT Petro Dolphin*, was struck by a drone or unmanned aerial vehicle while at Sohar anchorage. Nine of its 11 Indian crew members were rescued and two were missing at the cutoff. [Akashvani News](https://newsonair.gov.in/two-tankers-attacked-near-strait-of-hormuz-and-oman-two-indian-seafarers-missing/), [NAVAREA IX warning](https://www.sealagom.com/navarea/9/message/24867/)
4. **CONFIRMED — High confidence:** The Indian government treated the events as two distinct attacks and said it was coordinating with Omani authorities. [Akashvani News](https://newsonair.gov.in/two-tankers-attacked-near-strait-of-hormuz-and-oman-two-indian-seafarers-missing/)
5. **UNSUPPORTED — High confidence:** No inspected source established a perpetrator for either incident before the cutoff. No inference from regional conflict, weapon type, or Houthi claims elsewhere meets the attribution standard.

### Audit verdict

**Verdict:** Two separate maritime attacks are verified, including two missing seafarers from *Petro Dolphin*. Attribution is open. The incidents raise navigational and insurance risk around the Strait of Hormuz and Sohar even without a verified common attacker.  
**Confidence:** High on the event count, vessel identities, and crew status; low on motive, coordination, and attribution.

### Denominator and provenance note

The vessels, locations, casualty status, and reporting chains must remain separate. UKMTO, Indian government reporting, and NAVAREA are operational records, but some facts originate with vessel operators and coastal authorities. No claim was made about the share of regional traffic affected.

### Audit verdict

**Verdict:** The multiple launches, mixed profiles, and longest observed range are verified. Missile classification and motive remain under analysis.  
**Confidence:** High on observations; medium on the irregular-trajectory assessment; low on motive.

### Denominator and provenance note

Japan's “at least two” and South Korea's “multiple” are compatible but do not establish an exact total. No label such as hypersonic is warranted without a technical assessment or North Korean announcement supported by observable evidence.
