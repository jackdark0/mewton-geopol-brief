## Claim: The United States offered Ukraine NATO membership in exchange for withdrawing from the Ukrainian-held Donbas

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A strong reading of President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's November 14 account; repeated in headlines and commentary.
- **Why it matters:** An authorized offer would be the most concrete territorial-security bargain yet disclosed in the current talks and would engage every NATO ally.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Zelenskyy said U.S. negotiators asked for the first time whether Ukraine would withdraw from Donbas territory it still holds if it were admitted to NATO. He said he would consult Ukrainian society if the United States and other NATO partners confirmed a precise proposition.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Zelenskyy's own conditional wording indicates the concept was not yet a confirmed offer. No U.S. policy statement, NATO consensus, Russian acceptance, map, sequencing rule, or guarantee text was found.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial on the remarks, no on the proposition as an authorized offer. Reuters and AP independently reported the same claimant's account.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear / overstated as an offer.** Supported that U.S. negotiators raised the question; not established that Washington or NATO made a deliverable offer.
- **Confidence:** high in the narrowed assessment; medium-low on the negotiators' exact authority and language.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-nato-us-zelenskyy-f9a8629626c087424f05adc85d127780 - Zelenskyy's conditional wording and policy constraints.
  - https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/us-floated-idea-of-ukraine-ceding-donbas-for-nato-membership-zelenskiy-says-5027318 - Reuters' “floated” characterization and first-time account.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain a U.S. readout or direct official response. Determine whether “withdraw” means all of Donetsk and Luhansk or only a defined area, and whether accession would precede withdrawal.

## Claim: Trump has decided not to launch new attacks on Iran

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Simplified readings of Reuters' report and Trump's public statements suggesting less likelihood of major renewed warfare.
- **Why it matters:** A settled stand-down would materially reduce near-term escalation risk and shape Iranian, Gulf, and market behavior.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Three people told Reuters that Trump said he may hold off absent new Iranian or Houthi provocations. Trump publicly said he was taking a slower route at regional allies' request. Saudi and Turkish leaders reportedly urged restraint.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** “May hold off” is conditional and not a decision. The White House emphasized presidential discretion. A replacement carrier is moving to the region and all three could overlap. No stand-down order, ceasefire, negotiation text, or defined provocation threshold was found.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for a restraint signal; no for a settled decision. The public statement is separate from the anonymous-source deliberation but does not establish operational orders.
- **Current assessment:** **qualified / unclear.** A conditional restraint signal is supported; a final decision against strikes is not.
- **Confidence:** high that the categorical claim overstates the evidence; medium on current operational intent.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trump-tells-advisers-he-may-hold-off-on-iran-attacks-barring-new-provocations-sources-say-ce785ededa8af425 - complete Reuters report, including the countervailing force posture.
- **Questions for auditor:** Is there an execute or stand-down order? What events qualify as provocation? Will three carriers overlap and for how long?

## Claim: Three oil tankers struck Iranian naval mines and caught fire on November 14

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Iran's conventional navy and IRGC navy through Iranian state media.
- **Why it matters:** Three confirmed mine strikes in one day would be a major escalation in the world's most important oil chokepoint.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Separate Iranian statements describe one tanker east of the strait and two inside or south of it, allegedly using unauthorized routes with tracking systems off. Iranian media aired military-supplied footage associated with the claims.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No names, flags, owners, crews, exact coordinates, distress calls, flag-state statements, shipowner notices, or independent imagery were provided. The inspected JMIC advisory predates the claims and documents other incidents, not these vessels. Media reports share the same Iranian source chain.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** **weak / unestablished.** Established only that Iran made the claims and warned unapproved shipping.
- **Confidence:** high that public evidence is insufficient; low on whether one or more incidents occurred.
- **Sources:**

## Claim: South Sudan's move to a February 11 election is a lawful scheduling adjustment rather than another postponement

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A commission spokeswoman said the election was not postponed, only adjusted to allow other processes to finish.
- **Why it matters:** The legal classification affects the legitimacy of the first post-independence vote and authority after the transition expires.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The commission issued a detailed calendar, began registration, and scheduled nominations, campaigning, voting, results, runoffs, and disputes. The delay is under two months.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The prior polling date was December 22. Local legal reporting identifies statutory language requiring elections in December 2026, a three-month voter-register lead, six months' notice, and voting 60 days before the February 22 transition end. The new dispute period runs to July. No inspected court or legislative act authorizes the change.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes on the date change; partial and not adjudicated on legality.
- **Current assessment:** **disputed / legally unresolved.** It is a postponement in ordinary time terms; legal validity requires authoritative adjudication.
- **Confidence:** high on the date change; medium on the identified legal conflict; low on final legal outcome.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/c62033bb79a606ef8ce7e335cfc8478b - event, commission calendar, and missing explanation.
  - https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nec-pushes-elections-beyond-december-deadline-set-in-law - relevant statutory and transition provisions.
  - https://nec.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Endorsement-of-the-Parties-Signatory-to-the-R-ARCS_240917_101953.pdf - transition roadmap and December 2026 election commitment.
- **Questions for auditor:** Find the 2026 National Elections Act amendment and any gazetted legal instrument changing the date. Establish which court or body has jurisdiction.

## Claim: Canada and ASEAN have completed a free-trade agreement covering nearly 700 million people

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Canadian government language and headlines compressing “substantial conclusion of negotiations” into a completed agreement.
- **Why it matters:** A completed, signed pact would materially alter Canada's access to regional supply chains and diversify trade away from the United States.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Canada says negotiations are substantially concluded and projects gains to GDP, jobs, and exports. It announced $5 million in technical help for finalization and ratification.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Canada itself says remaining steps must be completed and signature is only intended for 2027. No treaty text, schedules, ASEAN collective confirmation, ratification, or entry-into-force date was inspected. Canadian numerical projections lack a disclosed model in the inspected release.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial. AP independently reports Canada's announcement; that does not replace an ASEAN-side text.
- **Current assessment:** **qualified.** Negotiations are substantially concluded, not a signed or operative FTA. The separate Philippines negotiation is described by both governments as concluded but also awaits signature.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/11/14/canada-concludes-negotiations-two-new-free-trade-agreements - primary wording, remaining steps, and projections.
  - https://apnews.com/article/canada-carney-trump-us-trade-deal-philippines-00dea030028a7c221840458b25469e02 - independent report preserving the negotiation/status distinction.
  - https://www.pna.gov.ph/article/ph-canada-conclude-negotiations-for-free-trade-agreement - Philippine confirmation for the bilateral negotiation only.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain ASEAN's outcome text and annexes. Identify unresolved chapters and modeling assumptions. Do not report market coverage as current legal access.

## Claim: NATO canceled the 2027 Albania summit because the alliance is fracturing

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Interpretive coverage linking the cancellation and reduced ministerial schedule to disputes over spending, Iran, Ukraine, and Trump's behavior.
- **Why it matters:** If political fracture caused the change, it would be a material alliance-cohesion signal during war and negotiations with Russia.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Albania was previously promised the next summit; the July declaration omitted it; some allies reportedly opposed rewarding Albania over spending; ministerial meetings are also being reduced; senior U.S. officials have skipped or shortened NATO meetings; disputes over Iran and burden-sharing are public.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Rutte describes flexible scheduling. NATO historically held summits every two years. Hosting costs about $100 million and requires long preparation. AP quotes a former NATO official arguing that existing spending and force-planning decisions may leave no need for a 2027 summit. No NATO decision document attributes the change to fracture.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes on the schedule change; no on the causal narrative.
- **Current assessment:** **unclear / weak as a causal claim.** Reduced political cadence is real; alliance fracture is one interpretation, not an established cause.
- **Confidence:** high that causation is unproved; medium on the practical significance of reduced cadence.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/nato-summit-meetings-trump-spending-cuts-rutte-492167d1083b5858973d5e9cf43d4634 - direct comments, alternative explanations, costs, and broader meeting reductions.
  - https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/transcripts/2026/07/08/press-conference-by-nato-secretary-general-mark-rutte-following-the-meeting-of-heads-of-state-and-government-at-the-2026-nato-summit-in-ankara - primary prior commitment.
- **Questions for auditor:** Find the reorganization package and any North Atlantic Council record. Separate Albania-hosting objections from the decision to skip a leaders' meeting entirely.
