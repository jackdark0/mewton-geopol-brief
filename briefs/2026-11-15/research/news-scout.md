## Candidate: U.S. floated Donbas withdrawal for NATO membership

- **Event time/date:** Zelenskyy's remarks were released on 2026-11-14; Reuters published at 07:25 ET and AP at 11:21 UTC.
- **Region / actors:** Ukraine, United States, Russia, NATO.
- **What changed:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said U.S. negotiators raised, for the first time in the current talks, whether Ukraine would withdraw from the parts of Donbas it still controls if NATO members admitted Ukraine. He said Kyiv would not recognize occupied territory as Russian, would not accept neutrality, and would consult Ukrainian society if the United States and other allies made a precise offer. He also said Russia rejected an energy ceasefire.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It links the central territorial dispute to the security guarantee Kyiv has long sought.
  - The proposal depends on unanimous NATO acceptance and Russian assent; neither is established.
  - It exposes a possible bargaining structure without showing that Washington has adopted it as policy.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high that Zelenskyy made the account; medium-low that it represents an agreed U.S. or allied offer.
- **Source chain:** Reuters and AP both report Zelenskyy's embargoed remarks, so they are independent reporters but share the same principal claimant. No U.S., NATO, or Russian confirmation was found.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/us-floated-idea-of-ukraine-ceding-donbas-for-nato-membership-zelenskiy-says-5027318 - Reuters report of Zelenskyy's account, the first-time characterization, and lack of progress on an energy ceasefire.
  - https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-nato-us-zelenskyy-f9a8629626c087424f05adc85d127780 - AP's fuller account of the proposed exchange, Zelenskyy's conditions, and his rejection of neutrality.
- **Questions for auditor:** Did the U.S. pose a question, test a concept, or make an offer? Has any NATO member accepted the exchange? Did Moscow reject the energy ceasefire directly, or is that also Zelenskyy's account?

## Candidate: Trump privately signals a conditional hold on new Iran strikes

- **Event time/date:** Reuters published 2026-11-14 at 06:04 ET.
- **Region / actors:** United States, Iran, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Yemen's Houthis.
- **What changed:** Reuters reported that Trump told senior advisers he may hold off on new strikes if Iran and the Houthis do not launch new provocations. The same report says Saudi and Turkish leaders urged restraint, U.S. intelligence assesses blockade and sanctions pressure is hurting Iran, a third carrier is moving toward the region as a replacement, and all three could remain if needed. The White House did not announce a policy or commitment.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It is a conditional de-escalation signal after weeks of strike threats.
  - The force posture preserves escalation capacity and makes the signal reversible.
  - Reported stockpile strain and assessments that strikes would not shift Iran's demands may be shaping the choice.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** medium; the account rests mostly on anonymous officials and people familiar with discussions.
- **Source chain:** One Reuters investigation with multiple unnamed-source categories; public Trump remarks and a Turkish call readout provide partial support for restraint, not the private decision process.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trump-tells-advisers-he-may-hold-off-on-iran-attacks-barring-new-provocations-sources-say-ce785ededa8af425 - full Reuters report, including the conditional signal, allied advice, carrier posture, munitions concern, and intelligence assessment.
- **Questions for auditor:** Is there a written or operational order? Which actions count as a “provocation”? Is the third carrier a normal relief, an overlap, or a lasting increase? Can the reported munitions condition be independently supported?

## Candidate: South Sudan moves its first national election to February 2027

- **Event time/date:** National Elections Commission timetable published 2026-11-14; AP published 10:57 UTC.
- **Region / actors:** South Sudan, National Elections Commission, SPLM, opposition parties, UNMISS.
- **What changed:** The commission moved polling from 2026-12-22 to 2027-02-11 while starting voter registration. The revised calendar pushes voting close to the 2027-02-22 end of the extended transition. The timetable itself gave no reason; a commission spokeswoman later described the change as an adjustment for unfinished preparations.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - The country has never held a national election since independence.
  - A narrow post-election dispute window before the transition expires increases legal and security risk.

- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** AP, Reuters, Radio Tamazuj, Eye Radio, and Anadolu all inspected the same commission calendar; their context and interviews are partly independent, but the date has one primary origin.
- **Sources:**

  - https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nec-pushes-elections-beyond-december-deadline-set-in-law - local reporting on the legal deadline and post-election dispute period.
  - https://eyeradio.org/national-elections-commission-pushes-december-elections-to-february-11-2027/ - local account of the commission's calendar.
