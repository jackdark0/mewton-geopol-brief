## Candidate: Iron Gate is an interagency pressure architecture, not yet an interdiction force

- **Underlying development:** CENTCOM directed an approximately 20-person Tampa staff to coordinate U.S. action against Iranian land- and Caspian-route movements of weapons, dual-use goods, sanctioned materials and revenue.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Headlines imply a new operational cordon. The release describes an internal coordinating staff and gives no partner states, rules of engagement, funding, legal authorities or field assets.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Better intelligence fusion and sanctions/customs coordination could raise transaction costs and expose procurement chains that bypass the sea blockade. Effect depends on cooperation by Russia, Central Asian states, Pakistan, Turkey, the South Caucasus and Caspian shippers—none of which the release establishes.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The task force's establishment, size, base and stated portfolio; Reuters' prior finding that Iran used Caspian, overland and air routes.
- **What remains inference:** Operational access, successful disruption, partner cooperation and economic effect. CENTCOM's claimed $25 billion in prevented crude exports lacks a disclosed denominator and counterfactual.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [CENTCOM release archive](https://www.forth.news/lists/centcom/Cgoet5KhSp6nWZnwnUeuR); [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/expanding-on-naval-blockade-us-military-looks-to-restrict-iranian-land-commerce-ce785fd2d18df127).
- **Questions for auditor:** What authorities and partner arrangements exist? What source data support the $25 billion estimate?

## Candidate: The Huda strike turns blockade policy into direct merchant-vessel risk

- **Underlying development:** A U.S. aircraft fired on the stern of a named commercial vessel after CENTCOM says it ignored warnings and left after an Iranian port call.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Coverage can treat the event as routine enforcement, but it is a kinetic act against a civilian-flagged merchant ship in a contested blockade.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A credible willingness to disable ships may deter port calls more effectively than sanctions notices. It also raises insurance, crew-safety, flag-state, neutral-shipping and escalation costs; an error or casualty could harden Iranian retaliation or foreign opposition.
- **What evidence is observable now:** CENTCOM's own acknowledgement, ship name and weapon-use description; AP's independent publication of the disclosure.
- **What remains inference:** Whether the warnings were effective, the ship violated a lawful restriction, the cargo was prohibited, the crew was safe, and the action changed traffic.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [CENTCOM release archive](https://www.forth.news/lists/centcom/CgpDKBG4rBuKCJV2Bw6z6); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/commercial-vessel-gulf-oman-breach-blockade-iran-bc902d3400cb5d022f8bafd247ef975b).
- **Questions for auditor:** Locate AIS history, flag/owner records, maritime notices and any crew or owner statement.

## Candidate: New Zealand's electoral arithmetic is more fragile than the party-vote headline

- **Underlying development:** The preliminary 131-seat projection gives National-ACT-New Zealand First 67 seats, a one-seat cushion over the majority threshold, while Labour has a marginally higher party-vote share than National.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** “Conservative bloc leads” hides the overhang/seat-allocation structure and approximately 399,365 uncounted special votes.
- **Strategic mechanism:** A seat shift can convert a nominal incumbent return into negotiations with Opportunity or a different coalition. Government formation affects New Zealand's China, AUKUS-pillar, Pacific and defense-spending choices, but those policy consequences should not be forecast before a coalition exists.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Official preliminary vote and seat totals; the special-vote estimate; November 27 official-results date.
- **What remains inference:** Final seat allocation, coalition terms and foreign-policy direction.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Electoral Commission release](https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/2026-general-election-preliminary-results); [official results](https://electionresults.govt.nz/ge2026/index.html); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/8c1728ae2533201f68d5a5e75fc9e907).
- **Questions for auditor:** Check the exact majority threshold and model no seat outcome from past special-vote patterns as fact.

## Candidate: Israel's recommendation majority does not equal a governing majority

- **Underlying development:** Herzog chose Eisenkot for the first mandate after 67 recommendations.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** Recommendation numbers look like a coalition majority, but recommending a candidate is not a signed confidence-and-supply agreement.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Eisenkot can bargain from first-mover status, while Netanyahu must prevent an Arab-supported or unity alternative. The outcome could alter war oversight, investigation of October 7 failures, conscription and relations with Washington, but none of those changes has occurred.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Presidency statement, faction recommendation totals and 28-day plus possible 14-day timetable.
- **What remains inference:** Coalition composition, prime minister, policy platform and Arab-party role.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [Israeli Presidency](https://www.president.gov.il/en/president-herzog-concludes-consultations-and-announces-he-will-task-gadi-eisenkot-with-forming-a-government/); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/israel-elections-75934bfce1f2f2e43e1823de3761b12d).
- **Questions for auditor:** Which recommendations came with coalition commitments?

## Candidate: Paraguay's partial reimbursement is a stabilization signal with an undefined denominator

- **Underlying development:** The central bank told AP that 1.2 million Ueno customers had received partial or full reimbursement, while Peña was charged and detained.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** A large customer count sounds like resolution, but partial and full payments are combined and the total affected population, deposits, insured claims, public-fund exposure and funding source are not disclosed in the inspected report.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Fast insured payouts can limit a broader bank run and political unrest. Poorly targeted or fiscally opaque compensation can transfer losses to the state and prolong legitimacy problems.
- **What evidence is observable now:** Charges, detention, central-bank claim and planned teachers' strike.
- **What remains inference:** Guilt, full depositor recovery, total fiscal cost and systemic contagion.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/paraguay-president-pena-ueno-bank-0a3cddd03f9fb36c870fa3117aeeb4ac).
- **Questions for auditor:** Split partial from full reimbursements and obtain amounts, deposit-insurance funding and remaining claims.

## Candidate: Yemen's escalation has an attribution problem at its center

- **Underlying development:** Homes near Sanaa were struck with mass casualties, followed by a Houthi claim of retaliatory attacks on Saudi Arabia.
- **Why it may be under-covered:** The humanitarian imagery is strong, but attribution, casualty totals and retaliation effects remain source-dependent.
- **Strategic mechanism:** Civilian deaths can expand Houthi recruitment and retaliation incentives; Saudi denial or silence without forensic evidence does not resolve responsibility. Repeated unverified attributions can also trigger escalation on false premises.
- **What evidence is observable now:** AP imagery of casualties and rubble; attributed health-ministry totals; Reuters report that Saudi authorities were investigating.
- **What remains inference:** Perpetrator, weapon, intentional targeting of rescuers, final toll and damage in Saudi Arabia.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-houthi-saudi-attacks-1b5aef5b86be2e96e1cd8a18165afaf6); [Reuters](https://sa.marketscreener.com/news/houthi-run-health-ministry-says-airstrikes-it-blames-on-saudi-arabia-kill-27-near-yemen-capital-ce785fd2d089f121).
- **Questions for auditor:** Seek independent munition, satellite, radar and hospital evidence.

- **What remains inference:** Bill text, constitutional effect, Senate support, veto override and operational impact.
- **Initial priority:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/democrats-house-majority-iran-war-healthcare-tariffs-0658bbfc51d2bc23331714377cddb0d2).
