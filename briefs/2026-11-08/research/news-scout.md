## Candidate: U.S. aircraft disables M/V Huda in the Gulf of Oman

- **Event time/date:** November 7, 2026; disclosed before cutoff.
- **Region / actors:** Gulf of Oman; U.S. Central Command; St. Kitts-flagged M/V *Huda*.
- **What changed:** CENTCOM says a U.S. aircraft fired precision munitions at the ship's stern after warnings and after directing the civilian crew away from the targeted spaces. AP independently reported that CENTCOM announced the action, but neither source independently establishes the ship's conduct, cargo, crew condition, or damage beyond the U.S. account.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - It is a direct U.S. kinetic action against a commercial vessel enforcing the Iran blockade.
  - The incident increases legal, maritime-safety, and escalation risk around Iranian ports and coastal waters.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high that CENTCOM acknowledged firing; medium on the circumstances; low on independent damage and crew details.
- **Source chain:** The CENTCOM release is the originating account. AP is independent reporting of the disclosure, but its incident facts still derive from CENTCOM.
- **Sources:** [CENTCOM release archive](https://www.forth.news/lists/centcom/CgpDKBG4rBuKCJV2Bw6z6) (U.S. account, ship name, claimed warnings, weapon use); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/commercial-vessel-gulf-oman-breach-blockade-iran-bc902d3400cb5d022f8bafd247ef975b) (independent publication and surrounding maritime context).
- **Questions for auditor:** Where was the ship, what law or notice governed the blockade, what did the crew receive, were there casualties, and is there independent tracking or imagery?
