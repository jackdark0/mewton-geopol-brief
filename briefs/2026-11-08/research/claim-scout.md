## Claim: CENTCOM's blockade prevented more than $25 billion in Iranian crude exports

- **Origin / principal promoters:** CENTCOM's November 7 Task Force Iron Gate release; repeated by Reuters.
- **Why it matters:** The number is the main quantitative claim offered to show that the blockade works and to justify extending pressure to land and Caspian routes.
- **Evidence offered for it:** CENTCOM states the estimate categorically.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No methodology, counterfactual export volume, price series, time interval, diverted volume or distinction between delayed, discounted and permanently prevented exports was disclosed.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear / claimed.
- **Confidence:** high that CENTCOM made the claim; low in the estimate's independent validity.
- **Sources:** [CENTCOM release archive](https://www.forth.news/lists/centcom/Cgoet5KhSp6nWZnwnUeuR); [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/expanding-on-naval-blockade-us-military-looks-to-restrict-iranian-land-commerce-ce785fd2d18df127).
- **Questions for auditor:** What numerator, baseline, dates and prices produce $25 billion?

## Claim: M/V Huda ignored repeated warnings and tried multiple times to violate the blockade

- **Origin / principal promoters:** CENTCOM.
- **Why it matters:** The claim supplies the immediate factual and legal justification for firing on a civilian-flagged merchant vessel.
- **Evidence offered for it:** CENTCOM says it warned the crew, directed them away from targeted spaces, and fired at the stern after the ship visited an Iranian port.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No warning audio, radio log, AIS reconstruction, notice to mariners, crew statement, flag-state account, cargo evidence or independent imagery was inspected.
- **Independent corroboration:** no; AP independently reports the announcement but not the underlying conduct.
- **Current assessment:** plausible but unverified.
- **Confidence:** medium-low.
- **Sources:** [CENTCOM release archive](https://www.forth.news/lists/centcom/CgpDKBG4rBuKCJV2Bw6z6); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/commercial-vessel-gulf-oman-breach-blockade-iran-bc902d3400cb5d022f8bafd247ef975b).
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain ship tracking, communications, ownership and crew status.

## Claim: Saudi Arabia carried out the Bani Hushaysh/al-Shariya strikes that killed at least 27 people

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Houthi-run Health Ministry and Saba; Houthi officials.
- **Why it matters:** Attribution can trigger retaliation and shape judgments about the resumed war and civilian protection.
- **Evidence offered for it:** AP imagery supports that homes were struck and civilians were harmed. Houthi authorities attribute the attack to Saudi Arabia.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No independently verified munition remnants, aircraft/radar record, satellite imagery establishing the platform, or Saudi admission. Saudi authorities told Reuters they were investigating.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for strike and casualties; no for perpetrator.
- **Current assessment:** supported that a deadly strike occurred; unclear who carried it out.
- **Confidence:** high on event, medium-low on toll, low on attribution.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-houthi-saudi-attacks-1b5aef5b86be2e96e1cd8a18165afaf6); [Reuters](https://sa.marketscreener.com/news/houthi-run-health-ministry-says-airstrikes-it-blames-on-saudi-arabia-kill-27-near-yemen-capital-ce785fd2d089f121).
- **Questions for auditor:** What physical evidence distinguishes Saudi aircraft, Saudi-backed aircraft, drones, missiles or internal fire?

## Claim: Eisenkot has a governing majority because 67 Knesset members recommended him

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Simplified headlines and political commentary following Herzog's decision.
- **Why it matters:** It converts a procedural advantage into a premature claim that Netanyahu has been replaced.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The presidency confirms recommendations from factions totaling 67 seats.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The same statement says Eisenkot still receives a 28-day mandate, extendable by 14 days. AP reports that neither bloc won a majority and that the possible coalition requires difficult compromises. A recommendation is not a signed coalition agreement or confidence vote.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for recommendation total; no for a completed governing majority.
- **Current assessment:** false as stated / materially premature.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Israeli Presidency](https://www.president.gov.il/en/president-herzog-concludes-consultations-and-announces-he-will-task-gadi-eisenkot-with-forming-a-government/); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/israel-elections-75934bfce1f2f2e43e1823de3761b12d).
- **Questions for auditor:** Which parties have committed to confidence and supply?

## Claim: New Zealand's incumbent coalition won the election

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Election-night shorthand based on the preliminary seat projection.
- **Why it matters:** It would determine the government and foreign-policy continuity.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The official preliminary projection gives National, ACT and New Zealand First 67 of 131 seats.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** About 399,365 special votes remain outside the preliminary count; the Electoral Commission calls the results indicative and says they will change. Official results are due November 27. Labour narrowly leads National in preliminary party votes.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for the preliminary numbers; not for a final win.
- **Current assessment:** qualified / premature.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Electoral Commission](https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/2026-general-election-preliminary-results); [official results portal](https://electionresults.govt.nz/ge2026/index.html).
- **Questions for auditor:** What seat changes are mathematically possible after special votes?

## Claim: Santiago Peña caused the Ueno collapse and faces up to 20 years in prison

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Paraguayan prosecutors for the charges and maximum exposure; political opponents for culpability claims.
- **Why it matters:** The distinction between charge and guilt is central to the legitimacy of the transition and criminal process.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Prosecutors charged Peña with breach of trust and administration for personal gain and sought asset restraint and detention.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** A charge and detention order are not conviction. The inspected report does not include the charging instrument, underlying transactions, defense evidence or a judicial merits finding. Peña denies wrongdoing.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for the procedural facts; no adjudication of guilt.
- **Current assessment:** supported that he was charged and detained; unsupported that guilt is established.
- **Confidence:** high on procedure; low on culpability.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/paraguay-president-pena-ueno-bank-0a3cddd03f9fb36c870fa3117aeeb4ac).
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain the formal charging document and distinguish maximum aggregate penalty from likely sentence.
