## Claim: Trump ordered or nearly ordered strikes on the Houthis, then stopped them

- **Origin / principal promoters:** New York Times reporting repeated by Reuters; Axios separately reported deliberation.
- **Why it matters:** It bears on how close Washington came to direct entry into the renewed Yemen war and on the operational credibility of U.S. restraint.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Reuters says the Times reported bombs being loaded before Trump changed course. Axios says Trump weighed strikes and held off.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No inspected White House, Pentagon or Saudi primary statement confirms an order, target package, launch time or cancellation.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial at the level of deliberation; no public confirmation of the strongest operational detail.
- **Current assessment:** plausible.
- **Confidence:** medium-low.
- **Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/21/trump-houthis-yemen-saudi-bin-salman) supports deliberation; [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/houthis-push-for-control-of-yemen-highlands-as-trump-is-said-to-have-called-off-strikes-ce785adbdd8df624) attributes the last-minute account to the Times.
- **Questions for auditor:** What was authorized, if anything? Did military preparations proceed beyond planning?

## Claim: The Houthis are taking the Kahboub Mountains and can lock in control of Yemen's Red Sea coast

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Five Yemeni military sources quoted by Reuters; analytical framing in Reuters.
- **Why it matters:** Control of the heights could determine whether the coastal advance is reversible and whether Bab al-Mandab pressure becomes durable.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Reports of fighting near named districts and a stated Houthi push toward the heights.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No inspected imagery, geolocation or public map establishes current control. “Could become critical” is an assessment, not a verified territorial fact.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for fighting; no independent confirmation of seizure.
- **Current assessment:** supported as an attempted advance; unclear as control or decisive effect.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/houthis-push-for-control-of-yemen-highlands-as-trump-is-said-to-have-called-off-strikes-ce785adbdd8df624) supports the source account and geography.
- **Questions for auditor:** Which heights changed hands by cutoff? Can imagery or local reporting confirm positions?

## Claim: Pakistan's September 21 strikes killed 28 militants

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Pakistan's Information Ministry and security officials.
- **Why it matters:** The militant count is central to Pakistan's claim of a calibrated counterterrorism action rather than indiscriminate interstate force.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Pakistan says the sites were safe havens linked to recent attacks and reports 28 militant deaths.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No names, target packet or independent casualty audit was inspected. AP images show destroyed homes; UNAMA confirmed at least three civilian deaths and four injuries.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for 28 militants; yes for civilian harm and the occurrence of airstrikes.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** high that the public evidence does not establish 28 militants; low on the true militant toll.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/pakistan-carried-out-strikes-inside-afghanistan-95178c5833c7a6a621c9bb0f06a78915) distinguishes Pakistan's claim from UNAMA's finding.
- **Questions for auditor:** Did Pakistan release target or post-strike evidence? Did Taliban or local authorities publish names?

## Claim: North Korea tested a new hypersonic system with a 908.2 km range

- **Origin / principal promoters:** KCNA and North Korean display graphics.
- **Why it matters:** A proven manoeuvring short-range system could complicate allied missile defence and affect deterrence planning.
- **Evidence offered for it:** KCNA images identify Hwasong-11Ma and show speed, altitude and range figures; the missiles have conical, finned vehicles consistent with previously described systems.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** South Korea tracked two flights at about 450 km and 600 km, not 908.2 km, and says it is still assessing the claim. Public imagery alone does not prove sustained hypersonic manoeuvre.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for launches; no for performance or novelty.
- **Current assessment:** supported as a North Korean claim; unclear as tested performance and new capability.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/north-korea-missile-kim-hypersonic-27b540de28525e4bf23c89b90991393d) reports the primary claim and allied tracking discrepancy.
- **Questions for auditor:** Is the displayed 908.2 km a planned trajectory, telemetry figure or propaganda graphic? What did Japan track?

## Claim: The German state results show AfD is about to take national power

- **Origin / principal promoters:** AfD's own momentum framing and commentary after repeated state wins.
- **Why it matters:** It affects expectations about Germany's position on Russia, Ukraine, the EU and immigration.
- **Evidence offered for it:** AfD topped Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, its second state victory within a month, and leads some national polls.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** AfD fell short of a state majority; other major parties still refuse coalition; state results do not mechanically translate into a federal majority. No leadership change occurred by cutoff.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for results, not the forecast.
- **Current assessment:** weak as an imminent-power claim; supported as evidence of growing national pressure.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/merz-clings-on-after-german-election-disaster-ce785adbda8cf225) supports both the gains and the firewall constraint.
- **Questions for auditor:** What do current national coalition arithmetic and polling show? Are any major parties changing the firewall?

## Claim: United Russia's 57.83% proves majority support for the war

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Kremlin framing of the election as a test of unity and war support.
- **Why it matters:** The inference is used to claim domestic legitimacy for continued war.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Near-final official results give United Russia 57.83%, 355 seats and record official turnout.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The anti-war national list was excluded; many opponents were barred, imprisoned or exiled; no OSCE mission observed; voting included occupied Ukrainian territory. The official count measures the administered election, not free opinion.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for official numbers; no for the preference inference or integrity.
- **Current assessment:** weak.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/pro-putin-party-keeps-grip-on-parliament-anti-war-party-gains-tiny-regional-foothold-ce785adbda81fe21) supports the result and restrictions; [EU statement list](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/?CouncilConfiguration=122512) records nonrecognition of voting on Ukrainian territory.
- **Questions for auditor:** What can be said about institutional continuity without translating the result into free public support?

## Claim: The United States is about to sanction the ICC as an institution

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Anonymous Dutch, court and U.S. officials quoted by AP.
- **Why it matters:** Institution-level measures could impair payroll, IT, banking, witness protection and detention operations.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Dutch officials reportedly received warnings and are preparing mitigation; the U.S. has already sanctioned numerous court personnel.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Two U.S. officials said scale and scope were not finalized and no announcement was expected this week. No draft instrument was inspected.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for preparations; no for the final policy.
- **Current assessment:** plausible as contingency planning; unsupported as a finalized measure.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/icc-trump-sanctions-eu-israel-netherlands-2c1cc314732f920c2de59396d3b556f6) contains both the warning and the narrowing U.S. caveat.
- **Questions for auditor:** Is “institution-level” sourced or inferred? Which legal authority and targets are under discussion?
