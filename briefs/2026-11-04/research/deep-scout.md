## Candidate: DS-01 — Greenland approval advances military access, but does not activate the agreement

- **Strategic mechanism:** ASSESSMENT: a further approval gate has cleared for a larger US Arctic presence. The bargain connects military access to local economic benefits and retained sovereignty; implementation can therefore turn on technical terms and local contracting, not just high-level alignment.
- **What evidence is observable now:** The older September 22 agreement is inspected context. Article IV allows modernization at Pituffik and an additional Defense Area at Narsarsuaq and Mestersvig under mutually agreed modalities. Reuters says Denmark's vote remains pending. These records establish agreed text and an approval step, not construction or deployment.
- **What remains inference:** How quickly access becomes capability; whether investment screening excludes particular projects; whether new facilities receive budgets. Countercase: approval could mostly regularize existing strategic cooperation while implementation remains slow. No evidence here establishes annexation, new operating bases, or an active Golden Dome installation.
