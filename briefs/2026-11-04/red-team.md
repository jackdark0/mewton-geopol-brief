## Finding: The Israel-aid headline can outrun the operative record

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** "Trump announces an end-of-2026 cutoff" and any final wording that treats aid as legally terminated.
- **Problem:** The record establishes a presidential statement and an existing MOU subject to appropriations. It does not contain an Israeli confirmation, rescission, budget request, statutory change, or treatment of existing obligations.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** The same Fox interview is the origin for the deadline and the claim of Netanyahu's consent. Reuters and ABC independently report the speech, not the private agreement. The MOU continues through FY2028 and Congress controls appropriations.
- **Best competing interpretation:** Trump announced a political objective or bargaining position that may be narrowed during implementation rather than a completed bilateral-policy change.
- **Required editorial action:** **qualify.** Use "announced" or "said the package would end" in the headline and first sentence. State that the implementing mechanism and Israeli confirmation were absent. Do not say aid "ends" as an accomplished fact.
