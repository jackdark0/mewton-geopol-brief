## Story: Trump announces an end-of-2026 cutoff for annual US military aid to Israel

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED]** In a November 3 *Fox & Friends* interview, President Donald Trump said the roughly $4 billion annual package would stop at the end of 2026 while Israel could continue buying US weapons. The original broadcaster's report preserves the statement; Reuters and ABC separately reported the same public interview ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-says-israel-stop-receiving-4b-us-aid-years-end-can-still-buy-weapons), [Reuters](https://new.streetinsider.com/Reuters/As%2BAmericans%2Bvote,%2BTrump%2Bsays%2BUS%2Bwill%2Bstop%2B$4%2Bbillion%2Bin%2Baid%2Bto%2BIsrael/27278003.html), [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/amp/Politics/trump-israel-aid-end-year-citing-political-reasons/story?id=137809564)).
- **[CONFIRMED]** The standing 2016 memorandum covers fiscal years 2019-2028 and sets $3.3 billion a year in Foreign Military Financing plus $500 million for cooperative missile defense, subject to congressional appropriations ([US-Israel memorandum](https://2009-2017.state.gov/documents/organization/265160.pdf?safe=1), [Congressional Research Service](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R44245/R44245.127.pdf)).
- **[SUPPORTED]** Trump said Netanyahu raised the idea and that they had made a deal months earlier. Netanyahu had publicly said in May that he wanted aid to draw down to zero, but no inspected Israeli statement or implementing text confirmed the end-2026 agreement or its exact scope ([Fox News, May interview report](https://www.foxnews.com/media/netanyahu-reveals-he-wants-american-financial-support-israel-draw-down-zero)).

### Material claims not established

- The announcement does not itself establish that Congress has rescinded or declined the relevant FY2027-FY2028 authorizations or appropriations.
- It does not establish that every form of US security cooperation, missile-defense cooperation, stockpile access, or arms sales will end.
- The exact implementing mechanism, treatment of existing cases, and Israeli acceptance of the deadline were not published by the cutoff.

### Source provenance

- Fox is the speech origin. Reuters and ABC independently reported the same interview but do not provide independent evidence of the alleged private deal.
- The MOU and CRS report independently establish the existing framework and Congress's role; they do not show that Trump cannot seek a change.

### Open questions

- Will the administration send Congress a formal request, rescission, or FY2028 budget change?
- What happens to joint missile-defense funding and previously approved sales?
- Will Israel confirm the timing and scope?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Lead with the policy announcement and the implementation gap, not with a claim that aid has already ended.
