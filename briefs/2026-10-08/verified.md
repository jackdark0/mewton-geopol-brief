The authority said one attack occurred on 6 October and one on 7 October but did not map each airport to a date. [Reuters, updated 7 October 22:01 UTC](https://www.aol.com/articles/houthis-launch-missiles-yemens-aden-012102000.html)
- **[CLAIMED, moderate confidence]** The Saudi-led coalition said it struck more than 80 Houthi military sites in Saada, Hodeidah, Jawf and Marib. The inspected record establishes the coalition's claim, not 80 independently verified successful strikes. [Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/houthis-launch-missiles-yemens-aden-012102000.html)
- **[CONFIRMED at IOM-record scope, high confidence]** IOM's displacement tracker recorded approximately 200,161 people displaced between 1 August and 6 October and 201,217 since the June escalation. These are tracked displacement estimates, not a national census. [IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix](https://dtm.iom.int/taxonomy/term/14)
- **[CONFIRMED as a policy commitment, high confidence]** Türkiye, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan said on 5 October that they would move to practical implementation of their collective-defence commitments and prepare agreed forces and capabilities for rapid deployment, subject to national law. [Turkish Foreign Ministry joint statement](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/joint-statement-on-the-emergency-meeting-of-the-strategic-political-and-defense-committee-of-the-makkah-alliance-for-defense-5-october-2026.en.mfa)
- **[SUPPORTED, moderate-high confidence]** On 7 October, AK Party spokesman Ömer Çelik said the pact was expected to reach Turkey's parliament within days. The treaty was not yet ratified, and no inspected record establishes a Turkish combat deployment. [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/turkey-send-mecca-pact-parliament-opening-path-saudi-deployment)

### Material claims not established

- More than 80 sites were successfully destroyed or that the attacks materially reduced Houthi capability.
- Turkish ratification, force assignment, arrival, rules of engagement or participation in offensive operations in Yemen.
- Durable Yemeni-government control of Dhubab district, the eastern heights, Mocha or Bab el-Mandeb. Reuters could geolocate some combatant footage but could not verify filming date or extent of control.
- A verified casualty total for the wider Yemen campaign. Both sides make larger claims without adequate detail.

### Source provenance

- Reuters' Saudi/Yemen report combines Saudi aviation and coalition statements, Houthi claims, IOM data and named/unnamed reporting. Those are separate actor records, not mutual corroboration.
- IOM is an independent humanitarian tracking lineage for displacement only.
- The Turkish Foreign Ministry statement is a participant record. Al-Monitor directly reports Çelik's later domestic-process statement; it does not prove deployment.

### Open questions

- Which airport was hit on which date, and how long was either airport disrupted?
- What forces or systems will Turkey and Pakistan actually assign, under what command and mandate?
- What territory is held continuously around Dhubab, and can independent imagery show control rather than passage?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Lead with civilian aviation exposure and retaliation; treat alliance implementation and Turkey's ratification as the observable next gate. Keep territorial control and deployment claims explicitly unresolved.

That percentage applies to the 46 jet-powered drones, not all missiles and drones in the attack. [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/latest/At-least-32-killed-in-Ukraine-Zelenskiy-condemns-one-of-Russia-s-most-vile-strikes-12426983/)

The observed damage supports an air-defence-capacity problem but does not by itself identify the missing systems or interceptor inventory.

- That Russia intentionally targeted civilians rather than striking military/industrial targets with weapons that hit civilian areas.
- The cause of the reported improvement in jet-drone interception or whether the rate will persist.

### Source provenance

- The Reuters account uses Ukrainian emergency-service, police, air-force and presidential records plus Russia's Defence Ministry account. These are official belligerent sources with different competence and incentives.

### Open questions

- How many ballistic missiles were intercepted, by which systems, and at what interceptor cost?
- What energy services or industrial production were actually lost and for how long?
- Does the higher jet-drone interception rate survive another large attack?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Keep the casualty total tied to the pre-cutoff update and the 46-drone denominator explicit.

## Story: Bulgaria moved toward protection while rejecting premature Black Sea attribution

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED, high confidence]** Bulgaria halted the search for the crew of the sunken *Alfa Watan*. Maritime official Rumen Nikolov said 12 Syrian crew members were believed aboard; 12 missing is not 12 confirmed dead. [Reuters via Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/bulgaria-halts-rescue-operation-crew-sunken-ship)

[Darik](https://dariknews.bg/novini/bylgariia/radev-komunikaciia-s-ukrainskite-sluzhbi-kakvi-sa-dronovete-ne-e-imalo-kakvo-e-kazal-zelenski-si-e-za-negova-smetka-2468468)
- **[CONFIRMED as the Ukrainian claim, high confidence]** Zelenskyy's 6 October address said Ukrainian and Bulgarian naval contacts had established a combined Russian maritime-and-aerial drone attack. The statement is a direct primary record of Kyiv's claim, not proof of the operator. [Ukrainian presidency](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/ye-onovlena-informaciya-vid-rozvidki-pro-pidgotovku-rosiyana-106769)
- **[SUPPORTED, moderate-high confidence]** Bulgaria's president proposed consultations with Romania and Turkey and possible requests for added allied surveillance and counter-drone capabilities. Radev said the government would approve five more surface-to-air missile systems. These are proposed consultations and a purchase-approval step, not deployed capability. [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/bulgaria-halts-rescue-operation-crew-sunken-ship)

### Material claims not established

- Russia or Ukraine operated the drones. No inspected debris, trajectory, radar, electronic intercept or command evidence publicly establishes the operator.
- That there was no Bulgaria-Ukraine contact of any kind. Radev's denial is narrower: he denied communication establishing drone identity.
- That the five air-defence systems have been contracted, delivered or made operational.
- That the incidents occurred in Bulgarian territorial waters or automatically trigger NATO collective defence. They occurred in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone/search-and-rescue area outside the 12-nautical-mile territorial sea.

### Source provenance

- The Ukrainian and Bulgarian statements are opposed participant accounts.
- Reuters independently reports the public decisions and interviews, but it has no separate forensic attribution stream.
- Darik and bTV both report Radev's remarks; they share the same underlying statement and are not two attribution investigations.

### Open questions

- Will satellite, debris, radar or electronic evidence identify launch platform and operator?
- What exactly was communicated between Ukrainian and Bulgarian officials?
- Will consultations produce a standing surveillance or counter-drone mission?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** The change is Bulgaria's protective response and the public break over attribution process, not a settled Russian finding.
