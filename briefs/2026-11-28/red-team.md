## Critical challenges and required corrections

### 1. Do not call the *Pacific Archer* a proven blockade runner

CENTCOM supplies the only evidence for departure from Bandar Abbas, warnings and noncompliance. Public tracking data add tension but do not resolve actual orders. The headline may say the United States disabled a merchant ship after it left Iran; the prose must say CENTCOM *alleges* or *says* it ran the blockade.

**Failure mode:** A compressed headline such as “U.S. disables Iranian blockade runner” would collapse flag, ownership, conduct and legal status into one unsupported label.

### 2. Do not imply the Cloud One intrusion reached named weapons or command systems

The reporting establishes that those applications use the platform, not that an intruder accessed or altered them. “A platform hosting sensitive applications was breached” is supported. “Hackers penetrated U.S. bomb tracking” is not.

**Failure mode:** Converting system context into breach scope, especially because readers will infer operational sabotage.

### 3. Keep the EU's political support separate from an adopted loan

The official result is 24-state support and a December return. The final should avoid a loan amount unless the precise proposal and legal instrument are directly cited. It must not conflate this unresolved package with previously adopted Ukraine lending.

**Failure mode:** “EU approves aid but delays payment” is wrong; approval itself is missing.

### 4. Ratification is not entry into force, and collective defence is not automatic combat

The Gazette proves Turkish approval; the committee statement proves implementation work. Neither proves that all treaty notifications are complete or that forces have been committed. The final should not repeat shorthand comparisons with NATO Article 5 unless clearly framed as commentary.

**Failure mode:** “Türkiye is now obligated to fight the Houthis” outruns both the treaty record and domestic authorization.

### 5. New Zealand's arithmetic is verified; the foreign-policy consequence is not

Opportunity's leverage is a political assessment supported by AP, not a constitutional allocation of veto power. Coalition outcomes can change through abstention, confidence arrangements or new bargaining. Use “apparent gatekeeper” or “pivotal in the current arithmetic,” and keep Five Eyes implications in the watch section.

**Failure mode:** Treating a possible coalition role as a policy shift before negotiations produce terms.

### 6. Australia's sanctions are fact; the operational attribution is a government claim

The final must say Canberra *assesses* the two men organized the attacks for the IRGC. It should not call them “IRGC agents” as fact or imply a conviction. The absence of public evidence lowers public confidence but does not refute the assessment.

**Failure mode:** Either laundering an intelligence claim into fact or dismissing it because sources and methods remain secret.
