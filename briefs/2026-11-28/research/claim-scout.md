## Claim: Hackers breached the Air Force's Cloud One platform and may have exposed sensitive military data

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Reuters, citing five unnamed people; Federal News Network, citing a person familiar with the incident.
- **Why it matters:** Cloud One supports personnel, logistics, maintenance, air-defense-feed and munitions-tracking applications. A control-plane breach could affect confidentiality and availability across many users.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Reuters reports disruption, paused migrations, blocked updates, revoked administrator rights and a suspected control-plane entry. Federal News Network separately confirms a breach and some administrative disruption.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The Air Force neither confirmed nor denied the breach and did not disclose data loss or operational effects. Actor, vulnerability, duration and exfiltration are unknown.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial. FNN says its source confirmed some details after Reuters's report; source independence cannot be proven.
- **Current assessment:** supported that a breach occurred; unclear whether sensitive data were accessed or operations compromised.
- **Confidence:** medium-high on breach; low on impact.
- **Sources:** [Reuters copy](https://new.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Hackers%2Bbreach%2BUS%2BAir%2BForce%2Bflagship%2BCloud%2BOne%2Bcomputing%2Bplatform/27405284.html); [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/air-force/2026/11/hackers-breach-air-forces-cloud-one-computing-environment/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Which facts are independently sourced? Is there an incident number, contractor disclosure or Cyber Command confirmation?

## Claim: *Pacific Archer* was trying to breach the U.S. blockade of Iranian ports

- **Origin / principal promoters:** U.S. Central Command.
- **Why it matters:** The claim is the stated predicate for a U.S. aircraft firing on and disabling a civilian merchant ship in the Gulf of Oman.
- **Evidence offered for it:** CENTCOM says the ship left Bandar Abbas, failed to comply and was struck after warnings. The strike and disabled propulsion are acknowledged by the actor that carried it out.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** AP reports public tracking listed Singapore as the destination. No crew, owner, flag-state, cargo, AIS track or warning record was inspected.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for the strike; no for intent or noncompliance.
- **Current assessment:** supported that the U.S. disabled the ship; unclear that the ship was attempting a blockade breach as characterized.
- **Confidence:** high on the use of force; low-to-medium on the justification.
- **Sources:** [AP dedicated report](https://apnews.com/article/iran-naval-blockade-us-military-disabled-ship-800620518a351282f098faa9381b3375).
- **Questions for auditor:** What did AIS and the voyage plan show? Were warnings recorded? What law and geographic blockade limits did CENTCOM invoke?

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Australian Foreign Minister Penny Wong and the Australian government.

- **Current assessment:** supported that Australia made the assessment and imposed sanctions; the underlying operational attribution remains unverified publicly.
- **Confidence:** high on official action; low-to-medium on factual attribution.
- **Sources:** [Australian foreign minister](https://www.foreignminister.gov.au/minister/penny-wong/media-release/further-action-against-irans-irgc-attacks-australia); [DFAT Consolidated List](https://www.dfat.gov.au/international-relations/security/sanctions/consolidated-list).

## Claim: Russia proposed an oil-strike pause while Ukraine proposed a Black Sea ceasefire

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha, as reported by Reuters.
- **Why it matters:** The claim suggests a narrow bargaining track over Black Sea shipping and the two sides' asymmetric energy-strike leverage.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Sybiha publicly described Ukraine's offer and Russia's reported condition; Ukraine has previously supported a reciprocal energy ceasefire.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No Russian statement accepting or describing the terms was inspected. The wording, scope, duration and verification arrangements are unknown.
- **Independent corroboration:** no; Gulf Times and BGNES are the same Reuters/Sybiha lineage.
- **Current assessment:** supported that Ukraine advanced or publicized these terms; unclear that Russia made a formal proposal or accepts reciprocity.
- **Confidence:** medium on Ukraine's position; low-to-medium on Russia's.
- **Sources:** [Gulf Times PDF](https://www.gulf-times.com/gulftimes/uploads/pdf/2026/11/28/main-20261128-1-1795817076.pdf?ts=1795817076); [BGNES](https://www.bgnes.com/conflicts/ukraine-proposes-black-sea-ceasefire-as-russia-demands-halt-to-oil-attacks); [OSCE record](https://resources.osce.org/sites/default/files/documents/official_documents/2026/10/pcdel1022%20ukraine%201581pc.pdf).
- **Questions for auditor:** Retrieve Sybiha's exact words and a Russian response; determine what is genuinely new.

## Claim: The Mecca pact now obliges Turkey and Pakistan to defend Saudi Arabia against Houthi attacks

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Pakistani and Turkish officials; AP characterization of the Joint Mecca Defense Agreement.
- **Why it matters:** It affects deterrence, alliance entanglement and possible foreign deployments in the Saudi-Houthi confrontation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Erdogan completed Turkey's approval; officials met under the pact and reaffirmed support for Saudi security. AP says the agreement treats an attack on one party as an attack on all and records prior rapid-deployment language.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The treaty text, entry-into-force notice, automatic-response language, command arrangements and actual deployment are not public in the inspected sources. Turkish officials say no troops would be sent to fight in Yemen.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for ratification and meeting; not for operational obligations.
- **Current assessment:** supported that domestic ratification and committee activity advanced; unclear whether an automatic military obligation or deployable force exists.
- **Confidence:** high on institutional steps; low-to-medium on military effect.
- **Sources:** [Turkish Foreign Ministry](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/no_-231_-ortak-savunma-icin-mekke-anlasmasi-kapsaminda-tesis-edilen-stratejik-siyasi-ve-savunma-komitesi-nin-ikinci-toplantisi-hk.en.mfa); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/pakistan-saudi-turkey-meeting-trilateral-alliance-6c17e4827e5aef3e69f78cdb01d597dc).
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain the Official Gazette and joint communique; distinguish approval, entry into force, alliance obligation and deployment.

## Claim: The drone that entered Latvia was Russian

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Inference from geography and the continuing Russia-Ukraine war; the inspected Latvian statement does not attribute it.
- **Why it matters:** Russian attribution would make the event a direct NATO airspace violation; accidental Ukrainian or civilian origin would carry different escalation implications.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The drone moved near Latvia's eastern border during an active regional war, and Latvia has experienced prior Russian-origin incursions.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The November 27 statement identifies no model, route, wreckage, operator or country of origin. It says only that a drone briefly entered Latvian territory.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** weak as an attribution; confirmed only as an unidentified drone incursion.
- **Confidence:** high that origin is unresolved.
- **Sources:** [Latvian Ministry of Defence](https://www.mod.gov.lv/lv/zinas/nacionalie-brunotie-speki-informe-par-gaisa-telpas-apdraudejumu-ludzas-un-balvu-novados).
- **Questions for auditor:** Did Latvia issue a later identification before cutoff? Was the track observed from Russian or Belarusian airspace?

## Claim: China's November 27 patrol was election interference

- **Origin / principal promoters:** The temporal framing in media and broader Taiwanese allegations of Chinese electoral interference.
- **Why it matters:** It changes the event from routine coercive signaling into an attempt to influence democratic choice.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Taiwan reported a 30-aircraft patrol on the final campaign day; 26 aircraft reportedly crossed the median line or extension.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Similar patrols occur regularly, including one six days earlier. No inspected Beijing statement ties the sortie to the election, and Taiwan's local vote centered mainly on domestic issues.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for activity; no for motive.
- **Current assessment:** plausible timing signal but unproven motive.
- **Confidence:** medium that the timing was politically salient; low that electoral influence was the demonstrated purpose.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/taiwan-local-elections-a7e3622548f69a41da3e1ec6ce9c9f19); [Taiwan Defence report](https://taiwandefence.com/article/chinese-warplanes-and-warships-stage-a-joint-patrol-around-taiwan-on-the-eve-of-its-local-elections).
- **Questions for auditor:** Compare sortie patterns with a 2026 baseline and search Chinese official messaging for a linkage.
