## V1 - Iran: verified claim ledger

### Admitted facts

1. On November 9, Trump said the war was “militarily ... effectively over” and said new U.S. strikes did not appear necessary at that point. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-trump-qatar-c38770b168faf5e094e96c1e1e212cff); [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-says-he-will-hold-fresh-iran-strikes-now).
2. The United States continued its naval blockade and Trump retained the option of future strikes. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-trump-qatar-c38770b168faf5e094e96c1e1e212cff); [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-says-he-will-hold-fresh-iran-strikes-now).
3. Reuters reported that Washington had rejected Iran's existing truce proposal and that no settlement was imminent. [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-says-he-will-hold-fresh-iran-strikes-now).
4. Qatar had publicly described mediation as an effort to bridge gaps, not a completed agreement. [Qatar Foreign Ministry](https://mofa.gov.qa/en/qatar/latest-articles/latest-news/details/2026/11/03/advisor-to-prime-minister--official-spokesperson-for-ministry-of-foreign-affairs--qatar-continues-its-efforts-to-bridge-gap-between-washington--tehran).

### Not admitted

- That the war is durably over.
- That either side accepted a common text.
- That the blockade ended or that future strikes are ruled out.

### Source independence

AP and Reuters independently reported the appearance, but both derive the central signal from the same public speaker. Reuters adds separate reporting on mediator and Iranian positions. This is two-source reporting, not two independent proofs of policy durability.

## V2 - France cyber attribution: verified claim ledger

### Admitted facts

1. France publicly attributed the October 11-12 refrigeration-controller campaign to Iran and an IRGC-linked unit on November 9. [France Diplomatie](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/presse-et-ressources/decouvrir-et-informer/actualites/declaration-d-attribution-a-l-iran-de-cyberattaques-visant-la-france); [ANSSI](https://cyber.gouv.fr/actualites/campagnes-de-d%C3%A9stabilisation-et-sabotage-men%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-lencontre-des-int%C3%A9r%C3%AAts-fran%C3%A7ais-par-la-r%C3%A9publique-islamique-diran/).
2. ANSSI said more than 700 retail sites were affected and at least 15 fires occurred. [ANSSI](https://cyber.gouv.fr/actualites/campagnes-de-d%C3%A9stabilisation-et-sabotage-men%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-lencontre-des-int%C3%A9r%C3%AAts-fran%C3%A7ais-par-la-r%C3%A9publique-islamique-diran/).
3. France summoned Iran's ambassador and said it would propose EU sanctions. [France Diplomatie](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/presse-et-ressources/decouvrir-et-informer/actualites/declaration-d-attribution-a-l-iran-de-cyberattaques-visant-la-france).
4. AP independently confirms that France made the attribution and reports the physical effects, but its attribution evidence still comes from French authorities. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/france-cyberattack-supermarket-refrigerators-iran-6914ecf1a3285291653e103655f9e4a4).

### Not admitted

- That public evidence alone proves a direct order from Iran's political leadership.
- That 700 fires occurred.
- That the EU had adopted the proposed sanctions by cutoff.

### Verdict

**Official attribution: supported. Independent proof of state direction: incomplete.**

[Global Affairs Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2026/11/minister-anand-announces-additional-sanctions-to-reduce-russias-missile-capabilities-and-raise-economic-costs.html).

### Japan-Vietnam landing craft

Japan's Defense Ministry said the two governments would study possible cooperation on high-speed landing craft and continue vice-ministerial talks in December. This is exploratory, not an acquisition or production agreement. [Japan Ministry of Defense](https://www.mod.go.jp/en/article/2026/11/944820541eaa85daf05c0b2efba6adb52bc0f474.html).

### Piracy warning

AP reports Operation Atalanta's count of 79 attacks since late 2023 and roughly 30 since the Iran war began; AP separately tracked six hijackings from April through August. The totals use different periods and possibly different definitions, so no rate or percentage is calculated. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/piracy-shipping-aden-eu-atalanta-somalia-mandeb-27bc3f388afd489c1c0d189037eb8995).

### South Africa arrests

South African authorities arrested nine foreign nationals late November 8 on suspicion that they intended to travel to Russia for military recruitment. The reported record did not establish nationality, recruiter, belligerent, or guilt. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/south-africa-russia-ukraine-war-recruits-arrest-2cc387cac06f3663eb4c817194b7ed8c).

- “The Iran war is over.”
- “France proved publicly that Iran's leadership ordered 700 fires.”
