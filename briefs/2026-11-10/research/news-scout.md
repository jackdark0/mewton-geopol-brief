## Candidate: Trump says new Iran strikes are unnecessary for now while keeping the blockade

- **Event time/date:** November 9, after a White House meeting with Qatar's prime minister.
- **Region / actors:** United States, Iran, Qatar, Gulf states.
- **What changed:** Trump said the Iran war was "militarily ... effectively over" and that fresh U.S. strikes did not appear necessary at this point. He kept the U.S. naval blockade in place and retained the option to strike if circumstances change. Reuters reports that Washington still rejects Iran's existing truce proposal and that officials do not expect an imminent settlement.

- **Source chain:** AP and Reuters independently reported the public remarks; both rely on the same underlying Trump appearance for the core statement. Reuters separately reported mediator and Iranian positions.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-trump-qatar-c38770b168faf5e094e96c1e1e212cff) supports the remarks, force posture, and continuing blockade; [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-says-he-will-hold-fresh-iran-strikes-now) supports the temporary hold, rejected Iranian plan, and mediator positions; [Qatar Foreign Ministry](https://mofa.gov.qa/en/qatar/latest-articles/latest-news/details/2026/11/03/advisor-to-prime-minister--official-spokesperson-for-ministry-of-foreign-affairs--qatar-continues-its-efforts-to-bridge-gap-between-washington--tehran) establishes that Qatar was exchanging proposals but had not announced a breakthrough.
- **Questions for auditor:** Did Washington issue any operational stand-down? Is there a common text or reciprocal first step? Which blockade measures remain active?
