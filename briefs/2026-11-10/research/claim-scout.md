## Claim 1: “The Iran war is effectively over.”

**Claimant and wording:** President Donald Trump said the war was “militarily ... effectively over,” as reported by [AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-trump-qatar-c38770b168faf5e094e96c1e1e212cff) and [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-says-he-will-hold-fresh-iran-strikes-now).

### Atomic subclaims

1. Trump said fresh U.S. strikes did not appear necessary for now. **Supported.**
2. The United States ended its blockade. **Contradicted:** both reports say it remains.
3. Washington and Tehran accepted a common settlement. **Unsupported before cutoff:** Reuters reports Washington rejected Iran's existing plan; [Qatar](https://mofa.gov.qa/en/qatar/latest-articles/latest-news/details/2026/11/03/advisor-to-prime-minister--official-spokesperson-for-ministry-of-foreign-affairs--qatar-continues-its-efforts-to-bridge-gap-between-washington--tehran) describes continued mediation.
4. Further U.S. strikes are impossible. **Unsupported:** Trump retained the option.

**Evidence for:** a public U.S. signal against immediate new strikes; a high-level Qatar meeting; reported market response to lower strike risk.
**Evidence against or limiting:** continued blockade, no common text, rejected Iranian proposal, and an explicitly retained strike option.
**Verdict:** **Qualified.** The statement supports a near-term pause in new U.S. strikes, not a durable end to the confrontation.
**Confidence:** high on the public signal; low on durability.

## Claim 2: Iran directed destructive cyberattacks on French supermarkets.

**Claimant:** The French government and ANSSI.

### Atomic subclaims

1. Refrigeration controllers at more than 700 French retail sites were affected on October 11-12. **Supported by [ANSSI](https://cyber.gouv.fr/actualites/campagnes-de-d%C3%A9stabilisation-et-sabotage-men%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-lencontre-des-int%C3%A9r%C3%AAts-fran%C3%A7ais-par-la-r%C3%A9publique-islamique-diran/) and reported by [AP](https://apnews.com/article/france-cyberattack-supermarket-refrigerators-iran-6914ecf1a3285291653e103655f9e4a4).**
2. At least 15 fires occurred. **Supported by ANSSI; this is not 700 fires.**
3. France officially attributed the campaign to Iran and the IRGC-linked Shahid Kaveh unit. **Supported by [France's Foreign Ministry](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/presse-et-ressources/decouvrir-et-informer/actualites/declaration-d-attribution-a-l-iran-de-cyberattaques-visant-la-france) and ANSSI.**
4. Publicly released evidence independently proves a direct Iranian state order. **Not established.** AP's reported indicators support France's case, but no independent reproduction of the full forensic chain was found.

**Evidence for:** official technical attribution, an identified unit, Iran-linked infrastructure and Persian-language artifacts reported by AP, and a defined physical-effects pattern.
**Evidence against or limiting:** the underlying forensic record is incomplete in public; attribution and state authorization are not the same proposition.
**Verdict:** **Supported as an official French attribution; independently unconfirmed at the state-direction level.**
**Confidence:** high that France made the attribution and the incidents occurred; moderate that the public record alone establishes Iranian direction.

## Claim 3: China is rehearsing how to block outside intervention around Taiwan.

**Claimant:** Taiwan Defense Minister Wellington Koo, reported by [Reuters](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/taiwan-says-china-is-conducting-area-denial-drills-in-western-pacific-ce785fd3d081ff23).

### Atomic subclaims

1. The Liaoning and associated PLA formations were active in the western Pacific. **Supported by Taiwan's observations and prior deployment reporting.**
2. The activity constituted area-denial training. **Claimed by Taiwan; not independently verified from a published PLA plan.**
3. China announced a new named exercise. **Unsupported before cutoff.**
4. China characterized the deployment as routine annual training. **Supported as China's earlier stated position, reported by [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/politics/202610290014).**

**Evidence for:** force composition and location can fit an access-denial mission; Taiwan's defense minister made the assessment publicly.
**Evidence against or limiting:** no PLA exercise objectives were published, and routine training is a competing explanation.
**Verdict:** **Unclear.** The deployment is real; the operational purpose remains an assessed intent.
**Confidence:** high on deployment; low-to-moderate on purpose.
