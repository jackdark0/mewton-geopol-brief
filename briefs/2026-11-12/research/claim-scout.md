## Claim: Iran destroyed the navigation systems on Didamar Island

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Iranian state media, citing an IRGC strike.
- **Why it matters:** Destruction could impair infrastructure used to restore Gulf oil flows and would test the G7's maritime response.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Three informed sources confirmed an Iranian attack; Reuters verified smoke in video and thermal activity in Copernicus imagery.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Reuters could not identify what burned or whether systems were destroyed; no damage assessment exists; other maritime sources said redundant systems should limit shipping effects; Oman and CENTCOM did not comment.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial on an attack; no on destruction or operational impact.
- **Current assessment:** supported that an attack occurred; unclear that navigation systems were destroyed.
- **Confidence:** medium-high / low respectively.
- **Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/iran-attacks-shipping-navigation-systems-on-oman-s-didamar-island-sources-say-ce785edbde80f420).
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain operator statements, imagery, service status, and shipping-flow data.

## Claim: Indonesia's vice-presidential resignation proves Widodo directed the wiretap operation

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A political inference from Gibran's resignation, the Prabowo-Widodo split, and suspects' ties to the former president.
- **Why it matters:** It would turn a succession crisis into an allegation of former-presidential involvement in illegal surveillance.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Senior police officials were implicated; a former police chief close to Widodo was detained; the political alliance is breaking.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Investigators had not identified the origin of the order, Widodo had not been implicated, and the credential case was separate from the wiretap investigation.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unsupported.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/indonesia-prabowo-wiretap-police-jokowi-f51d8a85a34cb3f3db4640602d4c8de4); [AP resignation](https://apnews.com/article/indonesia-gibran-rakabuming-raka-resigns-abfe5e288fad3b8b26719eae634f2ee0).
- **Questions for auditor:** Preserve the separation between institutional ties, motive, and evidence of command.

## Claim: The Abu Dhabi talks put a broad peace deal within reach

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Optimistic descriptions by participants and commentary treating continued meetings as convergence.
- **Why it matters:** It changes public expectations about territorial concessions, guarantees, and war duration.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Direct talks occurred; the parties accepted a large POW exchange in principle; follow-on Putin and Zelenskyy meetings were announced.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No final exchange number, ceasefire sequence, territorial formula, guarantee architecture, or agreement on Europe's role existed at cutoff.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial. AP directly reported the outcome; participant statements share the same talks as provenance.
- **Current assessment:** weak as a peace-deal claim; supported as evidence of a functioning humanitarian channel.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-talks-kushner-witkoff-7c8d1024302e0c6ad8be064eec852a64); [UAE Foreign Ministry](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/MediaHub/News/2026/11/10/10-11-2026-UAE-Russia-Ukraine-USA).
- **Questions for auditor:** What was agreed verbatim, by whom, and with what implementation timetable?

## Claim: The G7 declaration means Trump's Iran war is ending multilaterally

- **Origin / principal promoters:** An inference from Trump's earlier strike-pause language plus the G7's November 11 declaration.
- **Why it matters:** It determines whether audiences understand the confrontation as de-escalating or becoming an enduring coalition enforcement regime.
- **Evidence offered for it:** No new U.S. strike was announced; G7 language calls the maritime initiatives defensive.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The G7 backed demining, escorts, verification, sanctions enforcement, and demands on Iran. No reciprocal settlement or blockade termination was announced.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes on published policy positions; no on the inferred end state.
- **Current assessment:** weak.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** [G7 declaration](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/joint-declaration-following-the-g7-foreign-ministers-meeting-in-paris); [AP on Trump's earlier signal](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-trump-qatar-c38770b168faf5e094e96c1e1e212cff).
- **Questions for auditor:** Identify any actual change in blockade enforcement or rules of engagement.

## Claim: China and ASEAN have completed 80% of an effective South China Sea code

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Three Southeast Asian diplomats cited by AP.
- **Why it matters:** A percentage can create false precision about progress on a flashpoint involving a U.S. treaty ally.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Negotiators reportedly provisionally agreed about 80% of draft paragraphs.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The text, paragraph denominator, legal force, geographic scope, and unresolved provisions are not public. Talks missed the agreed 2026 deadline.
- **Independent corroboration:** no public independent record located.
- **Current assessment:** plausible as a paragraph-count report; unclear and potentially misleading as a measure of substantive completion.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/south-china-sea-code-of-conduct-southeast-asia-8ef757e6ed486a5632e4c2299ee00064).
- **Questions for auditor:** What share of decisive clauses remains unresolved? Do not convert paragraph share into conflict-risk reduction.

## Claim: Colom's Mississippi victory is final and gives Democrats 55 Senate seats

- **Origin / principal promoters:** AP's projection, local reporting, and derivative reports supplying a national seat count.
- **Why it matters:** The incoming majority affects oversight, appointments, sanctions, treaties, and war-powers politics.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Colom led by 4,669 votes in AP's inspected count; AP estimated fewer than 12,000 ballots remained; Hyde-Smith conceded.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** County certification was due November 13; the complete national seat denominator and treatment of independents were not established by the Mississippi race report.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes on the projection and concession; partial on the national seat count.
- **Current assessment:** supported as a projected Colom win; unclear as an exact 55-seat claim until the whole incoming Senate is reconstructed.
- **Confidence:** high / medium respectively.
- **Sources:** [AP race-call explanation](https://apnews.com/article/mississippi-senate-colom-hydesmith-midterm-7f098b8834f598c923ba73572ea2223f); [Mississippi Today](https://mississippitoday.org/2026/11/11/scott-colom-wins-senate-mississippi-hyde-smith/).
- **Questions for auditor:** Use projection language and define caucus/party denominator.

## Claim: South Sudan's December election will cause a return to large-scale war

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Warnings from the AU envoy and analysts; the strongest formulation is a forecast, not an established fact.
- **Why it matters:** It affects preventive diplomacy and whether the vote is framed as democratic consolidation or a peace-agreement rupture.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Dissolution of the transitional government, Machar's detention, missing registration dates, limited opposition activity, continuing violence, and explicit AU/UN concern.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** South Sudan's government and election commission say the vote can consolidate peace and promise impartiality; no inevitable causal chain is proved.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial. Multiple actors identify risk, but they assess a common institutional record.
- **Current assessment:** plausible risk; unsupported as an inevitable outcome.
- **Confidence:** high on elevated risk, low on a categorical forecast.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/south-sudan-election-kiir-machar-2f416a2ec9a0d6e11f492a19f7418154).
- **Questions for auditor:** Separate preparation failures from violence forecasts; locate the UN report and election law.

## Claim: NATO allies added more than $250 billion of real defense capability in 2025-26

- **Origin / principal promoters:** NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte.
- **Why it matters:** The figure is used to judge burden sharing and deterrence capacity.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Rutte's primary-source speech says European allies and Canada increased investment by more than $250 billion across the two years.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Currency basis, inflation, baseline, country contributions, appropriations versus outlays, and delivered capability are not supplied in the speech; Rutte separately said demand exceeds supply.
- **Independent corroboration:** no arithmetic replication located.
- **Current assessment:** supported as NATO's stated nominal investment figure; unclear as delivered real capability.
- **Confidence:** high / low respectively.
- **Sources:** [NATO transcript](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/transcripts/2026/11/11/keynote-speech-by-the-nato-secretary-general-at-the-atlantic-future-forum).
- **Questions for auditor:** Find the table, denominator, price basis, and delivery record.
