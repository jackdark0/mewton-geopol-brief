## Finding: Do not turn Europe's absence into proof of a defective settlement

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** Abu Dhabi analysis: “excluding Europe can ... weaken implementation credibility.”
- **Problem:** The mechanism is plausible, but no settlement exists and no verified list of guarantor obligations exists. Europe could be consulted through separate formats and join implementation later.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** AP establishes that European, EU, and NATO officials were outside the trilateral room and that Russia opposed their direct participation. It does not establish that Europeans lacked influence or would reject later obligations.
- **Best competing interpretation:** A smaller core format may be a sequencing choice that permits candid bargaining while European consultations run in parallel.
- **Required editorial action:** qualify. Present representation as an unresolved design problem, not an observed cause of negotiation failure.
