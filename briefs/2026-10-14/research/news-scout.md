## Method and timing controls

The repository inputs, role instructions, prior 2026-10-13 brief, and both Pangburn sibling standards were read. The prior brief supplied continuity questions only. The Empirical Fact-Checking System and Forensic Fact-Checking Replication Standard retain separate identities and numbering. The discovery pass records atomic event/statement propositions, source lineages, narrowing evidence, and open questions; confidence below concerns the basic event, not all attribution or strategic implications.

Live source pages were inspected. Search snippets and Just Security's roundup were used to discover underlying reports, not to establish their claims. Dates attached to syndicated Reuters text are checked against the version actually opened; newer navigation headlines are ignored. Explicit pre-cutoff timestamps are recorded where available. Unresolved versions are held, even when their facts might have been known before cutoff. No source inspected after retrieval is presumed to have been available before cutoff merely because it describes an earlier event.

Broad query families covered Oct. 13 international reporting, Ukraine/Russia, Iran/Saudi/Hormuz, EU accession, intelligence and sabotage, sanctions enforcement, India, China/Pacific, and legal proceedings. Reuters origin pages often failed to fetch; accessible full-text licensed relays are named as relays and never counted as new independent reporting.
