- **Current assessment:** **Weak as stated**; the inspected record supports a proposal and Ukrainian support, not demonstrated bilateral implementation. Pangburn disposition: **Unsupported**, not False. Do not call every subsequent attack a proven breach of agreed terms.
- **Confidence:** **High** that these inspected sources do not establish the proposition; **low** on any undisclosed agreement. Confidence describes the evidence classification, not certainty that secret assent was impossible.

## Claim: C03 — Washington deliberately stopped intelligence sharing to punish Ukraine for Russian refinery strikes

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A consequential inference circulating around Zelenskyy's warning complaint and reports of U.S. pressure. **Do not assign this stronger proposition to Zelenskyy's presidential address.** The directly inspected NPR interview reports a threatened cutoff; threat and implementation are separate atoms.
- **Why it matters:** A deliberate cutoff would be a major change in U.S. support and coercive diplomacy. Reporting a threat as an executed policy changes the public understanding of both Ukrainian vulnerability and U.S. conduct.
- **Evidence offered for it:** NPR directly interviewed Zelenskyy, who said the administration warned it could cut off intelligence unless refinery strikes stopped. The White House did not answer NPR's question about the threat, referring instead to Trump's continued-sharing statement. C02 supplies the real missing-warning complaint. These create a reasonable verification question, not proof of implementation or motive.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Trump publicly says intelligence sharing continues. Sybiha also says cooperation continues. AP reports an unnamed senior Ukrainian official describing a failure rather than a deliberate decision. No order, message, technical logs, responsible official, intelligence category, suspension interval, or evidence that the warning gap was punishment was retrieved. Broad continued cooperation does not rule out a narrower restriction; official denial is not a full audit of the system.
- **Independent corroboration:** **No** for deliberate implementation. **Partial** for pressure/threat reporting, with NPR's direct interview an identified claimant stream. Trump and Sybiha are distinct official statements, but both remain statements about undisclosed operations. The inspected Kyiv Post account cites the Guardian for Sybiha and is derivative on that component.
- **Current assessment:** **Weak as stated**; **plausible and directly attributed** that a threat was communicated, but deliberate execution remains **unclear**. Pangburn disposition on executed punishment: **Unsupported**. Publish that missing warnings and a threatened cutoff are reported while both governments say sharing continues.
- **Confidence:** **High** that the stronger causal proposition is not established by this packet; **medium** on the existence of the communicated threat; **low** on actual operational scope or cause of the gaps.
