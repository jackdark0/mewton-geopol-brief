## Audit controls

- Post-cutoff developments are excluded even when a current page now contains them. The edition is frozen at 02:00 EDT.
- Official statements establish what an institution said or did; they do not independently prove contested attribution, covert command, motive, or battlefield effect.
- Reuters syndications are treated as one Reuters lineage. AP copies are treated as one AP lineage. Allied recognition of one government's intelligence assessment is not independent intelligence corroboration.
- Verdict and confidence remain separate. `UNSUPPORTED` means the reviewed record does not establish a proposition at the stated scope; it does not mean the proposition has been disproved.
- Quantities below preserve their denominators. No selected-story count is converted into a rate about all current events, attacks, or claims.
