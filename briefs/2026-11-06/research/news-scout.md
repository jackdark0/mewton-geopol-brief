# News Scout — 2026-11-06

## Methodology and time boundary

- **Frozen research cutoff:** 2026-11-06 02:00:00 America/New_York (2026-11-06 07:00:00 UTC).
- **Approximate research window:** 2026-11-04 20:00:00 EST through the cutoff (2026-11-05 01:00:00 UTC through 2026-11-06 07:00:00 UTC).
- **No-post-cutoff rule:** Facts first published or learned after the cutoff are excluded from the evidence packet. A source page that later changes is usable only for the cutoff-safe material and timestamp recorded below. Ambiguous publication timing is marked as a hold rather than silently admitted.
- **Discovery method:** I inspected primary records when search surfaced them and otherwise opened the full wire-service or AP report. Search snippets, topic indexes, Wikipedia, automated summaries, and derivative rewrites were used only to discover leads. Each retained proposition is narrower than or equal to what the inspected source supports.
- **Evidence method:** Official claims establish that an institution made a claim, not that the underlying conduct occurred. Source lineages are labeled; multiple mirrors of one Reuters item count as one evidentiary chain. Verdict-like confidence labels describe the basic event, not downstream strategic effects.
- **Continuity:** `briefs/latest.md` was inspected only to identify ongoing threads. Every current fact below was re-retrieved. Yesterday's wording is not evidence.
- **Candidate universe:** 19 distinct developments were considered before pruning. The shortlist ranks 11 for source-auditor review. No regional quota was used.
