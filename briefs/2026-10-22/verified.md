- **CLAIM — medium confidence:** Saudi authorities said one person died and eight were injured in Dammam after a projectile fell, and that two people later died after an intercepted drone was headed toward Shaybah. These casualty and attribution details come through Saudi official accounts relayed by Reuters; this audit found no independent forensic confirmation. [Reuters](https://www.aol.ca/articles/us-preparing-issue-ordered-departure-185740000.html)
- **CLAIM — low-to-medium confidence:** Reuters reported that other US missions in the region were preparing family departures. At the cutoff, the audit found no comparably specific public order covering the wider Gulf.

### Not established

- That every US mission in the Gulf had issued an ordered departure by the cutoff.
- The weapon type, launch point, or complete casualty picture in the Saudi incidents.
- That the personnel order predicts an imminent US escalation rather than prudent force protection.

### Provenance and lineage

Reuters is the principal independent reporting lineage. Saudi casualty details originate with Saudi authorities; CNN is a second outlet for US staffing changes, not an independent forensic source for the strikes.

### Publication recommendation

**Publish.** Lead with the narrow formal order. Attribute incident details and keep the wider regional-departure claim provisional.
