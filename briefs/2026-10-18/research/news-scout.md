## Candidate: N01 — Romania reports shooting down two attack drones

- **Event time/date:** October 17, 2026, 18:01 and 18:05 Romanian local time, as reported in ministry release No. 337 dated October 17. Article body is dated October 17; the website footer says October 18 and must not be mistaken for an article update timestamp.
- **Region / actors:** Black Sea; Romania; unidentified drone operators.
- **What changed:** CLAIM: Romania's defence ministry says two Romanian F-16s destroyed two jet-powered Geran-family attack drones over Romanian territorial waters. The release places the shoot-downs southeast and east of Sulina. This is a specific reported defensive engagement; it does not establish who ordered the flights or their intended targets.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - ASSESSMENT: A kinetic response creates a more concrete escalation question than an unconfirmed airspace alert.
  - ASSESSMENT: The auditor should distinguish defence of territorial airspace from wider protection of commercial shipping.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** medium; a direct official operational record exists, but no independent radar, wreckage, or flight data were inspected.
- **Source chain:** Romanian Ministry of National Defence → its own operational statement. The operator's account is primary for what it claims, not independent confirmation of identification or intent.
- **Sources:** [Romanian defence ministry, October 17 update](https://www.mapn.ro/cpresa/19446_Informa%C8%9Bie-de-pres%C4%83-%E2%80%93-UPDATE) — inspected Romanian-language release; paragraphs beginning “Două aeronave F-16” and “Țintele neutralizate” support platform, location, and identification claims.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain a pre-cutoff timestamp/version if available and independent reporting. Does the identification have physical corroboration? Do not infer Russian command merely from the stated drone family. Check minute-level timing against the earlier release before using duration figures. Has NATO announced any new response, or is that still an inference?
