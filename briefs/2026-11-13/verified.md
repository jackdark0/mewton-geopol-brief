## 1. A next Ukraine negotiating round was announced with European participation, but the format is not settled

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED that U.S. and Ukrainian participants announced another round and said Europe would join. SUPPORTED, not confirmed by common text, that Moscow accepted the same format.
**Confidence:** High on the announcement and meetings; medium on Russian acceptance of the described format; low that the parties moved closer on substance.

### Atomic claims

- Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Vladimir Putin for about two hours and then Volodymyr Zelenskyy for more than an hour: **confirmed by direct participant statements reported by AP**.
- Witkoff and Zelenskyy said a next round had been agreed and Europe would participate: **confirmed as their public account**. No country list, representative, date, agenda, or common communiqué was published.
- Kushner said Putin was willing to try European participation: **confirmed as Kushner's account**, not independent evidence of Moscow's precise commitment.
- Putin renewed demands for NATO force rollback in Central and Eastern Europe and rejected European forces in postwar Ukraine: **confirmed as Putin's public position**.
- European leaders agreed that Ukraine chooses its alliances and that European security cannot be negotiated without Europe: **confirmed in the European Political Community chair's summary**. The meeting did not name a single negotiator.

**Best evidence:** [AP on the Moscow and Dublin meetings](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-putin-kushner-witkoff-683810203340077f33611fd1d1757b23); [AP on Europe's representation problem](https://apnews.com/article/dublin-epc-europe-ukraine-russia-war-2a96d57210055edd2e76f71693e0a37c); [European Political Community chair's summary](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/international-summit/2026/11/12/); [EU leaders' joint statement with Zelenskyy](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/12/joint-statement-by-president-costa-and-president-von-der-leyen-following-their-meeting-with-president-zelenskyy-in-dublin/).

**Not established:** that Europe will be a full party rather than an adjacent team; that Putin accepted a named representative; that a ceasefire, territorial formula, or guarantee text is close.

**Editorial recommendation:** Lead. Frame it as movement in negotiating architecture, not movement toward settlement.

## 2. Trump publicly removed Vance from Iran diplomacy as tanker attacks continued

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED that Trump redirected Vice President JD Vance to domestic work and publicly said the United States did not need an Iran deal. CONFIRMED that two tankers were struck by unknown projectiles in separate incidents. UNRESOLVED who attacked them.
**Confidence:** High on the statements and incidents; low on attacker identity and on whether every diplomatic channel has closed.

### Atomic claims

- Trump directed Vance away from Iran diplomacy and toward domestic corruption and crime: **confirmed by Trump's public post and a White House response reported by AP**.
- Trump said the United States did not need a deal with Iran and left open renewed strikes: **confirmed as his public position**. It is not proof that all indirect contacts ended.
- A tanker was struck in the Arabian Gulf on November 10; multiple casualties were taken ashore and the remaining crew were safe: **confirmed by UKMTO Warning 203-26**.
- A second tanker was struck in its engine room 14 nautical miles northwest of Ra's al Khaymah at 18:00 UTC on November 11; no casualties were reported and the crew evacuated: **confirmed by UKMTO Warning 204-26**.
- UKMTO described both projectiles as unknown: **confirmed**. Attribution to Iran, the United States, or another actor is unsupported in the inspected record.

**Best evidence:** [AP on Trump's order and Iran policy](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-vance-negotiations-d9671cbf66e3c2e337f4fb3485ad07d0); [UKMTO Warning 203-26](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261112-ukmto_warning_203-26.pdf?rev=724ce0c8e4a8458badd1f98205c92c4e); [UKMTO Warning 204-26](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261112-ukmto_warning_204-26-1.pdf?rev=ddd6808104d549258f9c47d71449746d); [JMIC incident summary through November 12](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/jmic_advisory_update_113_12-nov.pdf?rev=7fe7908661f24d2ebb037b750628f240).

**Not established:** that diplomacy has irreversibly ended; that either tanker was targeted by Iran; that the incidents caused a material change in Gulf throughput.

**Editorial recommendation:** Major story. Keep the political signal and the maritime facts adjacent but do not convert temporal proximity into attribution.

## 3. Ukraine and SpaceX gave incompatible accounts of Starshield restrictions

**Verdict:** DISPUTED. Ukraine says a 161 km/h limit returned in occupied areas and disrupted interception of jet-powered Shahed drones. SpaceX says there was no change.
**Confidence:** High that the dispute exists; medium that Ukrainian units experienced a material service problem; low on cause, configuration, scope, and intent.

### Atomic claims

- Zelenskyy said on November 12 that the 161 km/h limit had returned in occupied territory and affected drone interception: **confirmed as Ukraine's claim** in two presidential statements.
- SpaceX said, “This is false. There has been no change”: **confirmed as the company's denial**.
- Public evidence does not distinguish a new restriction from a previously configured limit, a regional service condition, or another technical fault: **confirmed evidentiary gap**.
- The claim that SpaceX intentionally restored the limit for political leverage: **unsupported**. No order, telemetry, contract instruction, or motive evidence was published.

**Best evidence:** [Ukraine presidential statement at 18:12](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/mi-znovu-zafiksuvali-obmezhennya-shvidkosti-starshield-na-na-107285); [Ukraine presidential statement at 20:42](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/situaciya-z-pereboyami-v-roboti-starshield-i-neobhidnist-pos-107301); [Ars Technica with SpaceX's direct response and contract context](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/ukraine-says-spacex-starshield-is-restricted-again-spacex-claims-it-isnt/).

**Not established:** whether the limit changed during the window; who controlled the relevant setting; how many systems or interceptions were affected; why.

**Editorial recommendation:** Major story because the capability dependency is strategic. Headline the unresolved control problem, not the unproved cap or motive.

## 4. Taiwan reported an unusually large unannounced Chinese drill across multiple theaters

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED as Taiwan's official account; PARTIALLY CORROBORATED by the Liaoning's acknowledged operations. The exact vessel count, attack simulation, and intimidation motive are not independently confirmed.
**Confidence:** Medium-high that a large coordinated operation occurred; medium on scale; low on motive and whether it was a blockade rehearsal.

### Atomic claims

- Taiwanese officials said more than 100 Chinese naval and coast-guard vessels operated in early November, with northern, eastern, and southern theater commands and the Liaoning participating: **supported by multiple Taiwanese officials quoted by Reuters**.
- Taiwan said the activity simulated attacks and was larger than Justice Mission 2025: **confirmed as Taiwan's assessment**, not independently proven by published imagery or logs.
- China called the carrier's operations routine and not directed at any country: **confirmed as China's stated alternative explanation**.
- A foreign intelligence source described the lack of announcement as deliberate intimidation: **claimed by one anonymous source**. Seasonal training provides a plausible alternative explanation.

**Best evidence:** [Reuters report carried by Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/china-stages-unannounced-pacific-drills-in-silent-intimidation-of-taiwan-officials-say-5024072).

**Not established:** a public authoritative count; a declared blockade scenario; Beijing's intent; whether the activity changed readiness beyond prior large exercises.

**Editorial recommendation:** Major story with strong attribution. Do not write “China rehearsed a blockade” as fact.

**Confidence:** High on the legal designations; medium on breadth; low on near-term revenue effect.

**Editorial recommendation:** Major story, but distinguish legal breadth from economic effect.

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED that a U.S. State Department-released KPMG report accounts for $12.26 billion in oil and other proceeds and reported $168.6 million remaining on September 30. The underlying recipient-level flow is not independently verified in the inspected record.
**Confidence:** High on what the report says; medium on the aggregate accounting; low on beneficial-recipient verification and policy effect.

### Atomic claims

- The report describes $12.26 billion in proceeds, principally from Venezuelan oil, and $168.6 million remaining at September 30: **confirmed in AP's inspection of the released report**.
- The arithmetic implies about 98.6% was disbursed or otherwise moved from the account: **confirmed calculation**. “Almost 99% returned” is the report's framing, not proof that all funds reached intended Venezuelan beneficiaries.
- Most disbursement occurred through foreign-exchange transactions to individuals and businesses, with a smaller direct-payment channel: **reported by AP from the review**.
- Twenty buyers were redacted and the report sampled selected payments: **confirmed limitation**. A September Senate Finance Committee letter documented earlier oversight concerns.

**Best evidence:** [AP inspection of the state-released KPMG report](https://apnews.com/article/venezuela-oil-sales-maduro-trump-6982bfdc5d64482e3500eea39891f0d1); [Reuters summary carried by Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/us-handled-12-billion-of-venezuelas-oil-revenues-report-says-5023141); [Senate Finance Committee oversight letter](https://www.finance.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/media/doc/09172026lettertotreasuryandstaterevenezuelanresourcestransactions.pdf).

**Not established:** complete independent audit access; identity of all buyers and final recipients; that disbursement equals broad public benefit; the counterfactual without U.S. custody.

**Editorial recommendation:** Major accountability story. Attribute the totals and explain the denominator.

- Sheinbaum disputed the premise and described Mexican actions to disperse or return members of a roughly 600-person group: **confirmed as her account**.
