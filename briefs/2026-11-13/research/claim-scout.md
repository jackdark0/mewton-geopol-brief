## Claim: China massed more than 100 vessels in an unannounced blockade rehearsal meant to intimidate Taiwan

- **Origin / principal promoters:** A senior Taiwanese security official speaking anonymously to Reuters, with public deployment details from Taiwan National Security Council Secretary-General Joseph Wu; a foreign intelligence source supported the “silent drill” interpretation.
- **Why it matters:** A coordinated blockade or anti-intervention rehearsal would indicate a larger operational capability and shorter warning cycle than routine carrier training.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Taiwan reported more than 100 navy and coast guard vessels at peak, participation by three theater commands and the Liaoning group, simulated strikes, and continued formations east of the Philippines.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** China called the carrier activity routine and did not announce a Taiwan drill. No inspected neutral imagery, full order of battle, Chinese exercise directive, or independent target simulation record establishes the scope or intent.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial on a large regional deployment; no on the exact peak, target set, or motive.
- **Current assessment:** **plausible** that a large cross-theater exercise occurred; **unclear** that it was specifically a blockade rehearsal or timed for coercion.
- **Confidence:** medium-high / low respectively.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/china-stages-unannounced-pacific-drills-in-silent-intimidation-of-taiwan-officials-say-5024072 - Reuters evidence and China's competing description.
- **Questions for auditor:** Corroborate hull count and formations; separate observation from Taiwan's operational interpretation; search Chinese notices and commercial imagery.

## Claim: SpaceX or the U.S. deliberately restored a 161 km/h Starshield cap to pressure Ukraine in peace talks

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Zelenskyy and Ukrainian officials establish the reported cap and operational degradation; unnamed Ukrainian officials told media they suspect coercive timing. SpaceX denies any change.
- **Why it matters:** Deliberate throttling would amount to indirect battlefield coercion through a contracted communications platform.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Ukraine reports consistent front-wide disruption, a specific 161 km/h threshold, prior similar restrictions, and effects on fast drone interceptors. The timing overlaps U.S.-Russia-Ukraine diplomacy.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** SpaceX says the allegation is false and there has been no change. No public telemetry, order, contract instruction, or decision-maker evidence shows that a cap was newly imposed or linked to negotiations.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for Ukrainian units experiencing a problem; no for the responsible actor or motive.
- **Current assessment:** **supported** that Ukraine reports significant degradation; **disputed** that a new cap exists; **unsupported** that it was deliberately imposed for negotiating pressure.
- **Confidence:** high / medium / high, with the last label meaning high confidence that motive is not established on the public record.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/mi-znovu-zafiksuvali-obmezhennya-shvidkosti-starshield-na-na-107285 - exact Ukrainian threshold claim.
  - https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/situaciya-z-pereboyami-v-roboti-starshield-i-neobhidnist-pos-107301 - claimed operational effect.
  - https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/ukraine-says-spacex-starshield-is-restricted-again-spacex-claims-it-isnt/ - SpaceX denial and absence of control evidence.
- **Questions for auditor:** Ask whether “no change” refers to configuration since October; seek Pentagon and telemetry evidence; separate service effect from intent.

## Claim: Putin agreed that Europe will join the next Ukraine peace talks as a full negotiating party

- **Origin / principal promoters:** U.S. envoys Witkoff and Kushner and Ukrainian President Zelenskyy. The EU says it was present at Abu Dhabi and will remain involved.
- **Why it matters:** European states are expected to fund Ukraine, supply forces and guarantees, manage sanctions, and bear regional-security consequences.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Witkoff said after meeting Putin that Europeans would join the next meeting; Zelenskyy said they would form a negotiating team with a voice; Kushner said Putin was willing to try.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No joint text, country list, representative, mandate, date, or Russian statement affirming the same terms. Kremlin spokesman Peskov conditioned participation on Europe naming a representative, while Putin rejected European forces in Ukraine and renewed NATO rollback demands.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes that the U.S. and Ukraine made the claim; no that Moscow accepted an identical full-party format.
- **Current assessment:** **supported as an announced U.S.-Ukrainian plan; unclear as a settled four-sided negotiating format.**
- **Confidence:** high / medium.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-putin-kushner-witkoff-683810203340077f33611fd1d1757b23 - participant statements and Russian conditions.
  - https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/12/joint-statement-by-president-costa-and-president-von-der-leyen-following-their-meeting-with-president-zelenskyy-in-dublin/ - EU involvement claim.
- **Questions for auditor:** Find a Kremlin or common statement; identify the representative and authority; distinguish attendance from negotiating-party status.

## Claim: Iran was responsible for two tanker attacks and two laden supertankers were burning in Hormuz

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Iranian state/IRGC-linked accounts described explosions affecting two tankers; separate public narratives attribute attacks near Iran to Tehran. UKMTO confirmed two struck tankers but not the attacker.
- **Why it matters:** Attribution could justify renewed U.S. strikes, wider escorts, sanctions, or allied entry into the maritime campaign.
- **Evidence offered for it:** UKMTO verified a projectile strike on a tanker's engine room on November 11 and reported a second projectile incident with medical evacuations. The attacks occurred in waters central to the U.S.-Iran confrontation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The verified warning calls the projectile unknown. Vessel identities, cargoes, imagery, weapon forensics, attacker, and the Iranian “two supertankers / four million barrels” description were not established in inspected direct records.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes for two incidents; no for Iranian responsibility or the cargo/fire claim.
- **Current assessment:** **supported** that two tankers were struck; **unclear** who attacked; **unsupported on current evidence** that two laden supertankers carrying more than four million barrels were burning.
- **Confidence:** high / low / moderate.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261112-ukmto_warning_204-26-1.pdf - direct verified warning.
  - https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-vance-negotiations-d9671cbf66e3c2e337f4fb3485ad07d0 - both incidents and attribution limits.
- **Questions for auditor:** Obtain UKMTO warning 203-26, vessel names, AIS/imagery, cargo records, and official investigations.

## Claim: The United States returned almost 99% of $12.26 billion in Venezuelan resource revenue to Venezuela

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Trump administration summary of the State Department-released KPMG report; AP uses the same numerator framing.
- **Why it matters:** The percentage shapes whether the U.S.-controlled custody mechanism is seen as transparent stewardship, seizure, or leverage.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The report states $12.26 billion in sales, $168.6 million remaining on September 30, large foreign-exchange disbursements, and selected direct payments. Each buyer was vetted.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Buyer names are redacted; recipients and end use are not fully reconstructable from the public summary; “returned” includes foreign-exchange and direct-payment mechanisms rather than a single transfer to a sovereign account. The inspected reporting does not establish independent recipient verification or audit assurance scope.
- **Independent corroboration:** AP and Reuters agree on the report's contents but share the KPMG/State document as common provenance.
- **Current assessment:** **supported as the report's accounting conclusion; qualified as an independently verified public claim.**
- **Confidence:** high / medium.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/venezuela-oil-sales-maduro-trump-6982bfdc5d64482e3500eea39891f0d1 - figures, mechanisms, and redactions.
  - https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/us-handled-12-billion-of-venezuelas-oil-revenues-report-says-5023141 - Reuters account of the report.
- **Questions for auditor:** Recompute the numerator and denominator from the report; identify KPMG's engagement type; test whether disbursement equals receipt by intended Venezuelan beneficiaries.

- **Why it matters:** The claim is the stated premise for a potentially large trade measure during the USMCA review.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Groups of several hundred migrants were traveling in southern Mexico.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Sheinbaum said no caravan reached the northern border since January 2025, said a roughly 600-person group largely dispersed, and described talks encouraging another group to return. No inspected order identifies the tariff base, products, date, or legal authority.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial that groups existed in southern Mexico; no for an invasion at the U.S. border or an implemented tariff doubling.
- **Current assessment:** **weak as a border-status claim; supported as a threat; unimplemented at cutoff.**
- **Confidence:** medium-high / high / high.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/mexico-us-sheinbaum-trump-d9829fe92a0ce9afecb909cb35a2977d - both principals' claims and trade context.
- **Questions for auditor:** Inspect U.S. and Mexican official border data; search Federal Register and presidential instruments; define what tariffs and baseline Trump meant.
