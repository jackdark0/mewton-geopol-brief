## Candidate: U.S. envoys say Europe will enter the next Ukraine negotiating round

- **Event time/date:** November 12, Moscow and Dublin; follow-on statements before cutoff.
- **Region / actors:** Russia, Ukraine, United States, European governments.
- **What changed:** Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Vladimir Putin for about two hours, then Volodymyr Zelenskyy for more than an hour. Witkoff and Zelenskyy said a next negotiating round had been agreed and Europe would participate as a negotiating team rather than remain on the sidelines. No country list, representative, date, ceasefire, territorial formula, or guarantee text was announced. Putin simultaneously renewed demands reaching beyond Ukraine: NATO force rollback in Central and Eastern Europe and rejection of European forces in postwar Ukraine.
- **Why this may matter:**
  - European inclusion would change the negotiating format and give expected guarantors a direct role.
  - The unresolved representative problem could delay or dilute that inclusion.
  - Putin's stated security demands show that format movement is not substantive convergence.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high; medium that Moscow accepted the same format described by U.S. and Ukrainian officials.
- **Source chain:** AP directly reported the meetings and participant statements; the Council of the EU separately published European positions. Russian acceptance is filtered through U.S. envoys and Russian state-TV remarks rather than a common text.
- **Sources:**
  - https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-putin-kushner-witkoff-683810203340077f33611fd1d1757b23 - meetings, next-round claim, Putin's demands, and limits.
