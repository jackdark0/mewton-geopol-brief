## Findings that must constrain the final brief

### 1. Ukraine: do not turn parallel statements into a negotiated format

- **Failure mode:** “Russia agreed Europe will be at the table.”
- **Problem:** The public record contains U.S. and Ukrainian descriptions, Kushner's account of Putin's willingness, and European principles. It does not contain a common participant list or mandate.
- **Required correction:** Say a next round was announced with European participation and Moscow's precise commitment remains unclear.
- **Residual risk:** “Europe” may mean an adjacent delegation rather than a full party. Confidence in a format breakthrough must remain medium or lower.

### 2. Iran: do not infer diplomatic closure or tanker attribution

- **Failure mode:** “Trump ended diplomacy as Iran attacked two tankers.”
- **Problem:** Trump's public order does not prove all backchannels closed. UKMTO says unknown projectiles and does not name an attacker.
- **Required correction:** Keep the political signal and maritime incidents as separate atomic claims. Use “as” only for simultaneity, never causation or attribution.
- **Residual risk:** Later intelligence may change attribution, but it lies beyond this cutoff.

### 3. Starshield: the denial prevents a causal verdict

- **Failure mode:** “SpaceX restored the cap to pressure Ukraine.”
- **Problem:** Ukraine reports a returned limit; SpaceX denies any change. No telemetry, configuration order, contract instruction, or motive evidence was published.
- **Required correction:** Headline the control dependency and the dispute. Attribute every claim about the cap's return or operational effect to Ukraine.
- **Residual risk:** The story may be a configuration mismatch rather than a deliberate restriction. That alternative does not remove the strategic dependency.

### 4. China: Taiwan's detailed account is not independent observation

- **Failure mode:** “China rehearsed a blockade with more than 100 ships.”
- **Problem:** The count, simulated attacks, and relative size flow primarily from Taiwanese officials; no inspected public track set or imagery reproduces them. A foreign intelligence source's motive claim is not independent proof of Beijing's intent.
- **Required correction:** “Taiwan says” belongs in the headline or first sentence. Describe blockade relevance as capability or scenario analysis, not fact.
- **Residual risk:** Multiple Taiwanese officials may share the same operational picture, so source count does not equal independent confirmation.

### 5. UK sanctions: designation breadth is not revenue damage

- **Failure mode:** “Britain shut down Russia's top ten oil producers.”
- **Problem:** A legal designation does not stop production. “Top ten” is the UK government's classification; no methodology or exposure denominator was published.
- **Required correction:** Report the named legal actions. State that enforcement and economic effect remain unmeasured.
- **Residual risk:** Repeating “nearly every part” without attribution would convert government rhetoric into an audit finding.

### 6. Venezuela: movement from an account is not return to the public

- **Failure mode:** “The audit proves 99% of Venezuela's money was returned.”
- **Problem:** The 98.6% arithmetic measures the account balance change relative to receipts. It does not measure end-beneficiary receipt, distributional fairness, or welfare effect. The state released a KPMG review; the inspected sources do not establish a fully public independent audit.
- **Required correction:** Attribute the report, give both numerator and denominator, and call out redacted buyers and selected-payment sampling.
- **Residual risk:** “Returned” carries a normative conclusion the accounting denominator cannot support.

### 7. Mexico: a threat is not a tariff, and two principals are not a denominator

- **Failure mode:** “Trump doubled tariffs because Mexico let caravans through.”
- **Problem:** No tariff base, legal order, date, or product list was announced. Trump and Sheinbaum made conflicting factual claims; neither supplies independent migration data.
- **Required correction:** Use “threatened.” Treat the caravan premise as disputed. Focus the strategic analysis on uncertainty during the USMCA review.
- **Residual risk:** Existing sectoral tariffs make “double” meaningless without naming the base rate.

## Rapid Fire corrections

- **Yemen:** Resolution 2837 renews and staffs an existing architecture. It does not itself authorize automatic vessel interdiction or add a designation.
- **MINUSCA:** A 14.6% cut in the authorized military ceiling is not proof that 2,046 deployed troops will immediately leave.
- **UK-UFORCE:** Industrial intent is verified; production scale, delivery date, and military effect are not.
- **South Africa:** Arrest and allegation are facts; recruitment and guilt remain claims until adjudicated.

## Forecast audit

- No forecast should exceed the confidence of its mechanism.
- A new Ukraine participant list would be evidence of process change, not probability of peace.
- Maritime incidents can raise risk without documented throughput loss.
- Sanctions can increase transaction costs without observable revenue decline.
- An ambiguous military drill can degrade warning quality even if it was seasonal training.

5. Use “announced,” “reported,” “threatened,” and “disputed” where those verbs carry the evidentiary limit.
