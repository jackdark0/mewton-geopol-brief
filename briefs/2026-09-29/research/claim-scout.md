## Claim C-01: Iran directed the five men arrested near RAF Fairford

- **Exact atomic proposition / type:** The Iranian state tasked or directed the five men arrested near RAF Fairford on 27 September. Attribution, not merely whether Iran had motive or whether the men committed an offence.
- **Origin / principal promoters:** Anonymous sources in the Guardian's 27 September reporting put an Iran link high in investigators' thinking; stronger versions of the narrative treat Iranian command as established. Iran's London embassy denied involvement, as AP reported on 28 September.
- **Why it matters:** Proven Iranian direction would make the incident a state-linked attack attempt on British soil associated with the U.S.–Iran war, with escalation and base-protection implications.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The vans were reported travelling toward a base used by U.S. bombers; Iran had warned about bases used for strikes. Those facts establish a possible motive and target, not tasking. Police explicitly said a foreign proxy line was among their inquiries.
- **Contrary / narrowing / missing evidence:** Counter Terrorism Policing said on 28 September that it kept an open mind and was exploring multiple lines. It released all five men on bail under conditions. No public communications, payments, command chain, sponsor identification or device finding was provided. The embassy denial is a party statement, not proof of innocence. Bail is not exoneration.
- **Independent corroboration:** **No** for Iranian direction. Guardian anonymous sources, AP's account of police speculation and repetitions of either are not separate evidentiary chains for command.
- **Current assessment:** **Weak** if asserted as an established Iranian operation; the investigative possibility remains **unclear**. This does not classify the underlying suspicion as false.
- **Confidence:** **Medium** in the absence of public corroboration and the narrow assessment; **low** on who, if anyone, directed the men. Police may hold unreleased evidence.
- **Provenance / source lineage:** `CTP-PRIMARY` = police's own pre-cutoff statements, competent to establish investigation status; `GUARDIAN-ANON` = its unidentified investigative sources, one opaque origin; `AP-REPORTING` = direct police and embassy reporting, not independent confirmation of sponsorship.
- **Direct inspected sources and entailment:** [CTP 16:00 update, 28 September](https://www.counterterrorism.police.uk/news/update-on-raf-fairford-investigation-1600-28-september-2026/) — explicitly describes open mind, possible foreign proxies and release on bail; [CTP 12:30 update](https://www.counterterrorism.police.uk/news/update-on-raf-fairford-investigation/) — names the five as British nationals and says knowledge/motivation were still being established; [Guardian original report](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/sep/27/arrests-suspected-terrorism-charges-explosives-found-near-raf-base-us) — says Iran was an investigative possibility and other motives were not ruled out; [AP, 28 September](https://apnews.com/article/uk-fairford-us-air-base-arrests-investigation-4b6fb8b4636c0c360bd7d3d2c44e8075) — reports the Iranian embassy denial and police's multiple lines.
- **Questions for auditor:** Has any pre-cutoff U.K. charge, forensic update or attributed intelligence finding identified a sponsor? Was RAF Fairford the intended target or only direction of travel? Can the embassy's denial be located in its original publication?

Other summaries hardened “suspected” into “viable.”
- **Why it matters:** A functioning device changes the assessment of attack capability and how narrowly the incident can be described.
- **Evidence offered for it:** A bomb-disposal response, extended cordon and Guardian source account of suspicious materials.
- **Contrary / narrowing / missing evidence:** AP reported that Britain's PA news agency said, without naming its source, that police had not found viable explosives. The inspected CTP updates described forensic examination and an ongoing inquiry but did not announce a device or a positive viability test. Neither the anonymous Guardian view nor anonymous PA result is a published forensic record.
- **Independent corroboration:** **No** direct independent forensic corroboration of a viable device. The Guardian and PA assertions point in different directions; the police statements verify a precautionary examination only.
- **Current assessment:** **Unclear**. “A viable bomb was found” exceeds the inspected public evidence.
- **Confidence:** **Medium** that the public record is insufficient; **low** on the underlying device status because the forensic result was withheld.
- **Provenance / source lineage:** `GUARDIAN-ANON-MATERIAL` = unidentified investigators/material view; `PA-UNNAMED` = PA report recounted by AP, anonymous and contrary; `CTP-PRIMARY` = direct process account, silent on result. Reuters or other copies of PA do not add independence.

A willingness to consider relief is not an implemented concession. Whether AP and Axios used the same official is unknown; parallel publication does not prove independent access.

Trump's denial is an independent, interested counterstatement about his position, not a documentary disproof of all staff discussions.
- **Current assessment:** **Unclear** for actual authorization; **supported** only that U.S. officials briefed a conditional-relief position and Trump publicly denied that account. Do not merge these separate propositions in the final brief.
- **Confidence:** **Low** on the internal authorization because no negotiator document or named decision-maker was available; **high** that the public accounts conflicted before cutoff.
- **Provenance / source lineage:** `US-ANON-AP` and `US-ANON-AXIOS` = potentially overlapping, unnamed administration sources; `TRUMP-DENIAL-via-AXIOS` = first-person public rebuttal reproduced by Axios; `AP-REGIONAL` = separate regional mediator sources for continuing talks, not authorization.

- **Current assessment:** **Plausible** at the stated date, grounded in convergent opposing-party testimony rather than mere impossibility of disproof. Control durability and exact force composition remain **unclear**.
