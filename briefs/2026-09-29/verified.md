## Story: U.S.-China product lists are approved, but tariff cuts are not yet in force

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** The United States and China published comparably valued lists of products under a "30-for-30" framework. The roughly $30 billion per side is based on calendar-year 2024 bilateral trade values. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/u-s-china-board-of-trade/) [terms of reference](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Terms-of-Reference-for-30-for-30-Framework.pdf) [Chinese Ministry of Commerce](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/rcxwfb/art/2026/art_7f879be341ec4137a22f6d0360a5a291.html)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence]** The primary terms say future tariff reductions must be determined and implemented through each side's domestic legal processes. Publication of the lists therefore did not itself change applied tariff rates. [terms of reference](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Terms-of-Reference-for-30-for-30-Framework.pdf)
- **[CONFIRMED | High confidence as China's stated commitment]** China's ministry said more than 90% of covered products would receive removal of all additional bilateral tariffs and most-favored-nation treatment after domestic procedures, with synchronized implementation. This is an announced intended treatment, not proof that the rates were already effective. [Chinese Ministry of Commerce](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/rcxwfb/art/2026/art_7f879be341ec4137a22f6d0360a5a291.html)
- **[SUPPORTED | Moderate confidence]** The U.S. list focuses largely on consumer goods, while China's list includes agricultural products, personal-care goods, medical devices and coal. Strategic sectors such as semiconductors, EVs and batteries were not the focus of the published lists. The sector observation is based on list inspection and AP's reporting, not an express blanket exclusion clause in the terms. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/u-s-china-board-of-trade/) [AP](https://apnews.com/article/us-china-trade-tariffs-deal-trump-xi-f47781187c501e7c4f10a686fb2572e2)

### Material claims not established

- A $60 billion tariff cut, $60 billion in current trade or $60 billion in savings.
- Any effective date, U.S. tariff proclamation, Federal Register action or Chinese customs implementation by the cutoff.
- Realized trade creation, price reductions or stabilization of the broader strategic relationship.

### Source provenance

- White House terms and lists are one U.S. primary lineage; the Chinese ministry is a distinct primary issuer.
- Agreement between the two governments corroborates the joint procedural action. It does not independently validate either side's forecast of economic effects.
- AP is independent reporting but derives the framework's content from the same official documents.

### Open questions

- What rates, effective date and legal instruments will each side adopt?
- Which partial tariff lines and exclusions remain after domestic review?
- What is the current-year trade value and likely tariff-revenue effect?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Emphasize lists and a mechanism now, tariff relief later if legal implementation occurs.
