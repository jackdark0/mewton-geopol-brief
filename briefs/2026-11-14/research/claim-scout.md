## Claim: Iran struck and damaged a U.S. early-warning radar in Bahrain

- **Origin / principal promoters:** IRGC statement carried by Iranian state media.
- **Why it matters:** A successful hit would show Iranian ability to penetrate defenses around a key U.S. Gulf base and could trigger retaliation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Iran said the strike retaliated for an attack on a telecommunications site in Sirik.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** CENTCOM confirmed two drones were launched but said both were intercepted, with no injury or damage. Bahrain had not issued a confirming public account by the cutoff. No imagery or sensor record was available.
- **Independent corroboration:** no for damage; partial for the launch.
- **Current assessment:** false or unsupported as a damage claim; supported that two Iranian drones headed toward Bahrain.
- **Confidence:** high on the intercept account; medium-high that the radar-hit claim is not sustained by the public record.
- **Sources:** https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-bahrain-radar-strait-hormuz-5d73a23b5b8647d2515c35b267b36c08 (conflicting accounts); https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-13-2026/ (CENTCOM wording).
- **Questions for auditor:** Can Bahrain or independent imagery confirm no damage? Did either drone approach the claimed radar?

## Claim: A U.S. attack on Sirik caused Iran's Bahrain strike

- **Origin / principal promoters:** IRGC and IRNA.
- **Why it matters:** The causal sequence determines whether the Bahrain launch was retaliation, initiation, or part of an ongoing exchange.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Iran reported an “enemy” air attack on a telecommunications tower in Sirik and tied its response directly to that event.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No U.S. acknowledgment, independent strike attribution, target function, casualty report, or timing record was inspected. A real explosion would not by itself establish U.S. authorship.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear.
- **Confidence:** medium that an incident occurred; low on attacker and causal command chain.
- **Sources:** https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-bahrain-radar-strait-hormuz-5d73a23b5b8647d2515c35b267b36c08 (Iranian claim and limits); https://www.gulfnews.com/world/mena/us-israel-iran-war-enemy-strike-hits-irans-sirik-as-trump-warns-tehran-over-threats-to-us-officials-houthis-claim-attack-on-saudi-pipeline-1.500745854 (IRNA report).
- **Questions for auditor:** What observable damage exists at Sirik? Who had capability, access, and an acknowledged operation?

## Claim: China deployed more than 100 vessels and rehearsed attacks on Taiwan and other forces

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Anonymous senior Taiwanese security officials speaking to Reuters.
- **Why it matters:** The scale and mission would indicate a major advance in cross-theater blockade or regional-war preparation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Taiwan reported more than 100 military and coast-guard vessels, all three theater commands, the Liaoning group, attack simulations, and a submarine ICBM launch.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No public ship-level list, complete tracking data, imagery set, exercise order, or Chinese notice establishes the denominator or simulated missions. China calls the carrier activity routine and not directed at a country.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial for a large operation; no public independent confirmation of the strongest details.
- **Current assessment:** supported that a large coordinated operation occurred; plausible but unproved at the claimed scale and attack-simulation scope.
- **Confidence:** medium-high / medium-low respectively.
- **Sources:** https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/china-stages-unannounced-pacific-drills-in-silent-intimidation-of-taiwan-officials-say-ce785ed9db8df526 (Reuters evidence and Chinese response).
- **Questions for auditor:** Can Taiwan defense releases, satellite imagery, or allied statements reproduce the count and launch claim?

## Claim: The Trump-Xi-Putin meeting will launch three-way nuclear arms control

- **Origin / principal promoters:** White House framing and optimistic public commentary.
- **Why it matters:** A real process could replace part of the verification vacuum after New START; a ceremonial meeting could falsely signal restraint.
- **Evidence offered for it:** U.S. officials say nuclear reductions will be discussed and call the meeting a beginning of conversation.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Officials say no agreement is expected. China has resisted a trilateral frame and has a much smaller arsenal. No mandate, baseline, weapons scope, verification concept, or follow-up meeting is public.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes that the discussion is planned; no that a negotiation will begin.
- **Current assessment:** supported as an agenda item; unclear as an arms-control process.
- **Confidence:** high / low.
- **Sources:** https://apnews.com/article/trump-xi-putin-summit-china-5fe92253ef38100bae913834a9b74d72 (White House plans and caveats); https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trump-to-meet-putin-and-xi-in-china-white-house-says-ce785eded88cf12d (Reuters).
- **Questions for auditor:** Have Beijing and Moscow accepted “arms control” rather than a general strategic-stability discussion?

## Claim: Europe now has a full seat and common mandate in Ukraine peace talks

- **Origin / principal promoters:** U.S., Ukrainian, and European participant statements after Dublin.
- **Why it matters:** A genuine mandate could make sanctions, guarantees, and funding commitments executable.
- **Evidence offered for it:** Costa named a team of Britain, France, Germany, Italy, Poland, and EU institutions; U.S. envoys said Europe will join.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No common Russian confirmation of that exact team, no agenda or date, no NATO role, and no delegation mandate have been published.
- **Independent corroboration:** partial.
- **Current assessment:** supported that Europe has defined a proposed team; plausible but not established that it has a full negotiating mandate accepted by all sides.
- **Confidence:** high / medium-low.
- **Sources:** https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-putin-trump-zelenskyy-europe-157d168cb7ff9cae318cbf6cecb3988f (team and claims); https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/12/us-envoys-arrive-in-moscow-for-latest-round-of-talks-with-putin-a94236/pdf (Russia's stated conditions).

## Claim: The Turkey-Qatar agreement automatically treats an attack on one as an attack on both

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Strong readings of the phrase “unified stance” in the joint communiqué and secondary summaries.
- **Why it matters:** Automatic collective-defense obligations would materially alter deterrence and escalation in the Gulf.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The official communiqué says the pact aims at collective defense and a unified stance against an external armed attack targeting either or both parties.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** The treaty text, response clause, ratification requirements, entry-into-force provision, force commitments, and command arrangements are not public in the inspected materials. “Unified stance” does not necessarily mean automatic military action.
- **Independent corroboration:** yes that the pact was signed; no on automaticity.
- **Current assessment:** supported as a mutual-defense pact; unclear as an Article 5-style automatic obligation.
- **Confidence:** high / low.
- **Sources:** https://www.mfa.gov.tr/turkiye-cumhuriyeti-ile-katar-devleti-arasinda-yuksek-stratejik-komite-on-ikinci-toplantisi-ortak-bildirisi--13-kasim-2026.en.mfa (primary communiqué); https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-bahrain-radar-strait-hormuz-5d73a23b5b8647d2515c35b267b36c08 (signing confirmation).
- **Questions for auditor:** Retrieve signed text or ratification bill; identify the operative verb for armed response.

## Claim: An LNG/LPG tanker struck an Iranian naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz

- **Origin / principal promoters:** IRGC Navy through Iranian state media.
- **Why it matters:** A confirmed mine strike on a gas carrier would affect shipping risk, insurance, and escalation.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The IRGC says the vessel ignored warnings, used an unauthorized route, struck a mine, and exploded.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** No vessel name, flag, owner, cargo record, crew account, distress call, imagery, AIS trace, or independent maritime-security report was available. Reuters called it an Iranian claim; AP said it could not independently verify it.
- **Independent corroboration:** no.
- **Current assessment:** unclear and presently weak.
- **Confidence:** high that evidence is insufficient; low on whether an incident occurred.
- **Sources:** https://gcaptain.com/iran-says-tanker-carrying-lng-hit-mine-suffered-explosion-in-hormuz/ (Reuters); https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-bahrain-radar-strait-hormuz-5d73a23b5b8647d2515c35b267b36c08 (AP).
- **Questions for auditor:** Check UKMTO, IMO, flag-state, insurer, and AIS sources for a matching casualty.

## Claim: Houthi missiles hit Riyadh airport and the East-West Pipeline

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Houthi military statements.
- **Why it matters:** Either hit would threaten Saudi aviation or the main oil bypass around Hormuz.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The Houthis claimed attacks on King Khalid International Airport, a pipeline pumping station, and Saudi forces in Jazan. Saudi civil defense briefly alerted Jazan.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Saudi authorities did not confirm impacts, flight or pipeline disruption, damage, casualties, or a connection between the Jazan alert and the Houthi claim.
- **Independent corroboration:** no on target or impact; partial for an alert.
- **Current assessment:** plausible launches; unclear targets and effects.
- **Confidence:** medium / low.
- **Sources:** https://www.2014.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/11/13/yemen-s-houthis-say-they-fired-missiles-at-saudi-forces-in-jazan (Reuters report).
- **Questions for auditor:** Check Saudi defense, airport operations, Aramco, NASA FIRMS, and commercial imagery.

## Claim: The IAEA's lack of access proves Iran diverted near-weapons-grade uranium

- **Origin / principal promoters:** Escalatory interpretation of the IAEA report.
- **Why it matters:** A diversion finding could drive military or diplomatic decisions.
- **Evidence offered for it:** The IAEA cannot verify the stockpile, bombed sites, or the Isfahan plant and calls the information gap an urgent proliferation concern.
- **Evidence against it / missing evidence:** Inability to verify is not evidence of diversion. The agency report, as quoted, says it cannot establish status, not that diversion occurred.
- **Independent corroboration:** no evidence of diversion in the inspected sources.
- **Current assessment:** unsupported.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** https://apnews.com/article/iran-nuclear-weapons-inspections-monitoring-bushehr-war-a689242aa635c103033790bacbff7f07 (exact distinction between unknown and established); https://www.al-monitor.com/breaking-news (Reuters report).
- **Questions for auditor:** Preserve the IAEA's exact legal and evidentiary wording; do not upgrade “cannot verify” to “diverted.”
