## Finding: The Bahrain damage dispute is not adjudicated by repetition

- **Severity:** major
- **Targets:** “The U.S. account defeats the public damage claim unless new evidence appears” in the Bahrain analysis.
- **Problem:** CENTCOM's account is more specific and fits the absence of independent damage evidence, but it remains a belligerent statement. “Defeats” overstates what the public record can establish.
- **Evidence or reasoning:** Iran claims a radar hit; CENTCOM claims two intercepts and no damage; Bahrain and independent imagery are absent. AP and Reuters repeating the statements do not create sensor corroboration.
- **Best competing interpretation:** One or both drones may have been intercepted after reaching the relevant area, or a separate effect may have occurred without public evidence. That interpretation is not supported, but the record cannot eliminate it categorically.
- **Required editorial action:** qualify. Say the Iranian damage claim is unsupported and contradicted by CENTCOM, not disproved by independent evidence.

## Finding: The IAEA story must not imply a new legal exemption
