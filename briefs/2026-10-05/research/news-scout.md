## Candidate: U.S. bombers leave RAF Fairford

- **Event time/date:** October 4, before cutoff.
- **Region / actors:** United States, United Kingdom, Iran.
- **What changed:** The Pentagon confirmed that all U.S. bombers deployed to RAF Fairford had returned to their U.S. home stations. The move followed a British counterterrorism investigation near the base, but British police have publicly described foreign-state involvement only as one possible line of inquiry.
- **Why this may matter:** It removes a visible forward bomber concentration used in the Iran campaign; it may also show how base-security risk can alter posture without removing continental-U.S. strike capacity.
- **Initial priority:** high.
- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** Pentagon statement relayed by Axios and AP; British police are the primary source on the investigation.
- **Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/04/us-b-1-bombers-evacuated-from-uk-base-after-attack-threats-from-iran) carries the Pentagon statement; [Counter Terrorism Policing](https://www.counterterrorism.police.uk/news/update-on-raf-fairford-investigation-1730-01-october-2026/) confirms the arrests and says possible foreign-state involvement is still being investigated; an [earlier police update](https://www.counterterrorism.police.uk/news/update-on-raf-fairford-investigation-1600-28-september-2026/) says no improvised explosive devices were found, though petrol was recovered.
- **Questions for auditor:** Was the redeployment accelerated by the threat, and is there public evidence connecting Iran's state to the suspects?

## Candidate: Government-aligned forces enter Mekelle

- **Event time/date:** October 4.
- **Region / actors:** Ethiopia, Tigray, TPLF, Tigray Peace Force.
- **What changed:** Government-aligned fighters entered Mekelle after the TPLF said it had temporarily withdrawn to spare the city. Witnesses, photographs and multiple reporting lines support a transfer of visible control, but not a final defeat of the TPLF.

- **Confidence in basic event:** high.
- **Source chain:** AP witnesses/photos and Reuters' multiple local sources overlap on the basic event; TPLF statements supply its rationale.
- **Sources:** [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ethiopia-tigray-war-tplf-792f66094d4cc5171ec5478e8a994931) reports entry into Mekelle and the TPLF withdrawal statement; [Reuters relay](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/government-forces-seize-capital-of-ethiopias-tigray-region-from-rebels-4930913) cites three sources; the [African Union](https://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20260923/chairperson-calls-restraint-and-immediate-de-escalation-northern-ethiopia) previously warned that renewed fighting threatened the Pretoria agreement.
