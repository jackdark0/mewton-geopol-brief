## Story: Washington creates a large new sanctions weapon against Russia, with executive discretion built in

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED]** President Trump signed H.R. 5334 on September 18, 2026. The law expands Russia-related sanctions and trade restrictions and extends the Iran Sanctions Act through December 31, 2031. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/09/congressional-bill-h-r-5334-signed-into-law/) [enrolled bill](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr5334enr/html/BILLS-119hr5334enr.htm)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The statute requires reviews and sanctions involving specified Russian officials, state financial institutions, foreign financial institutions, shadow-fleet vessels and sanctions-evasion activity, with several actions beginning on a 30-day timetable. [enrolled bill](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr5334enr/html/BILLS-119hr5334enr.htm)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The statute permits duties of up to 500 percent on Russian products and up to 100 percent on goods from countries determined to fall within specified top-five Russian-energy-buyer or evasion-facilitator categories. The maximum rates are ceilings, not automatic universal tariffs on signature. [enrolled bill](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr5334enr/html/BILLS-119hr5334enr.htm)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The law preserves defined exceptions, a wind-down period for some existing activity, licensing authority and a presidential national-interest waiver subject to written certification and reporting. Russia-related termination is tied to an end to hostilities or a Ukraine-accepted peace arrangement and a congressional review process. [enrolled bill](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr5334enr/html/BILLS-119hr5334enr.htm)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The measure passed the Senate 86–11 and the House 262–159. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/donald-trump-russia-sanctions-ukraine-lindsey-graham-d1715ad5e8feccba76bfae51c5f28c45)

### Material claims not established

- The law has not already imposed the headline maximum tariffs on all Russian imports or every Russian-energy buyer. That formulation conflicts with the operative text.
- No inspected source establishes which foreign countries will be identified, what tariff rates will be chosen, which licenses will issue, or how Russia and major energy buyers will respond.
- The congressional review process creates political friction but does not eliminate presidential flexibility; its practical constraint depends on congressional votes and veto dynamics.

### Source provenance

- The GPO enrolled text is the controlling evidence for legal effect. The White House independently establishes signature but summarizes rather than interprets the provisions.
- AP independently supplies vote totals and political reaction. Its legal summary is secondary to the enrolled text.

### Open questions

- Which entities and countries will be named at the first deadlines, and at what duty rates?
- Will the administration use licenses or the national-interest waiver to turn the statute into negotiating leverage rather than maximal immediate pressure?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Center the implementation fork, not the headline ceiling.
