## Story: Ukraine mounts a four-digit claimed drone wave against Russia

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[SUPPORTED]** A very large Ukrainian drone attack reached the Moscow region overnight September 19-20. AP reported visible smoke and flame at the Moscow Oil Refinery and published imagery identified as the refinery after the attack. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-drones-moscow-5f7889d027c0247ca2c2c64425b9ffa9)
- **[CLAIMED]** Russia's Defense Ministry said it destroyed 1,110 Ukrainian drones across Russia, occupied Crimea and the Black Sea. The number is corrected in AP's published account but remains a Russian official total, not an independent census. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-drones-moscow-5f7889d027c0247ca2c2c64425b9ffa9)
- **[CLAIMED]** Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 450 drones were destroyed en route to the capital and called it the largest-ever drone attack on Moscow. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-drones-moscow-5f7889d027c0247ca2c2c64425b9ffa9)
- **[SUPPORTED]** Moscow-region authorities reported two deaths and 20 injuries; the mayor reported damage to a residential building and the refinery. These are official local figures and site accounts, with the refinery damage partly corroborated by imagery. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-drones-moscow-5f7889d027c0247ca2c2c64425b9ffa9)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The attack occurred on the final day of Russia's parliamentary election. [AP drone report](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-drones-moscow-5f7889d027c0247ca2c2c64425b9ffa9), [AP election report](https://apnews.com/article/russia-election-parliament-duma-putin-ukraine-6f3df3716cbcf8c96711b688762b450b)

### Material claims not established

- That Ukraine launched exactly 1,110 drones, as opposed to Russia recording or claiming that many defensive engagements.
- That Russia destroyed every drone represented in the official total.
- That Ukraine intended to affect the election rather than selecting the timing for military reasons; this is a Russian attribution claim.
- The refinery's operational loss, repair time, or effect on Moscow fuel supply.

### Source provenance

- The numerical totals, casualties and damage reports originate with Russian state or local authorities.
- AP adds independent journalism and visible imagery but does not independently validate the four-digit count.
- No accessible Ukrainian primary statement found during the run supplied a launch total.

### Open questions

- What number of airframes did Ukraine launch, and what types were used?
- How many reached intended targets, crashed or were intercepted?
- Did refinery throughput change after the attack?

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Lead with the scale claim and observable refinery damage; preserve attribution on all exact totals.

## Story: United Russia leads a constrained wartime parliamentary election

**Priority:** high

### Verified propositions

- **[CONFIRMED]** Polling ended September 20. With about 58% of precincts counted before the research cutoff, Russia's Central Election Commission reported United Russia near 58% in the 225-seat party-list vote; the party also led most single-seat races. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-election-parliament-duma-putin-ukraine-6f3df3716cbcf8c96711b688762b450b)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The election included Crimea and four partly occupied Ukrainian regions that Russia claims to have annexed. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-election-parliament-duma-putin-ukraine-6f3df3716cbcf8c96711b688762b450b)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Yabloko was removed from the party ballot by Russia's Supreme Court, leaving only some constituency candidates; other anti-war politicians had been imprisoned or forced abroad. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-election-parliament-duma-putin-ukraine-6f3df3716cbcf8c96711b688762b450b)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Russia did not invite an OSCE election observation mission. ODIHR and the OSCE Parliamentary Assembly said this breached Russia's OSCE commitments and deprived the process of independent, impartial assessment. [OSCE/ODIHR](https://odihr.osce.org/odihr/667020)
- **[CLAIMED]** Yabloko and the Communist Party reported observers being excluded and procedural violations. AP could not independently verify the specific allegations. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/russia-election-parliament-duma-putin-ukraine-6f3df3716cbcf8c96711b688762b450b)

### Material claims not established

- That the preliminary vote share is the final result. Later September 21 totals are outside the cutoff.
- That the result measures free popular support for Putin or the war.
- That every reported polling violation occurred as alleged or changed the numerical outcome.

### Source provenance

- Vote totals come from the Russian CEC through AP.
- The observer record comes directly from OSCE institutions.
- Party allegations and AP's observation at a polling place are distinct evidence streams; the former remain unverified.

### Open questions

- Final certified seat allocation and turnout.
- Scope of electronic-voting irregularities and observer exclusions.
- Foreign nonrecognition measures tied to voting in occupied Ukraine.

### Recommendation

- **Include major.** Use the cutoff-era preliminary count only. Frame the strategic result as institutional continuity under constrained competition, not democratic endorsement.

**Priority:** medium

### Source provenance

### Open questions

- System identification, trajectory details and any North Korean release.

### Recommendation

- **Rapid fire.** The launches are verified but do not yet show a strategic-capability change.
